# NEURAL-024 — causal next20ms neural-count forecasting

This delivery preserves the finite Indy2016-10-05→2016-10-06 count-forecast task:96 corresponding M1 electrode channels,22,100 complete forecast rounds,10 required unscored warm-up rounds and22,090 scored rounds. The target is a positive expected count per20ms bin, not velocity, a rate per second, a probability, or a claim that sorted neurons persist across days. All five released sort slots, including residual unsorted crossings, were aggregated in the existing conversion.

The only data files a candidate may receive in advance are `training/source_counts.npz` and `training/calibration_counts.npz`. Each contains exactly `counts` (little-endian uint32) and `bin_edges_seconds` (little-endian float64), with shapes18,700×96 /18,701 and3,000×96 /3,001 respectively. No velocities or endpoint arrays remain. Native clock edges are unchanged; requests use target-session-relative intervals. Source data precede the target day; calibration is its first60s. Do not change this split or remove channels/bins.

The exact existing interaction is in `PROTOCOL.json` and `ORIGINAL_TASK.md`: ready/initialize handshake, then22,100 newline-delimited JSON predict requests and complete96-mean replies, then a final stop acknowledgment. A request contains only the preceding bin's observation, released after its entire vector was durably committed. Sequence0 has no observation. Sequences0–9 are required warm-up; sequences10–22099 (target bins3010–25099) are scored. The candidate may retain state and learn from legally revealed history. Strict positive finite float64-compatible means are required, with the existing numeric, nonce, ordering and4,096B line rules. `OUTPUT_SCHEMA.json` describes response structure; the original protocol parser and trusted transcript admission also enforce the relational rules.

Primary score is the full Poisson log-likelihood gain against the immutable calibration-rate reference, divided by the total observed spikes across all2,120,640 scored cells and ln(2), in **bits per spike**. Full likelihood retains log-factorials. Mean NLL, per-channel results and all six signed fixed-reference comparisons remain unchanged. Warm-up is excluded from every metric; partial or favorable prefixes receive no score. Zero spike denominators are explicitly undefined. Negative gains are valid; finite arithmetic failure is not clipped or converted to a partial score. The supplied `calibration_rate.py` is the unchanged protocol-aware control source. It is not executed or fit during packaging.

**Cross-task exposure:** NEURAL-023's public assessment counts are this task's private future observations. Prior NEURAL-023 access invalidates a hidden-target claim here even when the later runtime is isolated. Historical labels and counts are exposed development material in any case; this package does not claim memorization resistance, protected testing or new independent animals.

This export supplies actual count-only training assets, private future/clock/reference assets, schemas and a portable private grader. It does **not** supply a new causal execution controller. The original protocol and its historical600s candidate/780s attempt budget are documentation of the existing route, not a new execution grant or verified environment. A complete valid offline journal can be scored, but its bytes alone cannot prove causal access. A trusted externally enforced producer must attest durable commit-before-reveal ordering and stopped state; a candidate-authored attestation is invalid operational evidence. The portable grader does not manufacture such an attestation.
