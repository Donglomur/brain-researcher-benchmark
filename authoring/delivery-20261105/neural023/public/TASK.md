# NEURAL-023 — across-day trailing hand-velocity reconstruction

Use Indy2016-10-05 neural counts and velocity labels, plus the first60s of labeled Indy2016-10-06 calibration, to predict all22,091 assessment velocity endpoints on2016-10-06. This delivery preserves the existing finite task's data, split, metrics and seven references. It is one exposed development pair from one monkey, not a new dataset or a newly qualified experiment.

The complete candidate data are `source.npz`, `calibration.npz`, `assessment.npz`, and `manifest.json` beside this document. Source has18,700×96counts and18,691 labeled endpoints; calibration has3,000×96counts and2,991 labeled endpoints; assessment has22,100×96counts and22,091 endpoint IDs without velocities. Counts are little-endian uint32, clock edges little-endian float64, endpoint IDs little-endian uint32, and training velocities little-endian float64. The manifest supplies the exact channel and coordinate conventions. M1 electrode identity is retained across days; sorted neurons are not asserted longitudinally tracked. All five released sort slots, including residual unsorted events, were aggregated during the original conversion.

Each target is the fixed trailing20ms mean velocity at its endpoint, in m/s, x then y. It is not future velocity. The source reference uses ten count bins ending at each endpoint; an arbitrary batch decoder can inspect later assessment counts. This contract does not imply online causality or a frozen adaptation policy. Use only source/calibration labels for training or selection; assessment velocities, private references and previous predictions must not be inputs. Exact source-support and reference fitting details remain in `REFERENCE_PROTOCOL.json`; `reference_core.py` is the unchanged reference-family implementation, provided as source without executing or refitting it.

Submit an ordinary `predictions.npz` containing exactly `case_id[22091]` and `velocity_m_s[22091,2]`. IDs must be every integer3010…25100 exactly once; record order may differ and the grader joins by ID. IEEE float16/32/64 predictions must be finite; IDs are signed/unsigned16/32/64-bit integers. Stored/deflated NPZ, C/Fortran layout and either byte order are accepted, with1,000,000B compressed and600,000B uncompressed caps. `OUTPUT_SCHEMA.json` records the array contract; the unchanged private `candidate_io.py` is the authoritative parser. `ORIGINAL_PUBLIC_CONTRACT.md` retains the complete inherited numerical policy.

Primary score is the **unweighted mean of x-axis and y-axis R²**, on every22,091 row. It is not variance-weighted R². A constant target axis makes the primary score undefined rather than dropping the axis. Negative scores and scientifically poor predictions are valid. MSE, MAE,3,000-row descriptive clock blocks including the remainder, and signed contrasts against all seven immutable references are retained. No amplitude clipping, success floor, new fit or new reference selection is added. Complete public validation precedes private target access. Finite values outside supported metric arithmetic produce `unsupported_numerical_result`; disagreement with independent arithmetic remains an operator error.

**Cross-task exposure:** this task's public `assessment.npz` neural counts are NEURAL-024's private future targets.
