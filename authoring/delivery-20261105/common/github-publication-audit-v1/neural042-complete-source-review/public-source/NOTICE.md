# Source attribution and retained licenses

The repository's existing MIT license remains in place for the original project material it governs. This snapshot does not replace, reinterpret or relicense third-party source under MIT.

The unchanged upstream source originates from `rutishauserlab/SBCAT-release-NWB`, commit `a25e8cc3f61630a428f190381745257d4506f148`, accompanying Daume et al., *Control of working memory by phase–amplitude coupling of human hippocampal neurons*, DOI `10.1038/s41586-024-07309-z`. The upstream `LICENSE` and `README.md` retain their BSD 3-Clause notices and attribution to the Rutishauser Systems Neuroscience Laboratory. Their respective original 2023 and 2024 copyright wording is preserved, not harmonized by this audit.

Four retained EEGLAB FIR helpers have their own embedded copyright/license notices: `firfilt.m`, `firws.m`, `pop_eegfiltnew.m`, and `windows.m`. They credit Andreas Widmann, University of Leipzig, and explicitly permit GPL version 2 or, at the recipient's option, any later version. Those full embedded notices remain unchanged. `licenses/GPL-3.0.txt` supplies the exact already-local full GPLv3 license text as an available later-version option; including it does not remove the original v2-or-later choice or assert project-wide GPL relicensing. The text is captured from the same pinned upstream source inventory's `helpers/3rdParty/permutationTest/LICENSE.txt`; that path supplies the generic license text, not the unrelated permutation implementation.

The retained historical `package/private/RIGHTS.md` states that the prospective `pac.py` filter implementation should be treated as GPL-v2-or-later-compatible derivative source when distributed with these helpers. That statement remains explicit; the repository's root MIT notice is not a replacement for it. The complete prospective reference implementations retain their own provenance and independence contracts. This content audit does not establish a new legal theory of derivation or extend one file's notice to all project code.

Public author emails, scientific citations, upstream example identifiers and ordinary source/data directory literals are preserved in their original public-source context. They are not private participant reidentification records acquired by this audit. The one Windows-1252 MATLAB body is preserved through exact-byte transport, with no replacement of its original characters.

DANDI 000673 / 0.240118.2135 is cited as the source dataset. Existing source metadata records CC-BY-4.0 attribution for that dataset. No NWB recording, stimulus, clinical mask, author example MAT mask, private control seed, numerical answer, identity key, fitted model or signed acquisition URL is distributed here. Metadata citation does not establish consent, clinical provenance or permission for a later redistribution of authentic recordings. Those decisions remain separate from this source snapshot.

Full fixed-source inclusion is distinct from operational dependency closure. The public inventory identifies unshipped upstream/historical source locators and native/runtime dependencies. Nothing in this notice claims that the examples execute, the original paper is reproduced, the source is scientifically qualified, or a benchmark result has been obtained.
