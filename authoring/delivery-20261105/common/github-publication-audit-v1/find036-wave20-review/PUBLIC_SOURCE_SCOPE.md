## Scientific and access conditions

Labels 1 and 2 are head-entry observations outside this endpoint. The original complete X/y MAT files remain required for native reproduction. The fixed window is **[-1,+1] seconds around head withdrawal**, forty 50-ms bins per recorded unit. This is retrospective within-recording discrimination from processed features. Whole-session normalization and symmetric smoothing are transductive; later training-only scaling cannot undo that preprocessing. This is not the six-second anticipation result, chronological forecasting, raw-spike reconstruction, new-session or new-animal generalization, causal evidence, an entire Figure 6 replication or whole-paper reproduction.
