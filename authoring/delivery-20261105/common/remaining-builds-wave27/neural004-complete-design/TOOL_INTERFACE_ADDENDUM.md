# Uniform HMP tool and mandatory diagnostic extraction

This additive clarification supersedes the permissive wording "may expose" in DESIGN.md and "each per-trial mass if computed" in ARTIFACTS_AND_REVIEW.md. It preserves every frozen V1 body, all source fit/count choices and all conditions. It is a fixed scientific interface specification, not runtime code or qualified data.

Every H and EH context, for both P and I modes, has the **same complete available HMP schema** for its own outer fold and all three event counts. An actor may choose which outputs to read within the common resource limit, but the operator cannot choose a different output subset, precision, support, model or normalization according to the actor, person or outcome. Existence and complete inventory are frozen before authoring. On-demand/range-bounded delivery is allowed solely as an access/size mechanism; it neither removes data nor changes the available schema.

The service has three concrete read-only operation families:

1. **Model state:** the exact fold/count source and fitted-state identity; declared preprocessing order; complete train-fitted PCA weights with named axes; training component normalization rules/state; channel magnitudes, duration parameters, grouping maps and event-template identity; complete EM trace/stop status; full source/version/fit-support lineage. Numerical states use fixed explicit shapes/dtypes/units and safe non-executable serialization. No arbitrary expression or provider-supplied completed prediction is accepted.
2. **Evaluation inventory:** complete target-free opaque person/trial roster for either outer training or held support; original sample-count/time-axis and response-offset convention; exact evaluated grouping/M/location=False and model state; ordinary and zero-gain result/failure dispositions. Actual held per-person normalization is labeled retrospective/transductive and its admitted operator-produced state is retained. The tool exposes no original epoch IDs, source paths, event codes, contrast/side/response metadata, correctness, raw waveform or cross-correlation array. Contrast training labels arrive through the separate common training view, not an accidental tool coordinate.
3. **Trial evaluation bytes:** ordinary and zero-gain event marginal arrays for every event/sample on the complete original support, original max-marginal event times, six intervals/two sums only where k=5, general k+1 intervals otherwise, and posterior-derived retained endpoint with units. Also expose the ordinary and zero **per-trial log masses and their signed difference**, plus finite/positive-normalizer status and the exact scalar group total. Failures and negative values remain visible. No 4/6-event interval inherits the fixed5 biological labels. Every quantity joins to the exact evaluation inventory and source state.

Complete ordinary/zero marginal/state retention is mandatory for the operator even if an actor never requests all bytes. The access profile must state preventive response-byte/live-memory/serialization limits and charge all reads, hashing and interpretation requests; it must support delivery of the full admitted inventory without quietly selecting shorter cases.

The pinned source originally returns a group likelihood and normalized marginal arrays, not a separately exported per-trial likelihood vector. The later implementation must therefore retain the per-trial forward/backward mass immediately before the source's group log sum, or compute an independently reviewed numerically equivalent quantity **with the same group M and support**. It must preserve the current ordinary scalar and posterior result and prove equivalence on authorized engineering controls before science. Re-running one trial at a time with a different M is not that extraction. No completed-report callback or guessed division of a group score supplies per-trial mass.

The 312 evaluation count covers the prescribed model/support/ordinary-or-zero evaluations. Extra extraction, copying, stable log evaluation, hashes, serialization or duplicate verification calls have real work costs and are recorded separately; no added HMP fit is implied. Source equivalence and the actual environment remain qualification gates. B never receives these EEG-trained states or results. E has no supplied fitted HMP service; any method it trains incurs its own full authorized work.
