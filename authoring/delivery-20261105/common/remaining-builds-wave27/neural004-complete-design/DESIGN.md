## Actor modes and information conditions

Freeze eight global policies before numerical task exposure: two actor modes by four information views. Each policy and its generated-state rules are source-bound. A policy can legitimately analyze labeled outer-training data and fit/revise methods; its global information rules cannot change after results are seen.

**Program mode P:** the committed authoring policy generates/seals the full prediction procedure after training-only investigation, before the held-person view is opened. That sealed procedure may perform its declared deterministic processing of the entire unlabeled held-person view. It cannot request new human/model authoring after held exposure.

**Investigator mode I:** a fresh investigator may analyze the whole admitted held-person view, run permitted tools and revise generated code within its local budget, using only outer-training labels as feedback. Its inquiry is transductive and retrospective. It must seal all held forecasts before any target/scorer disclosure. Its within-training search diagnostics are not unbiased estimates of the complete investigator's performance. No cross-person conversation or memory is admitted.

Both modes receive the identical paper-informed question, source documentation, lawful training labels and general coding/analysis runtime. Provider identity, context/image capabilities, package environment and finite resource caps must be matched across the four views and pinned before authoring. No external dataset or pretrained task model is silently available. Genuine provider/deployment/spend authority remains an execution gate, not granted by this design.

## Independent references and scoring

Paired improvement is mean_h[L(Bref,h)-L(a,h)], with every difference retained. ## Scientific evidence and uncertainty

Preserve full person-level output and original report uncertainty limits. The 26 people, not their trial count, are the units for reported generalization heterogeneity. Overlapping LOPO training sets mean SD/sqrt26 is only descriptive; do not call it a calibrated population interval. Report individual paired losses, the mean, median, range and leave-one-person-out deletion sensitivity of the fixed 26 loss vector. This last sensitivity recomputes only the descriptive aggregation and is not a model refit or inferential jackknife. No p-value, population CI, significance threshold or causal claim is supplied by this design.

HMP marginals provide within-model timing uncertainty, conditional on fitted parameters and assumptions. Retain complete marginals and finite normalizers; summarize central quantiles for readability without calling them biological confidence intervals.
