# Required artifacts, actor sequence and scientific review

No artifact listed here currently exists as an authentic result. These are exact roles/required fields for later source implementation. A caller-provided Boolean or callback returning finished scores cannot establish scientific execution, input custody or target protection.

## Required private operator artifacts

- `source_admission`: exact 26-file roster/pins; source metadata schema and units; 32-channel order; reader/environment source identities; native sample axis; original trial membership, eligibility reasons and retained crop length; complete original-to-opaque identity map; source target-alias audit. Target mapping and protected target digests remain private. This is an independently authenticated admission, not a self-asserted success flag.
- `answer_manifest`: task/version, each original person/trial identity, opaque identity per context, exact logged contrast value and precision, source field/index and admission pin. Original RT/instruction/length support is joined exactly. No independently observed cognitive event labels are invented.
- `context_manifest`: policy/mode/view/outer-person identity, complete authorized training/held ID rosters, target-presence policy, paper/source exposure, trained-tool capabilities, isolated mount/environment identity, resource profile, immutable view pins and excluded aliases. It contains no single-target hash oracle.
- `global_policy_commitment`: eight prediction policies plus the two reconstruction-mode policies, their source and tool/environment/resource identities, authoring chronology and proof they were committed before numerical exposure. Static policy is distinct from train-fitted/adaptively generated state. Identical source may be shared with explicit identity; no duplicate independent-agent claim follows.
- `fit_state`: method/family/configuration ID; exact complete fitting/inner-validation/outer-disjoint rosters; every preprocessing mean/scale/constant mask/feature order; all numerical parameters or full kernel rows; training/source/environment/tool pins; solver residual/status; generated-code/revision ancestry; selection diagnostics and failures; process/observer/cost lineage. Any actual prior state access is logged. It never contains an outer-held target.
- `hmp_state`: all original fold/count states, PCA source/weights and transformed support, event/duration parameters, exact full EM trace and stopping reason, numerical validity, fitted grouping maps, source release/compatibility adapter and original input pins. The fixed fit/transform recipes remain distinct. No report-derived or public-paper-derived synthetic state is admitted.
- `hmp_evaluation`: ordinary or zero gain, outer/train-or-held/count, state pin, exact group/ordered trial support/M, location flag, PMF/normalizer convention, scalar total and each per-trial mass if computed, complete event marginals and source offset/axis, finite-domain/failure status and costs. If per-trial quantities require more source calls than an ordinary group transform, record them; do not assume an aggregate score can be split without computation. Preserve grouping/M while extracting marginals and per-trial masses.
- `reference_selection`: complete 25-inner-person table for all 23 unique candidate configurations, including shared B candidates, every failure and actual fitting cost; B/N selections under the fixed rule; all final complete fitted states/forecasts; competency/admission status. No outer target or outcome chooses the reference.
- `actor_trace`: ordered provider/tool/source-revision actions with exact request/response/code/source-byte identities, permitted view and fitted-state references, actual costs, errors, termination and independent observer closure. The trace proves what was attempted; it cannot supply a fabricated scientific result.
- `prediction_seal`: the complete ordered `(opaque_trial_id, prediction)` panel for one actor/mode/view/outer person; exact state/action closure and source bindings; every prediction finite and in [0,1]; complete roster equality, no duplicates/extras/missing records. Reference panels obey the same schema. The seal is durably closed before any held target access by the scorer or explanatory actors.
- `reconstruction_seal`: unchanged existing 26×114 report, original unit/support/undefined patterns and method/state evidence. A numerical mismatch is a reconstruction outcome. A failed HMP dependency can block H/EH or reconstruction but does not erase an otherwise independently valid B/E prediction outcome; whole-study completion remains false until required components are present.
- `grade`: target-release audit; complete seal/state/support joins; all per-trial squared errors, all 26 person MSEs, macro MSE, paired B-reference differences, defined secondary displays and failure status. No composite mixture with reconstruction units or human rubric. Grade source is independent from actor-generated methods.
- `study_closeout`: complete planned/attempted/completed/failed job/context roster; source, state, output and selection joins; provider/CPU/memory/storage/serialization/failure costs; natural termination and data-custody evidence; explicit unknowns. Missing physical/runtime evidence cannot be laundered by a producer receipt.

Artifacts containing authentic targets, original identities or signed acquisition details are private and are not source-publication payloads. Current design files contain only methods and already reviewed source bindings.

Both may access all held unlabeled covariates within their declared retrospective/transductive policy. Any normalization/adaptation depending on the held waveform is declared as such. 4. Seal every held forecast for all 208 selected prediction contexts and the reference panels. Failed contexts retain all attempts and are closed. No target/score feedback reaches another context. Only then open the target roster to the independent scorer.
5. Open the two reconstruction-mode programmes, each with 26 separate fold contexts, under the unchanged full-data contract and general scientific runtime. They receive source/data but no private reference report or completed operator reference states. Their actual analyses/fits are additional candidate work, not free reuse of the operator's 78 fits. Collect and seal each full report.
6. Create eight optional **required-for-reasoning-score** post-seal explanation contexts, one per prediction policy, with the same predeclared common evidence packet: completed source-bound cohort summaries, available reconstruction diagnostics, reference comparison results, failures/unknowns and fixed spatial evidence. They can write a scientific interpretation, not revise prediction/reconstruction results. If the reasoning evaluation is not executed, numerical results remain separate and the full reasoning score is absent.
7. Independent graders/experts issue separate numerical and reasoning records. Source policy, implementation, empirical reference competence and whole-study qualification are distinct statuses.

The complete specified evaluation has 208 prediction contexts +52 reconstruction-fold contexts +8 post-seal explanation contexts=268 actor contexts per evaluated model/policy set. Each of the 26 original people remains represented; these contexts are not extra tasks. Additional actor repetitions/models require a separate matched prospective cost/exposure plan. Reference training/observer jobs do not count as actor contexts.

## Matched access and hard prohibitions

Tool providers accept concrete operations on authenticated source/fit state and return actual bounded arrays/artifacts, not `predict_success`, `grade_ready` or arbitrary finished-score callbacks. No stage/contrast target enters a tool call through a filename, exception, metadata coordinate or reference result. Observer, protected scorer and actor process have different authority; source-level plans do not prove their physical separation.

A deployed profile must state exact finite limits for model/provider calls and input/output tokens, ordinary tools, generated-code bytes, process/fit/query counts, scientific and closeout clocks, cumulative reads/writes, retained bytes and peak live memory. It must include failed attempts and the full work graph. These values are currently unadmitted. Identical limits apply to compared actor modes/views, with the HMP tool allowance equally available to H/EH. Shared prepared immutable tool states are reported separately from candidate search costs; their construction is included in total study costs. No claimed matched comparison proceeds with missing caps, unidentified model/provider/environment, or unqualified tool behavior.

The reconstruction contexts and post-result explanation contexts are prohibited sources for all still-live prediction contexts, even when the same person/model/provider appears in multiple folds. New context identifiers alone do not prove physical isolation; real deployment must establish it. External model training exposure to this public dataset is an unresolved contamination factor, not erased by aliases.

## Fixed evidence packet and spatial records

The explanatory packet includes the original 2,964-cell report and defined comparison errors; all26 signed prediction/reference losses; all three-count ordinary/zero HMP diagnostics with within-model uncertainty and failures; and the spatial records below. It identifies which outputs are computed from the same inferred alignment and which use fixed alignment. No preferred sign, model or physiological name appears as the answer key.

For every person, five-event index and each of14 channel pairs, retain one event-aligned record: overall mean left-minus-right voltage, sample/trial counts and four instruction×response-choice means with their supports (undefined when empty). This creates26×5×14=1,820 records, each carrying its five summaries. Actual event indices and same-observation selection dependence remain explicit.

For every person, each of the six fixed windows in MATHEMATICS.md and each of14 pairs, retain one window record with the same five means/supports, all per-trial intersection lengths/undefined flags, and the fixed five-column OLS result `[1,c,a,c*a,right_response]` on finite nonempty-window observations. Use the existing singular-value rank criterion1e-10 and rcond1e-10; rank<5 yields explicit undefined coefficients. There are26×6×14=2,184 such descriptive regressions/records. Undefined window observations remain in the full roster; they do not change prediction support. Truncation depends on RT and limits interpretation. These windows and source-defined regressions are predeclared prospective descriptions, not paper-reproduced numerical targets or independent event validation.

No spatial p-value, cluster procedure, Bambi posterior or global physiological-correctness label is supplied. Exact reproduction of those omitted paper analyses would require their own complete source/environment/semantic specification. This design preserves rather than disguises that limitation.

## Separate blinded reasoning rubric

Each of the eight complete explanatory reports is scored independently by two neuroscience/statistical reviewers who do not see actor/model identity or competitor rankings. The report must state which evidence is actually available and link each substantive numerical claim to an artifact, field, support and units. Reviewers can inspect the common evidence packet; they cannot reward agreement with a favored paper result.

Six dimensions are scored0,1,2:

1. **Evidence correctness:** 0 for material fabricated/misquoted numbers or support;1 for mostly correct but incompletely linked claims;2 for accurate, fully linked claims with units and failure disposition.
2. **Information-source reasoning:** distinguish current-trial gain, RT/window structure, training-EEG priors and the independently trained B reference. Full credit requires explaining what the reported comparisons can and cannot establish.
3. **Identifiability and alternatives:** distinguish latent timing from observed truth and discuss specific competing sensory/motor/alignment/model explanations tied to the actual evidence. Generic caveats without a discriminating connection earn at most1.
4. **Exposure and uncertainty:** correctly describe retrospective preprocessing, paper-informed groupings, overlapping person folds and within-model uncertainty; do not inflate trials into independent people or descriptive variability into a population CI.
5. **Method/source fidelity:** identify material departures from the original paper and recognize the complete support/normalization/selection constraints. A fitted-state/target-leakage mistake that changes interpretation scores0 here.
6. **Discriminating next analysis:** propose a concrete further observation/control whose possible outcomes would separate stated explanations, including its additional assumptions and resource/data needs. Do not award points for merely promising larger models or stronger significance.

For dimensions2–6:0 means a material unsupported/confounded conclusion;1 means partial correct reasoning without adequate evidence linkage or a discriminating account;2 requires both correctness and specific evidence-supported reasoning. Record independent dimension scores and a rationale before adjudication. A third blinded reviewer resolves disagreements only for an additional consensus record; retain both originals. Report raw agreement, weighted agreement statistics when estimable and their small-report-count limitations. No validated human reasoning score exists until this process and reviewer reliability have been evaluated. An LLM judge can be a separately labeled exploratory aid only; it is not the reference authority.

A mechanically checked numerical claim ledger flags arithmetic/support inconsistencies for reviewers but does not substitute for scientific judgment. The rubric total0..12 is reported separately with all dimensions and disagreements. It is never averaged with MSE, HMP likelihood units or reconstruction errors, and cannot establish cognitive-stage ground truth.
