# Normative prospective mathematics, support and nulls

This is a source-derived specification, not executed numerical evidence. Profile P preserves the pinned pac.py/reference.py/score.py estimand. It does not silently cure its biological assumptions or the accuracy-support gate. All calculations use finite real double precision with qualified library versions; booleans are never accepted as numeric input. Native N is separate.

## Signal and features

Let x[w,t,k] be volts at channel w, retained trial t, k=0…999, and r[w,t] the equal-co-located-unit mean rate in Hz from the same half-open 2.5-second interval. FS=400. Trial order, load, correctness and probe labels are exactly joined; no interpolation/resampling/scaling inference is allowed.

For passband (l,h), 0<l<h<200, set df=min(max(0.25*l,2),l,200-h), M=2*ceil((3.3*400/df)/2), j=-M/2…M/2 and Hamming window v[j]. Define LP(f)[j]=(2*f/400)*sinc(2*f*j/400)*v[j], divided by its coefficient sum. The bandpass kernel is LP(h+df/2)-LP(l-df/2). The exact Hamming convention and sinc(pi*u)/(pi*u) matter. Repeat each trial's first/last value by M/2 samples, take valid symmetric convolution to obtain exactly 1,000 values, then the N=1000 Fourier analytic signal. For an independent explicit analytic transform: multiply DFT bins by 1 at DC/Nyquist, 2 at bins1…499 and 0 at bins501…999, then inverse DFT. No cross-trial concatenation before filtering or Hilbert transform.

For low centre f in (4,6), use passband f±1; for high centre g in 70,75,…,140 use g±f. Let phi be low analytic phase, a the nonnegative high amplitude, psi the high phase. There are exactly30 pairs, ordered low outer/high inner. Equal-weight theta power is the trial-time mean squared analytic amplitude, averaged over the two low bands. Gamma power averages squared high amplitude over time and all30 pair-defined bands; it is not one broad70–140 filter. Nuisance z=(log(theta_power),log(gamma_power),log1p(rate)). Any nonpositive theta/gamma power in any retained trial makes **all four channel design/contrast estimates unsupported** in the historical P code. No epsilon or selective bad-row removal is permitted; disclose this conservative all-channel gate.

Waveform asymmetry is (mean(max(diff(x),0)^2)−mean(min(diff(x),0)^2))/(their sum); zero denominator returns zero. This is a raw slope-energy proxy, not bycycle cycle symmetry. Harmonic PLV is |mean(exp(i*(round(g/f)*phi−psi)))| over selected trials/samples, averaged over30 pairs. Match Python's ties-to-even round, including half-integer ratios; it is a declared proxy, not proof of harmonics or independent generators. Phase/power/rate or surrogate values never use unselected adjacent/probe samples.

## Matching and subcomparisons

Stratum is (original row//35,probe), so four chronological blocks and two probe categories. Metadata rows remain on their original0…139 axis after artefact exclusion. Within each stratum, order each side by opaque trial ID. Count-only chooses the first min(nA,nB) trials on each side. Nuisance matching computes each covariate's population SD (ddof=0) over both complete comparison groups in that stratum, before any pair selection. A pair is valid when every absolute standardized difference≤0.5. Zero-SD covariates require exact equality; use denominator1 only to compute the necessarily-zero valid difference.

Among valid pairs, maximize cardinality without replacement, then minimize the sum of three squared standardized differences. Each valid edge cost≤0.75. The existing SciPy linear-assignment recipe gives every invalid edge penalty4*(min(nA,nB)+1), solves the complete rectangular assignment and discards invalid edges. Its exact deterministic tie outcome is part of the declared numerical recipe: sorted input IDs, SciPy1.15.3 and platform-qualified tie handling. Do not call an alternate optimal pairing numerically identical if it changes PAC; an independent verifier may validate optimum and reconstruct the explicitly selected tie rule. A future lexicographic tie redesign would be a versioned estimator change requiring review.

Concatenate selected pairs in stratum order. Require ≥10 pairs overall for each subcomparison, not10 per stratum. For each design, load is A=(load3,correct) versus B=(load1,correct). Accuracy has part0=(load1 correct versus incorrect), part1=(load3 correct versus incorrect). The channel contrast is A−B for load and half the sum of the two within-load A−B vectors for accuracy. Both accuracy parts are required. Fixed strata, calipers and support floors cannot be loosened after inspecting effects.

## Tort MI and deterministic surrogate schedule

For phase p and amplitude a concatenate selected trials and samples, wrap phase to [-pi,pi), and use 18 equal bins. Let n_b be bin count and m_b=(sum of amplitudes in bin b)/n_b. If any n_b=0, or sum(m_b)≤0/nonfinite, MI is undefined. Otherwise q_b=m_b/sum(m), MI=max(0, [sum over q_b>0 of q_b*log(q_b)+log18]/log18). The max only guards tiny negative roundoff; no pseudocount/pseudopower is inserted. +pi wraps to−pi. Zero q_b contributes zero. Early and late MI use samples0…499 and500…999 of the same already-filtered analytic signals; they do not refilter halves or create independent trials.

RNG seeds are the big-endian integer represented by the first8 SHA-256 bytes of UTF-8 `neural042-v1:` plus identity. Use NumPy2.2.6 default_rng/PCG64 under an explicitly pinned runtime. Condition identity is channelOpaqueID:design:contrast:part:a_or_b. Do not change IDs/order or reseed each frequency. In each condition one RNG is created, then30 pairs are traversed; within each pair and each of200 draws, first draw a uniform integer j∈{1,…,n−1} and cyclically roll amplitude trials by j, then draw one uniform offset o_t∈{200,…,800} for each trial and use a[t,(k−o_t) mod1000]. Record all selected draw/index descriptors as private reference provenance; raw private keys are never used to run this mathematics.

For each pair compute observed, early, late MI;200 trial-roll MI and200 time-shift MI. Any undefined MI makes the whole condition unsupported. A condition must have ≥10 trials. Average observed MI and each surrogate mean equally across30 pairs. `corrected_mi = observed_mi − trial_null_mean`; this can be negative. Other feature metrics average over the selected trials. Compare conditions as above. Across designs, the RNG identity differs by design; fixed source fidelity is not a claim of a variance-optimal common-random-number comparison.

The trial rotation resamples n−1 distinct shifts with replacement; 200 repetitions are not200 distinct permutations. The time shift introduces a seam and only preserves the amplitude sequence's spectral magnitudes. Neither null guarantees exchangeability across drift/probe strata or all nonstationarities. They are declared calibration/robustness procedures. Same-trial PAC, event-locking, cross-frequency harmonics and condition-dependent selection require distinct interpretation. No null p-value is computed from treating those200 repetitions as independent biological subjects.

## Hierarchy and exact row support

Each channel yields up to four vectors of eleven metrics. An unsupported condition/subcomparison invalidates the affected channel contrast, not the other independently supported contrasts unless the all-channel power gate above applies. Retain explicit channel/part/frequency failure reasons privately. For each design/contrast, a session mean averages its supported channels equally. A person's mean averages supported sessions equally, regardless of their channel counts. n_channels is the total supported channel count across those sessions; n_sessions counts those sessions. No session/participant is replaced by a zero vector. All36 person identities remain in the report.

The exact144 keys are sortedPerson × [count_only,nuisance_matched] × [load3_minus_load1_correct,correct_minus_incorrect_within_load]. Null metric rows have both support counts0. Non-null rows have all11 finite metrics. The accuracy row averages its two load parts at the channel level *before* session/person means; it is not a separate pooled count-weighted contrast. Group keys are the four design/contrast combinations. Their n_patients must match non-null person rows. This resolves the full144/four semantics directly from reference.aggregate and score.report_valid.

For n≥2 person corrected-MI contrasts d_i, group mean is sum(d_i)/n. Using identity design:contrast and one RNG, draw10,000 size-n patient bootstrap samples with replacement; each mean uses equal drawn-person weight, including duplicate draws. ci95 uses linear quantile interpolation at0.025/0.975. After those draws, draw10,000 independent length-n ±1 sign vectors from the same RNG stream. Let T0=abs(mean(d)); p=(1+number of |mean(sign*d)|≥T0)/(10001). With n<2, mean,ci95 andp are all null (even though a person's own statistic exists). Use the exact finite-RNG recipe for reference agreement; it is not an exact enumeration of all2^n signs.

Scientific interpretation: independent people and suitable sign symmetry are assumptions, not results of the code. Different session counts, clinical selection and reuse of the same data across four outcomes remain. Four unadjusted p-values do not constitute familywise evidence. The percentiles are descriptive estimator resampling intervals, not guaranteed coverage under arbitrary clinical population sampling. Any optional multiplicity adjustment/paired-support diagnostic must retain the canonical unadjusted rows alongside it and carry its own assumptions.
