## 7. Biological nulls, uncertainty and interpretation

The 200 trial-rotation and 200 temporal-shift surrogates per frequency/condition calibrate the specified within-condition estimators; they are not 200 subjects and do not create an exact general biological null. Rotation has only n−1 distinct trial shifts for n trials, with replacement across draws; event-locked nonstationarity, drift and exchangeability limits remain. Time shifts preserve amplitude-sequence Fourier magnitudes but introduce wrap seams. A positive mean-subtracted value alone does not establish separate oscillators or a mechanism.

Preserve the canonical patient-bootstrap intervals and patient-level sign-flip statistic, but label their assumptions. Independence is at biological person; sign symmetry/exchangeability under a zero-mean contrast is needed for interpreting the sign-flip p-value as a test. Matched trials/wires/loads are not independent sign-flip units. Accuracy is observational; conditioning on correctness or matching neural-derived power/rate may induce selection/collider bias. Matching supplies bounded balance, not exact covariate equality or causal identification.

Canonical four group p-values are unadjusted descriptive outputs. A separately labeled Holm/Bonferroni family adjustment may be calculated from the four valid individual p-values, counting unsupported tests conservatively as unavailable rather than favorable; any inferential claim still requires each individual null assumption. No familywise guarantee is asserted from the current bootstrap intervals. If comparing count-only to matched effects or agent losses, use person-paired intersections and report changed support explicitly; retain the complete original support-specific results as primary. Do not silently change the estimand to a favorable supported subset.
