## Native scientific conventions

### Behavior

`reference_behavior.m:18-53` selects all 23 animals and last two standard/first two reversal days. Correctness is outcome 1 or 4. Standard cue-short/long strata use `<100` and actual delay 2.5/5; reversal cue-short is actual delay 5 and cue-long actual delay 2.5. Reversal-condition slots on standard days remain initialized NaN. Latency subtracts behavior column 4, retains strict `0 < latency < 3`, then takes the **first retained entry in source list order**, not an independently sorted minimum (`:65-95`). Medians pool matching and nonmatching trials regardless of correctness (`:97-102`). Empty conditions/licks must remain source-undefined; no imputation or favorable removal. Validate list chronology before describing this as earliest physical lick.

### Sequence detection and sequentiality

`reference_sequence.m:20-39,49-81` retains `iscell > 0.3`, 2,000 circular shifts, p threshold 0.05, reliability 0.2, peak neighbors +/-31 frames, odor frames 63:94, sequentiality frames 95:169 and three entropy bins. Short/long shift windows are 63:169 and 63:247. Both classes come from standard-coded trials, including on reversal days. Strictly larger short-cue mean peak selects short; equal peaks select long (`:132-138`). `max` retains native first-maximum tie behavior. Reliability is positive activity at the selected peak divided by the **short-condition trial count** captured at `:122`; selected-long trial-count equality is assumed, not checked. Equality at 0.2 passes (`:145-148`), contrary to 'more than' and obsolete 0.15 comments. A peak must strictly exceed every neighbor (`:150-153`).

For each eligible ROI, 2,000 shifts independently sample `floor(rand(ntrials,1)*window_length)` and circularly shift each trial; shifted peak is the maximum of trial mean with `omitnan` (`:156-164`). The p value is `count(shifted_peak > observed_peak)/2000`, excludes ties and has no plus-one correction; p==0.05 fails (`:165-168`). These are pointwise source selections, not family-wise/FDR-controlled discoveries. Original ROI indices are restored with the cumulative `goodcells` mask. Sequentiality averages the selected time-cell signals across standard trials and uses the original entropy product square root (`:187-229`), including `hist`, native `nansum`, divisions by cell count/summed activity and `log2(numUnits)`. Empty/singleton/zero-activity/negative or NaN inputs can yield undefined, complex or failed native computation; no replacement rule is present.

Fig2F uses **short-window selection for both cue classes**, pooling significant-cell counts across the two reversal days within each animal. Half-open frame edges are `[63,79,94,110,125,141,156,171]`. Original script lines 816-841 yields percentages; exporter lines 30-41 deliberately yields the contract's fractions, equivalent to dividing original percentages by 100. Zero selected cells produces undefined division; never drop such an animal or use the long-window selection for long cues.

### Time decoding

`reference_time.m:20-25,61-86,103-151` uses seven ten-frame means: 97:106,107:116,...,157:166. The '10 time bins' comment is incorrect. `iscell > 0.3` selects source cells. `p.minrois=10000` means **all only when good-cell count <=10000**; above that, the source selects a 10,000-cell `randperm` subsample (`:72-79`). The source says 'all cells' but the code controls. The full census must establish which branch applies; the contract's allowed maximum 100,000 ROIs does not establish this gate.

Each cue/session helper concatenates bin-major trial observations and leaves out one **complete trial across every bin**. `fitcecoc` and `predict` are invoked without options. One shuffled training-target permutation is generated per helper call before the trial-LOO loop, and reused across its folds (`:112-136`). Real/shuffle matrices count predicted rows/actual columns, then divide by number of held-out trials; the scalar metric is MATLAB `corr` of all actual/predicted bins. No candidate-reported score, held-out frame leakage, alternative confusion normalization or arbitrary classifier replacement is source equivalent.

### Odor decoding

`reference_odor.m:20-28` obtains half-second bins from `floor(frametimes/0.5)`, forces the first entry to -4 and the final five entries to21, then adds5. The selected exporter requires26 resulting bins; exact frametime/schema admission must establish this rather than infer it merely from402frames. Every standard-coded condition pair is compared on all four days. On reversal days it also compares matched actual-short, matched actual-long and four match/nonmatch conditions. Original nearest-standard expressions `blockof5-1*5` and `blockof20-1*20` are retained (`:92-149`), including native first-minimum ties and possible repeated selected standard trials. They are not equivalent to parenthesized block starts and must not be silently corrected.

Each `binnedbinarySVM` call forms equal-size class0/class1 groups, seven contiguous within-class blocks, and train fraction6/7. Whole class trial counts must be equal and divisible by7; source code assumes this (`:172-198`). The training-label permutation is drawn once per helper and reused across every fold/bin. `fitcsvm` is called without options, and seven fold accuracies are averaged equally (`:189-215`). Default-model/failure behavior must be locked to native R2021a.

The second-odor-aligned family prepends/appends seven NaN bins (3.5 seconds at nominal0.5s bins), executes the helper over **all33bins**, and only afterwards overwrites the first seven result bins with NaN (`:106-109,164-165`). This source rule is retained even though the selected actual delays differ by2.5seconds. It is not safe to skip padded-bin fits or to assume classifier/predict behavior on all-NaN rows without qualification; source failure is not an admitted undefined result. Source `decode.totalgoodcells(animal,day)` omits daytype (`:71`); that metadata bug is preserved and is not one of the eight exported families.

### Information curves

`reference_information.m:19-28,77-119` uses five-frame means over frames1:400, baseline bin3 (frames11:15), usebins7:80 (74bins),20activity bins and `iscell >0.3`. Only two reversal days and four source conditions contribute. Source discretization is **floor(activity / ((max-min)/20))**, without subtracting the minimum; max/min cover all trials and all80bins of each ROI. Preserve this retrospective source calculation for reconstruction; it is not training-only preprocessing for improvement.

For each condition and output bin, `DeanMutualInfoPerCell` computes per-cell activity-pattern information about **baseline versus current time-bin identity**, then the outer code averages cells with `omitnan` (`:111-119,128-240`). The historical family name `odor_information` does not turn it into direct mutual information between activity and odor labels. ## Projection fidelity and disposition gates

Direct text comparison found the original numerical prefix and local-helper bodies preserved in all five projections. Changed lines are declared callable/fresh-output plumbing, exact variable-specific load paths, removal of behavior `clearvars`, explicit sequence169/247 parameter, and appended result saves. Plot/statistical sections and saved-answer loads are omitted. `SOURCE_CORRESPONDENCE.json` records retained original/reference line spans and source hashes. The original histogram computation omitted with plotting is explicitly recreated by the exporter with percentage-to-fraction conversion. This confirms inspected textual correspondence, not native equivalence or whole-paper reproduction.

The existing ABI accepts finite real values or JSON null; the exporter maps NaN to null and refuses Inf or complex (`export_values.m:97-105`; `contract.py:79-93`). It does **not** distinguish structural absence, empty support, algorithmic NaN, native failure or unstarted work. The next design must retain a complete56,868-slot disposition ledger and original native artifacts: finite value, structural source-undefined, computed source-undefined with reason/evidence, native failed, and unstarted. Failed/unstarted must never be fabricated as source NaN or omitted. Inf/complex currently remain a hard unresolved scientific admission gate; they must not be coerced to null or finite. These typed dispositions are a required design extension, not a claim that the old collector already supports them. Tiny negative information from native floating arithmetic is also rejected by the current nonnegative ABI; do not silently clip.

Before any future execution, independently authenticate originals and full structural membership; establish session-specific cells and both-variant identity; freeze exact runtime/defaults, missing-class/empty-array behavior and exporter dimensions; freeze private reference source/resources/RNG provenance; require natural native exit, original-clock closure, durable complete outputs and independent admission.
