## Scientific scope

This is a fixed-twelve-person lateralization methods adaptation, not the paper's
quality-controlled N2pc characterization. It measures signed PO7/PO8
contralateral-minus-ipsilateral 200–300ms amplitude, with equal target-field and
equal-person weights. It does not reproduce the paper's cohort, complete
preprocessing/QC or measurement window. The public method contract defines the
actual operator and limitations; a raw lateralized difference does not itself
establish artifact-free covert visual attention or a population effect.

No expected sign, effect size, fixed-electrode cancellation, significance,
model-difficulty or successful-execution claim follows from this source notice.
