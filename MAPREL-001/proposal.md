# MAPREL-001: signed published-map comparison under a centroid-spin null

This is a paper-derived **method control**, not a reproduction of a named
paper finding and not a calibrated hard task. It compares the released second
functional-connectivity gradient with HCP-S1200 cortical thickness across
Schaefer400/7 fsLR32k parcels. The 400 parcels are spatial locations, not
independent participants. Source-specific cohort information and reuse limits
are documented in `environment/SOURCE_NOTICE.md`.

Alexander-Bloch et al. (2018; doi:10.1016/j.neuroimage.2018.05.070) motivates
spherical correspondence testing; the neuromaps framework (Markello et al.,
2022; doi:10.1038/s41592-022-01625-w) supplies the released maps and computational
conventions. Neither paper is claimed to establish this particular map-pair
result. A conditional centroid-spin result does not establish causation,
absence of association, exact autocorrelation preservation or universal null
calibration.

## What is measured

The participant must authenticate and align the source surfaces and atlas,
recover signed parcel means and supports, generate a declared coupled-hemisphere
spin trace, and calculate an inclusive two-sided Monte Carlo p-value from the
submitted values. The public method contract specifies the numerical
conventions. There is no hidden target sign, effect size, p-value, null spread
or prose conclusion. `original`, `vasa` and `hungarian` assignment methods are
accepted with the disclosed seed/count range.

## Repair rationale

The preceding verifier admitted a fabricated Gaussian null with suitable
moments. The replacement independently reconstructs canonical source geometry,
regenerates every declared assignment, checks signed source measurements and
replays observed/null correlations and exact counts. It does not infer a valid
spatial null from its width. The historical answer bank is not an authority.

BrainModelAxis/vertex-ID joins replace an unverified flattened-position
assumption. The selected release actually has a complete vertex layout, so this
repair does **not** claim the previous flattening demonstrably changed these
particular measurements. Source identities, support and metadata are now
explicitly checked.

Data are downloaded and hash-verified during the image build, then baked into
the runtime image. Runtime analysis and verification are offline. There are
13 authenticated scientific/provenance files, totaling 2,602,422 bytes. The
original source bytes are not committed. Public access does not resolve all
map-specific redistribution or commercial rights; publication of the bundle
or image requires separate review.

## Verification boundary

Production scoring is all-or-nothing for complete valid evidence. Manufactured
regressions exercise malformed inputs, numerical edge cases and trace integrity.
Separate authoring QA checks equivalent encodings and effective mutations of
genuine outputs; these perturbation tests are not imposed on participant
submissions. Oracle/verifier agreement shares NiBabel and NumPy/SciPy numerical
dependencies and is not independent scientific validation. Local validation
receipts and any model calibration must be reported separately. No frontier
failure rate or difficulty claim is made.
