# DEVCONN-001: child-only movie connectivity and motion sensitivity

## Scientific target

Use the real, fixed 155-person development-fMRI release as an honestly labelled
methods case motivated by Fair et al. (2009), Figure 5. Reconstruct Power-264
sphere time series, signed short/long-distance Fisher-z connectivity, child-only
age and FD-rank-adjusted associations, participant-bootstrap uncertainty, and
full/restricted child/adult segregation descriptives. The movie data, ROI set,
distance tertiles and estimators differ from the named paper. This is not a
resting-state finding replication or proof of causal motion removal.

## Repair

The earlier proposal's required motion-collapse narrative, historical effect
table, keyword grading and measured-hardness claims do not apply to this revision.
Those claims are retired rather than reinterpreted as evidence for the new head.
FD<0.2 is explicitly a restriction, not matching. There is no forced sign,
attenuation, significance, effect floor or cross-person variation requirement.

All original image, full-confound, phenotype, coordinate and provenance identities
are pinned in a closed 316-file manifest. Build-time acquisition is bounded and
authenticated; runtime is offline. No source arrays or answer banks are committed.
The obsolete historical reference bank has been removed from scoring.

The public method/schema specify extraction, filtering, nuisance order, activity,
support counts, rank degrees of freedom, all 1,000 bootstrap slots, Welch edge
cases and typed nulls. The verifier independently reconstructs source primitives,
admits source-close cleaned signals, then recomputes their own downstream results
with the public SHA-bound reporting kernel. Generic parsers and numerical
libraries are shared explicitly; this is not a fully independent replication.
