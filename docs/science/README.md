# Scientific design

The two tracks address complementary questions: reproducing and improving defined
paper results, and interpreting neural or behavioral observations under explicit
access conditions.

## Current design guidance

- [Task scope and qualification](task-scope.md) defines what one task promises,
  how full-paper and endpoint scopes differ, and what qualification requires.
  Its dated adoption-time counts are historical context.
- [Study-design templates](study-design/README.md) describe how to bind a complete
  paper or neural comparison before observing outcomes. The templates are unbound
  planning artifacts, not executable studies.
- [Paired-study reporting](paired-study-reporting.md) documents descriptive
  reporting of trusted operator records without running or adjudicating science.

## Scope assessments and evidence

- [Authentic delivery and prospective freezing](benchmark-construction-wave-20261116.md) describes the four existing endpoints improved after the difficulty pilot, including source discrepancies and runtime limits.
- [SMC original-author calibration](smc-author-calibration-20261117.md) reports author-reference scores, including recovered output from a failed supervised stage. It does not establish an AI outcome or accepted reproduction.
- [MegNIST input preparation](megnist-input-preparation-20261117.md) records authenticated preparation of the complete released inputs for one participant across four sessions. No model or scientific score was run.

The [paper scope assessment](paper-scope/SCIENTIFIC_SCOPE.md) describes the
100 original paper candidates, the examined evidence and remaining gaps. It is a
dated assessment, not a new reproduction or ranking of novelty.

Use the task's `task.json` and implementation documentation for its exact population,
inputs, endpoint and limitations. Conditions, subjects, folds and alternative
implementations do not automatically define additional independent benchmarks.
[Current status](../status/README.md) separates registration and source ownership
from complete scientific qualification.
