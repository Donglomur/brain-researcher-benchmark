The new [Foraging endpoint checker](../../../benchmarks/paper/PAPER-FIND-036/implementations/foraging-programme/PAPER_ENDPOINT.md)
computes pooled session balanced accuracy and the equal-session mean through the
existing protected result/collection/preparation chain. It separates the published
0.7271 from an operator BNB equation reconstruction; actual author replay remains
null. The bounded archive access attempt was denied (403/401), so no authentic
roster, rat joins, labels, fits or new empirical results were obtained.

Foraging has a compact processed source archive, `EventClassificationData_4C.zip`
(15.09 MB in source metadata), in the [Dryad release](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.v9s4mw78c),
plus the [pinned author classifier](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/knowblesse/Lobster/2b74d2c2f8d6574b6a1b75c0feb99af5e18bb678/PythonCode/EventClassification/HierarchicalEventClassifier.py)
and [primary paper](https://elifesciences.org/articles/93994). The complete original
±1-second window is retrospective event decoding, not an early-warning task.

DeepCaImX has [pinned author source](https://github.com/KangningZhang/DeepCaImX/tree/305a4827929b2c6e82e44c5c69b2ea9d410e20a4),
a [software archive](https://zenodo.org/records/12650420) and the
[primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12327232/). The existing
post-assembly evaluator is a useful component; its software tests are not native
author reproduction. Synthetic-video and mini-model scores are different conditions.

aggregation or checkpoint/pretraining exposure.
