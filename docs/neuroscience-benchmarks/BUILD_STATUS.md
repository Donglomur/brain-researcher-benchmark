# Neuroscience benchmark build status

Updated November 10, 2026. This is a construction report, not a scientific leaderboard.

The target is 200 canonical assignments: 100 neuroscience paper reproduction/improvement projects and 100 neural-data tasks. Paper projects compare paper plus data against the same resources plus authenticated author code. Neural-data tasks separate signal understanding, tool use, analysis and scientific reasoning. Conditions, folds, cases and reused datasets are not additional benchmarks.

Reporting must retain failed, unavailable and unresolved outcomes, distinguish unknown counts from zero, and require every applicable metric for numeric completion. Candidate and reference publication provenance must survive even when output bytes match. Reviewers cannot independently accept source or designs they coauthored.

Engineering checks use invented inputs and inert scientific/native operations. They are not measurements of neural-data performance or evidence of SOTA. No new empirical study is claimed here. Authentic-data provenance, numerical/reference agreement, isolation, feasible measured resources and protected evaluations remain necessary before qualification.
