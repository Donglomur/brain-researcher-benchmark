# Neuroscience benchmark build status

Updated November 10, 2026. This is a construction report, not a scientific leaderboard.

The target is 200 canonical assignments: 100 neuroscience paper reproduction/improvement projects and 100 neural-data tasks. Paper projects compare paper plus data against the same resources plus authenticated author code. Neural-data tasks separate signal understanding, tool use, analysis and scientific reasoning. Conditions, folds, cases and reused datasets are not additional benchmarks.

The 35 source acceptances comprise 29 plus six with explicitly retained original-method gaps; source acceptance does not establish numerical agreement, data readiness or deployment qualification. The 274 registered conditions are not 274 benchmarks. Historical partial validation counts are not a substitute for this reconciled census.

Recent work has adopted reviewed designs for a shared source capture before paired development, consistent Context schema versions, and complete reporting populations. Reporting must retain failed, unavailable and unresolved outcomes, distinguish unknown counts from zero, and require every applicable metric for numeric completion. Separate candidate and reference publication provenance must survive even when their output bytes match.

Construction and independent review continue. Engineering checks use invented inputs and inert scientific/native operations; they are not measurements of neural-data performance or evidence of SOTA. No new empirical study is claimed here. Authentic-data provenance, numerical/reference agreement, isolation, feasible measured resources and protected evaluations remain necessary before qualification.
