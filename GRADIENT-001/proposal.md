# GRADIENT-001 — fixed-cohort gradient method sensitivity

This task applies four declared connectivity-gradient configurations to the
same authenticated released movie data: no band-pass/all20, band-pass/all20,
no band-pass/first10, and no band-pass/second10. It reports network-coordinate
means, apex/bottom labels, a descriptive leading-plane between/within ratio,
and signed cross-person consistency before and after full10-D alignment.

The participant is the unit of the source cohort. All20 literal participants,
all168 frames, all400 atlas parcel identities, all7 networks and all4 arms stay
accounted for. The ordered halves are age-confounded convenience subsets, not
randomized samples, independent replications, or an isolated sample-size test.
Mean aligned coordinates and an embedding of a mean connectivity matrix are
different reported quantities. Aligned coordinate1 need not be an individual's
first diffusion eigenvector.

The exact public method and eight-artifact schema are carried forward from
reviewed PR190 commit4cf8f988b4f5109055455d5781fbfb1bb1157f82. They disclose the
operational atlas/BOLD template transfer, float64 extraction, raw-scale numerical
activity rule, exact40 signed row-entry retention, symmetric-conjugate diffusion
operator, largest-algebraic nontrivial modes, sign convention, full10-D GPA and
complete/null semantics. These are named computational amendments to the old
BrainSpace-based recipe, not a claim of bitwise historical-software equivalence.

The private verifier authenticates original bytes and reconstructs source
support, preprocessing, FC and canonical spectral operators. Submitted spectra
must satisfy the public residual, normalization and leading-order certificates;
submitted GPA rotations must satisfy the public objective and history. Accepted
certified eigenvectors and rotations then determine all downstream coordinates
and summaries. No historical answer bank, canonical apex, minimum B/W, required
alignment gain, compulsory fragility, prose verdict or duplicate-person ban is
an acceptance criterion. Undefined estimands are valid completed results when
their support, masks, counts and nulls are correct.

The question is descriptive sensitivity on this fixed movie cohort. It is not
a reproduction or refutation of an adult HCP result, a population or causal
inference, a test of whether a network is uniquely neural, or a benchmark-model
difficulty claim. Stable, changing and incomplete-support outcomes are all
permitted. Prior PR190 executions validate their own frozen closure only;
PR200 packaging, I/O hardening, source reauthentication and delivery require new
explicit gates and receipts, recorded separately in the repair ledger. Local
engineering checks do not establish scientific validity or measured difficulty.
