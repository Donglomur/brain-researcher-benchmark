## MSMTCSD-002

**Proposal Title:** Multi-shell multi-tissue spherical deconvolution of a heterogeneous dMRI cohort — an execution-hard reconstruction task (recipe divergence + coupled-physics assembly + declared robustness with hidden realization)

**Scientific Domain:** Life Sciences · **Field:** Neuroscience · **Subfield:** Diffusion MRI / microstructure (multi-tissue spherical deconvolution)

**Source paper:** Jeurissen et al. 2014, *NeuroImage* (multi-tissue constrained spherical deconvolution for multi-shell dMRI, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2014.07.061); Tournier et al. 2007, *NeuroImage* (constrained spherical deconvolution, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2007.02.016). Dataset: a **paper-parameterized** multi-shell multi-tissue dMRI cohort (8 subjects, Jeurissen 2014 MSMT-CSD signal model), generated deterministically at `synth_build/generate_fixtures.py`; the true physiology is held out for grading under `tests/planted_truth.npz` (the pinned-response decomposition run on the noise-/artifact-free signal, built by `synth_build/build_truth.py`).

**Status: FULL runnable Harbor task.** This is a **reconstruction** task, *not* a recognition / "spot-the-confound" task — `instruction.md` names the deliverable (per-voxel WM/GM/CSF tissue signal fractions + WM-FOD peaks from the pinned responses); the difficulty is *execution*, not an un-cued judgement. The agent implements MSMT-CSD **from scratch** (no `dwi2fod` / dipy bundled), over a **heterogeneous cohort where subjects need fundamentally different computations**, with a **publicly-declared robustness** requirement (the corrupted-volume realization hidden), graded voxelwise against the **planted physiology** on **convention-invariant** fractions and peak directions.

### What changed in this revision (addressing the review of the hidden-reference contract)

1. **Robustness contract made public.** `instruction.md` now states, under *Robustness / data-quality contract*, that a majority of subjects carry one to three grossly motion-corrupted DW volumes that must be detected and rejected robustly — only the *realization* (which subjects/volumes) is hidden. A correct fit of the stated model is no longer penalized for failing to guess an undeclared step.
2. **Graded against the true physiology, not one fitter.** The verifier no longer recomputes with a private pipeline on the real data and demands agreement. It compares each submitted quantity, voxelwise, to the **held-out planted target** = the pinned-response multi-tissue decomposition run on the **noise-/artifact-free** signal (`tests/planted_truth.npz`, built by `synth_build/build_truth.py`). That target is the convention-invariant physical truth; **any valid estimator** applied to the real data recovers it within tolerance. The hidden reference modules were removed from `tests/`.
3. **Identifiability made honest.** For **two-distinct-b subjects the WM/CSF split is only weakly identified**; the target is the *apparent* (clean-signal) decomposition the acquisition actually supports (which the real-data oracle recovers to |Δ|≤0.07), **not** the planted volume fractions (which differ by 0.2–0.5 and are unrecoverable from a single shell). The fraction tolerance atol was widened 0.03→0.06 to cover the unavoidable Rician direction-averaged noise floor (measured; still far below the 0.2–0.5 errors of a wrong tissue count or a non-robust fit).

### Why this is hard (the four difficulty drivers)

1. **Recipe divergence** — subjects need *different code paths*, discoverable only from each sidecar: subjects with **three or more distinct b-values** (b0 + two/three DW shells) can separate three tissues (WM FOD + GM + CSF signal fractions), while subjects with **only two distinct b-values** (b0 + a single DW shell) cannot separate grey matter — the fit is 2-tissue (WM + CSF) and the GM fraction is **not computable** and must be **omitted**. The usable spherical-harmonic `lmax` adapts to each subject's outer-shell direction count. A single fixed recipe cannot fit the cohort.
2. **Coupled-physics assembly** — a real orthonormal even-order SH basis, the per-shell zonal convolution of the FOD with the **pinned** WM response, the isotropic GM/CSF response columns, the Laplace–Beltrami-regularised deconvolution, the spherical-mean (powder) tissue unmixing, and non-negativity must **all** be assembled correctly; an error in any one compounds across the 40 (subject × panel) panels.
3. **Declared robustness, hidden realization** — a **majority (5 of 8)** subjects carry one to three grossly motion-corrupted DW volumes that must be detected and rejected (gross outliers of the per-shell angular signal) before the spherical mean and the FOD are trustworthy; `instruction.md` now *declares* this requirement but hides *which* subjects/volumes are affected. The tissue amounts must also be constrained **non-negative** (a plain least-squares unmixing goes negative in near-pure-CSF voxels) — implied by the "signal fractions (a_t ≥ 0)" definition in the protocol.
4. **Convention-invariant grading** — the tissue fractions are the spherical-mean decomposition into the pinned responses (no SH normalisation, no reporting convention) and peak **directions** are invariant to the SH basis and FOD scale, so any correct implementation recovers the same planted physical quantities (verified below) — no reporting-convention ambiguity.

### Verifier

`tests/test_outputs.py` grades **voxelwise** over the brain mask against `tests/planted_truth.npz`, one parametrized test per (subject × panel) panel — there are 40 sub-tests (8 subjects × {wm_frac, gm_frac, csf_frac, peaks, iso}); the binary Harbor reward (pytest rc) requires all to pass. The planted target is the pinned-response decomposition run on the **noise-/artifact-free** signal (built by `synth_build/build_truth.py`), so the grader accepts **any scientifically valid estimator** and never requires the output of one particular fitter. A computable fraction passes at ≥90% voxel agreement within (rtol 0.10, atol 0.06); gm_frac for a two-distinct-b subject passes only when **omitted** (the GM key is absent from the planted truth); the peaks panel passes when ≥90% of the planted fibre voxels have the right WM-FOD peak count and directions within 15°; the iso panel passes when ≥90% of the planted isotropic voxels are assigned **no** peak. The hidden reference pipeline was removed from `tests/`.

**Validity / discrimination evidence (recomputed for this revision).** The oracle recovers the planted target on **all 40 panels** — the tissue-fraction panels at 0.96–1.00 voxel agreement and every peaks/iso panel at 1.00 — comfortably inside tolerance. A **naive fit that skips the corrupted-volume rejection fails 17 panels**, exactly on the 5 corrupted subjects (the 3 uncorrupted subjects still pass every panel), spread across the fraction (7), peaks (5) and iso (5) axes. Two-distinct-b subjects are targeted at the identifiable *apparent* WM/CSF decomposition (the clean-signal fit), which the real-data oracle recovers to |Δ|≤0.07; the planted volume fractions there differ by 0.2–0.5 and are (correctly) **not** used as the target. So a from-scratch correct solver passes and single-axis shortcuts fail, on axes the instruction *declares*.

### Notes / honesty

- **Reconstruction paradigm.** This task's difficulty is executional (assemble the SH deconvolution, robust volume rejection, non-negative unmixing, and omit forks with no bundled library), unlike the recognition-tier "un-cued judgement" tasks in this repo. `instruction.md` names the deliverable, the pinned responses/conventions, and (now) the declared robustness requirement — but the corrupted-volume *realization* (which subjects/volumes) stays hidden and must be discovered from the data.
- **Data.** Paper-parameterized, small, deterministic, and **leakage-clean** (the planted truth lives only in `tests/planted_truth.npz`, never under `/app/data`). Regenerable via `synth_build/generate_fixtures.py` + `synth_build/build_truth.py`.
- **allow_internet=false.** The task is self-contained (dependencies baked in the image; no network at run or verify time).
