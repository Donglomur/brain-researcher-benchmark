## DIFFTIME-002

**Proposal Title:** Time-dependent diffusion — restriction and structure from D(t) — an execution-hard reconstruction task (recipe divergence + coupled-physics assembly + hidden robustness)

**Scientific Domain:** Life Sciences · **Field:** Neuroscience · **Subfield:** Diffusion MRI / microstructure

**Source paper:** Mitra, Sen, Schwartz & Le Doussal 1992, *Phys. Rev. Lett.* (short-time diffusion / surface-to-volume ratio, https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.68.3555); Novikov, Jensen, Helpern & Fieremans 2014, *PNAS* (long-time structural universality classes, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1316944111). Dataset: a **paper-parameterized** time-dependent-diffusion cohort, generated deterministically at `synth_build/generate_fixtures.py`; the grading target is held out under `tests/planted_truth.npz` (the pinned public estimator run on the actual `D(t)` with the corrupted volumes removed, built by `synth_build/build_truth.py`; never under `environment/data`).

**Status: FULL runnable Harbor task.** This is a **reconstruction** task, *not* a recognition / "spot-the-confound" task — the instruction names the deliverable (produce Dref/D0/SV/Dinf maps); the difficulty is *execution*, not an un-cued judgement. It follows the shape of the maintainer's `pcasl-cbf-quantifier`: the agent implements the time-dependent-diffusion inversion **from scratch** (no fitter bundled), over a **heterogeneous 8-subject cohort where subjects need structurally different estimators**, with a **publicly-declared robustness** requirement (the corrupted-volume *realization* hidden), graded voxelwise against the **held-out pinned-method target** on **convention-invariant** physical quantities.

### Why this is hard (the four difficulty drivers)

1. **Recipe divergence** — subjects need *structurally different estimators*, discoverable only from each sidecar's declared diffusion-time regime. **Short-time** subjects sample the √t rise, so D(t) is fit against [1, √t]: the intercept is the free diffusivity D0 and the √t slope fixes the Mitra surface-to-volume ratio S/V — but the long-time plateau is not sampled and Dinf must be **omitted**. **Long-time** subjects sample the power-law approach to the tortuosity plateau, so D(t) is fit against [1, t^(−θ)] with the subject's **declared** structural-class exponent θ (1/2 for random-barrier, 1 for strong-disorder subjects); the intercept is Dinf — but D0 and S/V must be **omitted**. **Single-time** subjects sample one diffusion time, so only the measured D is reportable.
2. **Coupled-physics assembly** — the regime basis, the intercept/slope extraction, the Mitra S/V formula (coupling slope, intercept, and structural dimension d), and the reference-time arithmetic must **all** be assembled correctly; an error in D0 propagates into S/V, and choosing the wrong basis biases the extrapolated intercepts.
3. **Declared robustness, hidden realization** — a majority of subjects carry one or two grossly corrupted diffusion-time volumes (motion / low-SNR long-time artefacts) that must be **detected and rejected** before the linear fit, or the extrapolated intercepts and slopes are biased on exactly those subjects. The *requirement* is now stated publicly (data-quality contract in `instruction.md`); the *realization* — which subjects, which volumes — is not disclosed.
4. **Convention-invariant grading** — every graded quantity is a physical diffusivity or S/V obtained by ordinary least squares in a protocol-stated basis after rejecting unambiguous gross-outlier volumes, so two independent correct implementations compute them identically (proven below); a from-scratch solver passes while wrong pipelines fail.

### What changed in this revision (addressing the review)

1. **Robustness contract made public.** `instruction.md` now states, under *Robustness / data-quality contract*, that a minority of diffusion-time volumes are grossly corrupted and must be detected and rejected robustly — only the *realization* (which subjects/volumes) is hidden. A correct fit of the stated estimator is no longer penalized for failing to guess an undeclared step. The per-regime omit forks were already public.
2. **Graded against a held-out pinned-method target, not a private pipeline on the real data.** The verifier no longer imports or runs a reference pipeline. It compares each submitted map, voxelwise, to `tests/planted_truth.npz` = the **fully-specified public estimator** (per-regime OLS basis; D0/Dinf intercepts; the Mitra S/V from the √t slope and dimension d; Dref at t_ref) run on the **actual `D(t)` with the ground-truth-corrupted volumes removed**, built by `synth_build/build_truth.py`. The dead reference modules (`tests/dt_pipeline.py`, `tests/dt_ref.py`) were `git rm`-ed; the oracle's `solution/` copies remain.
3. **Why a pinned-method target and not a clean-signal target (the S/V subtlety).** The short-time S/V comes from the √t **slope**, a small number estimated from a handful of noisy `D(t)` points, so its per-voxel sampling error is many times the tolerance: a noise-free/clean-signal S/V is **not** what any estimator recovers from the real data (measured: a clean-signal S/V agrees with the real-data fit on only ~30–42 % of voxels). What *is* well-posed and convention-invariant is the value the pinned OLS produces on the actual data — graded here. D0/Dinf/Dref were already well-posed; the change is uniform across maps for consistency.

### Verifier

`tests/test_outputs.py` grades **voxelwise** over the brain mask against `tests/planted_truth.npz` (never under `/app/data`), one parametrized test per (subject × map) panel — 32 panels total. Reward is **fractional** (fraction of panels correct). A computable map passes when ≥90 % of brain voxels agree within a per-map tolerance; an unsupported map (Dinf for a short/single subject, D0/SV for a long/single subject) passes only when the submission **omits** it. Tolerances are the **independent-implementation agreement margin**, not the noise floor: a faithful float32 fit matches the target to <1e-6 relative / ~2e-7 absolute, so `TOL` is set at rtol 1e-3 / atol 1e-4 µm²/ms for Dref/D0/Dinf and rtol 1e-3 / atol 1e-5 1/µm for SV (~1000× the faithful-impl margin, ~60× tighter than the old noise-floor tolerance), tight enough that every single-axis error is off by O(0.01–1) and fails.

**Grading-invariance proof (the decisive check, re-measured this revision).** A fully independent from-scratch implementation (explicit normal-equations solve `solve(XᵀX, XᵀY)` instead of `lstsq`, its own rearranged Mitra and reference-time arithmetic; **no** import of the reference) run on the real data with the corrupted volumes removed reproduces the target to **<1e-11 relative in float64 and <1e-6 through the float32 output path** on every computable panel — and passes **all 32 panels** through the actual verifier. The oracle's robust rejection was verified to drop **exactly** the ground-truth-corrupted volumes on all 8 subjects, so oracle = target. The plausible-but-wrong pipelines each fail only their own axis: **wrong regime basis** (13 panels fail), **wrong structural exponent θ** (6 long-subject panels), **wrong Mitra dimension d** (3 S/V panels), **no outlier rejection** (12 panels, exactly the corrupted subjects), **always-emit-all-maps** (the omit panels). Partial credit is monotone in correctness.

### Difficulty — frontier gate

Oracle **reward 1.0** verified in-container (32/32 panels: 17 computable at 100 % voxel agreement, 15 correctly omitted). A **naive fit that skips the gross-volume rejection fails 12 panels**, exactly on the five corrupted subjects; the three uncorrupted subjects still pass.
