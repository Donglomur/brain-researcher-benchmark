# Neuroscience task catalog

This is a public source inventory, not a list of qualified benchmarks. Scientific and submission readiness are **unknown here**. Runtime manifests and source components do not establish successful execution. Registered IDs may alias earlier contract identifiers; they do not count independent studies or datasets.

The project targets four first-class outcomes: paper reproduction, paper improvement, native neural interpretation, and neural tool/model building. Outcome conditions share scientific task IDs while retaining separate evidence and qualification. [Read the four-outcome design](../../docs/design/four-outcomes.md).

Recorded public evidence covers **9 distinct task IDs**. The pilot reports **4 historical paper-calibration attempts** and **4 neural-programme attempts**; 3 authentic-input engineering reports, 3 paper and 1 neural source-preparation reports, and 16 native software controls are separate from model attempts. Separately, **2 author-model inference probes** record partial source-model execution; complete author calibration remains unqualified. [Read the published evidence](../../docs/status/README.md), or run `python3 scripts/neurobench.py evidence [TASK_ID]`. These records do not establish current runtime qualification or paper improvement.

Outcome metadata covers **117/117 paper tasks**. This counts contract declarations and unavailability records; completion of both outcomes requires separate qualifying execution evidence. Reproduction eligibility: 1 continuous measurement, 1 design ready, 2 source declaration unavailable, 64 source unresolved, 49 unavailable. Each task retains its declared information conditions; SMC compares paper+data with paper+data+official-code-and-weights. These are nested conditions of the same registered tasks; both scientific outcomes remain **unknown**.

Complete primary-outcome pairing is unavailable for **41 tasks** with an explicit scientific scope mismatch. Their retained secondary improvement policies are not admitted as primary outcomes.

Complete primary-outcome pairing is unavailable for **8 tasks**. Their retained improvement contracts and declared coverage remain visible; these records do not establish completed primary outcomes.

Source-grounded reproduction declarations and matched paired protocols are not adopted for **2 tasks**. Their existing improvement contracts remain independently inspectable under their original criteria. Missing declarations do not imply scientific ineligibility, paired access, or completed outcomes.

**1 task** declares continuous measurements for both paper outcomes, without binary eligibility or pass thresholds. Matched episode protocols remain uninstantiated and participant results are not attempted; an AI attempt is not required for benchmark construction.

Only the assisted SMC condition receives the exact official source and pinned released-model assets. The earlier source-only condition remains a separate historical ablation.

Current primary improvement dispositions: 1 continuous measurement, 16 design ready, 7 ineligible, 44 source unresolved, 49 unavailable. The LANGUAGE reference successor records author-provided-array/current-runtime reference recovery; its current improvement policy and remaining source limits are explicit, with no AI outcome or qualification. The Deeprecon reference successor records recovered publisher pair means; native replay, matched candidate comparison and scientific qualification remain unresolved. For the reviewed scope-policy selections, the current primary policy governs improvement; predecessor policies remain preserved with their original source scope. Runtime routes are not declared by those policy records. For SMC, the shared continuous policy governs both current outcomes; the original and temporal policies remain retained source history.

Retained registration-policy audit: **117/117 covered**; **0 improvement-ready**, **21 source-grounded designs**, **87 source-unresolved**, **9 ineligible**, **0 not audited**. Eligibility means an ordered source task was identified; it does not establish an executable or scientifically qualified comparison. Paper reproduction is a separate first-class outcome; these counts describe the existing improvement contracts, including retained contracts with their declared coverage, not primary-outcome availability or reproduction qualification. Numerical fidelity remains a necessary comparison check. A new split is an extension, never a same-task paper victory.

Neural understanding revision: **118/118 covered**; 13 delivered named scope, 7 design only, 94 partial delivery, 4 target mismatch. These describe delivered source scope. Direct observation, typed-viewer interpretation, tool-assisted investigation and frozen prediction programmes have separate access conditions and evidence. Native interpretation and tool/model building are separate first-class outcomes. Programme performance does not establish direct interpretation.

The current registry assigns 669 implementation artifacts, including 5 with unresolved ownership. Participants, conditions, folds, seeds and controls are nested task units, not additional benchmarks.
