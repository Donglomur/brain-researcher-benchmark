# Neuroscience task catalog

This is a public source inventory, not a list of qualified benchmarks. Scientific and submission readiness are **unknown here**. Runtime manifests and source components do not establish successful execution. Registered IDs may alias earlier contract identifiers; they do not count independent studies or datasets.

The project targets four first-class outcomes: paper reproduction, paper improvement, native neural interpretation, and neural tool/model building. Outcome conditions share scientific task IDs while retaining separate evidence and qualification. [Read the four-outcome design](../../docs/design/four-outcomes.md).

Recorded public evidence covers **9 distinct task IDs**. The pilot reports **4 historical paper-calibration attempts** and **4 neural-programme attempts**; 3 authentic-input engineering reports, 3 paper and 1 neural source-preparation reports, and 16 native software controls are separate from model attempts. Separately, **2 author-model inference probes** record historical partial source-model execution; those probes did not produce complete predictions or scores. The separate SMC report records **2 complete author-reference calibration arms**; the accepted recovered noncausal aggregate retains its failed supervisor. The separate SMC material report records a **71-file participant-material pair**, with no participant execution. [Read the published evidence](../../docs/status/README.md), or run `python3 scripts/neurobench.py evidence [TASK_ID]`. These records do not establish current runtime qualification or paper improvement.

Outcome metadata covers **117/117 paper tasks**. This counts contract declarations and unavailability records; completion of both outcomes requires separate qualifying execution evidence. Reproduction eligibility: 1 continuous measurement, 1 design ready, 90 source unresolved, 25 unavailable. Each task retains its declared information conditions; SMC compares paper+data with paper+data+official-code-and-weights. These are nested conditions of the same registered tasks; both scientific outcomes remain **unknown**.

Complete primary-outcome pairing is unavailable for **22 tasks** with an explicit scientific scope mismatch. Their retained secondary improvement policies are not admitted as primary outcomes.

Complete primary-outcome pairing is unavailable for **3 tasks**. Their retained improvement contracts and declared coverage remain visible; these records do not establish completed primary outcomes.

**1 task** declares continuous measurements for both paper outcomes, without binary eligibility or pass thresholds. Matched episode protocols remain uninstantiated and participant results are not attempted; an AI attempt is not required for benchmark construction.

Only the assisted SMC condition receives the exact official source and pinned released-model assets. The earlier source-only condition remains a separate historical ablation.

Current primary improvement dispositions: 1 continuous measurement, 18 design ready, 7 ineligible, 66 source unresolved, 25 unavailable. The LANGUAGE reference successor records author-provided-array/current-runtime reference recovery; its selected policy identifies either that reference stage or the separately authenticated original Figure3b continuous improvement scope, with full reproduction retained and no AI outcome or qualification. The Deeprecon reference successor records recovered publisher pair means; native replay, matched candidate comparison and scientific qualification remain unresolved. **26 tasks** select exact original author-reported computational endpoints under a new scope version. Broader biological reproduction and prior contracts remain separately preserved; data, comparator, access and runtime gaps remain explicit, with no scientific results or qualification. For the reviewed scope-policy selections, the current primary policy governs improvement; predecessor policies remain preserved with their original source scope. Runtime routes are not declared by those policy records. For SMC, the shared continuous policy governs both current outcomes; the original and temporal policies remain retained source history.

Retained registration-policy audit: **117/117 covered**; **0 improvement-ready**, **21 source-grounded designs**, **87 source-unresolved**, **9 ineligible**, **0 not audited**. Eligibility means an ordered source task was identified; it does not establish an executable or scientifically qualified comparison. Paper reproduction is a separate first-class outcome; these counts describe the existing improvement contracts, including retained contracts with their declared coverage, not primary-outcome availability or reproduction qualification. Numerical fidelity remains a necessary comparison check. A new split is an extension, never a same-task paper victory.

Neural understanding revision: **118/118 covered**; 13 delivered named scope, 7 design only, 95 partial delivery, 3 target mismatch. These describe delivered source scope. Direct observation, typed-viewer interpretation, tool-assisted investigation and frozen prediction programmes have separate access conditions and evidence. Native interpretation and tool/model building are separate first-class outcomes. Programme performance does not establish direct interpretation.

Participants, conditions, folds, seeds and controls are nested task units, not additional benchmarks.
