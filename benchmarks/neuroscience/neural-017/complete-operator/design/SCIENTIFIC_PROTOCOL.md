This scientific/API specification accompanies the complete source for the existing motor-population co-smoothing assignment. ## Question and estimand

Given supervised training recordings and the complete observed-neuron activity of new reach trials, can an LLM-directed investigation predict withheld-neuron counts better than the same LLM's frozen data-analysis program, under the same total information, tools and resources? The scientific contrast is paired predictive performance, not whether the agent repeats a preferred neural story. A correct conclusion may be no improvement, insufficient evidence, or performance explained by task condition and time.

The selected complete packet is one Jenkins macaque session, 2009-09-25: 1,721 official TRAIN trials and 574 official VALIDATION trials, 137 observed and 45 withheld units, 140 five-millisecond bins per trial. Bin left edges are −250,…,445 ms relative to the official rounded movement onset; the last bin ends at +450 ms. Every one of the 3,616,200 evaluation cells remains required. The public mask is retained, not inferred from successful predictions. This packet requires an all-true mask; a newly missing observation is a protocol/input failure, not permission to drop a unit, trial or bin. The lower-level scorer supports masks, but that does not silently expand this packet's domain.

This is retrospective co-smoothing: all 700 ms of the observed query trial are available, including later bins when predicting earlier bins. Symmetric filtering and whole-trial features are legitimate. It is not causal forecasting, online control, unseen-neuron transfer (the withheld units have training labels), session transfer, animal-population generalization, or identification of unique biological latents. The 574 trials are cases within ONE benchmark, not 574 benchmarks.

## Two genuine participant modes

F (frozen program): one arbitrary submitted Python program implements its own training/search/adaptation/prediction method. The controller invokes that source in an isolated process with the supplied data; it does not select among a finite menu of references. For the legacy 20-second condition the program is frozen before the complete public packet is mounted, exactly as before. The paired study likewise freezes F's method source and automated training/search/transduction policy before any empirical packet exposure. The authoring LLM may reason from schemas, primary papers, curated code in the code-access condition and explicit training-independent engineering feedback; it may not see empirical summaries through an unmetered side channel. The program itself receives training labels, may perform arbitrary training-only supervised search, and may perform declared unsupervised adaptation using observed evaluation activity.

I (case investigation): freeze the LLM identity, tool adapter, system/task prompts, training/search policy, interaction rules and budget before packet release. A shared preparation actor may perform training/search on the same training data, producing frozen tools/states. After preparation, create 574 fresh case contexts; each can inspect its complete trial, the same entire unlabeled query pool, training data and the same qualified tools, write and execute arbitrary Python, and produce a complete 140×45 prediction. Case analyses are not forced to choose a baseline or mimic its output. Case-specific code and nuisance/observed-query decisions are allowed within the frozen interaction policy and recorded. No private labels, scores, target-derived traces or other case's response/logs are available. This separation makes the experimentally varied factor continuing LLM analysis during query inference versus a previously written program.

Both modes see the entire unlabeled query pool, not an asymmetric subset. A shared adaptation policy may compute label-free transforms over that pool. It is committed before case dispatch, and each case starts from the same immutable adapted state. Alternatively a case may compute a local transform from the allowed whole pool, within its own budget, without mutating other cases. Exact input dependencies and any query-dependent state changes are recorded. Query-driven supervised model selection is prohibited; query-driven unsupervised adaptation is expressly permitted. A separate optional training-only ablation may prohibit transduction in BOTH modes, but cannot replace or relabel the main allowed-transduction condition.

F's program authoring tokens/feedback, I's preparation and 574 case interactions, all model calls and all scientific/tool compute are charged to each mode's same TOTAL study envelope. A per-case allowance in I is partitioned from that total, never multiplied on top of F's budget. F can spend its full token allowance on source authoring before exposure, and its full tool envelope on the resulting fixed algorithm. This is a matched upper-resource-envelope comparison, not a claim identical realized effort. Actual consumption, unused allowance, truncations and unavailable methods must be reported.

## Information factorial and controls

Core information conditions A and B use identical primary context, public data, host libraries, tool APIs and qualified reference-tool outputs. A has papers and task/data specification; B additionally has an audited set of authentic organizer method sources at commit42f8410b88e12db136910fa2f888b025ea0aa2ae and declared local baseline sources. B is therefore paper+data+curated code, not arbitrary private-repository access. A still gets explicit baseline-tool method descriptions; a secondary no-reference-tool condition is separately labeled. Before either exposure, curate B by excluding checkpoints, cached predictions, evaluation assets, execution receipts and target-derived plots/ledgers. Preserve exact upstream originals and bind any participant derivative separately. Local source comments/docstrings that reveal historical assessment outcomes must be withheld from BOTH primary arms (or disclosed equally in a separately labeled exposed-history condition); an audited comment-only derivative needs exact diff and AST-equivalence proof before release. Full primary papers retain their published results equally in A/B. Do not call a derivative byte-identical author code. Network/offline isolation does not prove absent model pretraining exposure. Code-source differences may improve tooling, not necessarily neural reasoning.

A single model/configuration paired study therefore has F-A, I-A, F-B and I-B. Five predeclared independent agent seeds are a prospective design default; each seed gets new independent development and case contexts, not a shared conversation. This S2 programme requires all five seeds. Any different seed count requires a separately reviewed prospective design and cannot be substituted into this frozen campaign or justified by its outcomes. Every declared seed/arm covers all574 cases, including failures. No factor, seed or case is chosen after scores. Arms are run with frozen scheduling/order and cannot inspect another arm's outputs or historical private artifacts.

Diagnostic conditions apply to both F and I on the complete same target roster: (1) intact observed activity; (2) a protected precommitted within-condition permutation of observed query trials, preserving the recipient's condition and time; (3) context-only, physically omitting all observed-neuron arrays and all states fitted from those arrays, while retaining training withheld-unit labels to fit competent nuisance models; (4) training-rate-only, providing only per-withheld-unit training count totals/opportunities and output axes, omitting training trial/time detail, condition IDs and query neural inputs; (5) tool-only fixed orchestration with no continuing LLM reasoning; (6) metadata-neutral prompt, preserving full units/axes/condition definitions while omitting neuroscience narrative. The first four define information ablations; tool-only and prompt changes define agent ablations. Metadata-neutral changes only the instruction framing; the same papers and code remain accessible, so it does not conceal neuroscience identity or measure domain-free reasoning. This is the required diagnostic roster for the full capability-disentanglement programme, not extra canonical tasks. Run the fixed diagnostic factorial separately from the four primary cells, with its own equal per-arm TOTAL budgets and multiplicity family; CONDITION_ROSTER.json fixes its complete logical support. Do not silently pool incomparable resource or information conditions.

For shuffled inputs, the observation-to-recipient permutation is committed by the protected operator from condition IDs and a newly generated private assessment seed before any activity or target inspection. INFORMATION_VIEWS.json fixes the single-cycle derangement construction; the seed and donor-to-recipient map are hidden from participants and shared across arms/seeds. Every tool receives the same transformed pool; intact counts, intact neural-adapted states and donor IDs are absent. Singletons retain identity and two-element strata have a uniquely invertible swap, both explicitly counted. Even larger strata preserve the multiset of neural trials and permit statistical alignment inference. This is therefore an alignment stress test, not a claim all neural information has vanished, a conditional-independence test or a causal necessity claim. A public predictable one-place rotation is not the selected control. Context-only references are competitively tuned on training labels; weak fixed context alone is insufficient. A no-tools text-only exploration is optional and not a substitute for the main full-support prediction task: any lossy rendering must be a declared separate input representation with its own complete-support feasibility gate.

## Development, exposure and publication

Whole-trial training splits prevent the same trial's bins entering both fit and selection. The existing seed20261030 condition-stratified 1,381/340 split remains the old ridge/context convention, not a new evaluation split. The official smoothing/GPFA cross-validation schemes use interleaved complete trials; the design adapts those schemes only to the1721 official train trials, then refits all1721. No574 evaluation labels are used for any new supervised fit, hyperparameter choice, early stopping, calibration, ensemble choice, reference choice or case decision. Do not import official scripts' default train+val into the local training stage.

All574 predictions from every primary arm/seed, required references and any preregistered diagnostics must be immutable, with all producer processes and descendants absent, before the first assessment-stage private target read or feedback release. Operator-only raw conversion/target packaging is a distinct earlier preparation role that necessarily handles withheld counts; it occurs without participant actors, cannot fit/select methods or publish target derivatives, and seals the target artifact before study release. No participant or reference producer receives that preparation privilege. The score service receives no participant source and never executes it. The task is arbitrary prediction: every aligned finite strictly-positive output is gradeable, including wrong predictions and negative gains. Reporting cannot require a positive neural finding, reference equality, named family or preferred prose conclusion.

Private per-trial likelihood ledgers and full target-derived diagnostics remain operator-only. Only after the global barrier may a distinct report actor see aggregate scores, uncertainty and allowed diagnostic summaries. That actor cannot revise predictions, sources, hyperparameters or case tools. Original public/outcome exposure is disclosed for all existing local comparator designs; their history cannot be erased by freezing new wrappers. Learned state/predictions from prior work are not public tool ingredients unless their exact permitted training lineage and absence of evaluation-target fitting are separately established.

Prior local method development used historically exposed validation material and may have been informed by outcomes. Private result histories are excluded from this checkout; freezing new source does not erase that exposure or turn it into fresh confirmation. New results need matched live source-bound producers, frozen evaluation and honest reporting. No claim of native neural understanding, novelty, SOTA, independent replication or submission readiness follows from this source.
