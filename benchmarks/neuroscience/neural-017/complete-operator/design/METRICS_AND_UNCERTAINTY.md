# Metric, uncertainty and scientific assessment

Let y_it u be evaluation spike counts, lambda_it u positive predicted expected counts per5ms bin, m the fixed boolean mask, N_u=sum_it m, S_u=sum_it m*y and S=sum_u S_u. The private normalizer is mu_u=S_u/N_u, with mu_u=1e-9 when S_u=0. Every unit requires N_u>0. The unchanged official-compatible pooled score is

B(lambda) = sum_itu m * [y*(log(lambda)−log(mu_u))+mu_u−lambda] / (S*log(2)).

This sums log likelihoods over all trial/bin/unit cells and divides by the total observed spikes. It is not the mean of per-trial or per-neuron bps. Rates inHz must be converted by the producer (multiply by0.005); the scorer never guesses units. All y on valid cells must be finite nonnegative integers≤2^53. All lambda cells, including masked cells, must be finite and strictly positive. The production NPZ parser additionally enforces its fixed typed/axis/size domain.

The independent implementation `metrics.poisson_bits_per_spike` cancels factorials algebraically and subtracts logs, avoiding lambda/mu overflow. Upstream `evaluation.bits_per_spike` uses summed Poisson NLL with gamma factorial and nanmean, clipping zero rates to1e-9. These are equation-equivalent on the valid positive domain; our strict zero/nonfinite rejection and undefined-zero-total status are deliberate extensions, not identical error behavior. Finite predictions that overflow arithmetic are valid-domain but unscorable arithmetic, not wrong-format cheating; the export's explicit classification stays. S=0 returns undefined and no fabricated zero. Underflow/NaN/overflow is not addressed by excluding difficult trials.

For two complete predictions A,B on identical support, D_i=sum_tu m*[y*(log(lambda_A)−log(lambda_B))+lambda_B−lambda_A] and S_i=sum_tu m*y. Delta= sum_i D_i / (log(2)*sum_i S_i).
