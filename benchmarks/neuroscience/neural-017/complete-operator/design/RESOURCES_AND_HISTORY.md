This document specifies source-level resource profiles and exposure limits. Proposed caps are design parameters, not grants, measured feasibility or promises to finish scientific runs. Scientific qualification and paid model execution require separate current authority. A source release does not renew an old allocation or supply private evidence.

## Prior exposure and evidence boundaries

The selected source is one Jenkins session with historically public validation labels. Local comparator development has used that exposed material. Freezing these sources or withholding targets operationally cannot establish a previously unseen assessment, undo outcome-informed method design or make the methods independently confirmed. Other Churchland, sliceTCA and NLB tasks can expose overlapping material; isolate the entire release environment rather than one target pathname.

Private historical scores, cost records, acquisition transports, control outputs, source/review histories and fitted artifacts are excluded from this checkout. Their absence does not mean those earlier costs or failures were zero. A future operator must establish current source, capacity and cost authority from genuine evidence; source inspection is not a substitute. No prior prediction array or fitted state is supplied as a reference-tool output.

All 1,721 training and 574 validation trials, 137 observed and 45 withheld units, and 140 bins remain required. The different 375/125-trial Maze-Large panel is outside this source-selected campaign. No private historical comparator outcome is a threshold or a new result of this release.
