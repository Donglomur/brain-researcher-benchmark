# NEURAL-054: complete THINGS-MEG scientific and participant-interface design

## Question and contribution

Can a general agent produce useful predictions of the original 27 overlapping object annotations from THINGS-MEG under an explicitly weighted probability score, and determine whether the specified early MEG representation improves prediction beyond independently qualified visual and ocular pipelines? The unit is one existing benchmark, NEURAL-054. The four participants are the same source cohort used by NEURAL-053. Conditions, folds, labels, windows, programs, and source files add neither benchmarks nor independent datasets.

This is a prospective neural-data investigation, not reproduction of the paper's 1,854-way pairwise LDA, continuous animacy/size regression, MDS, fMRI, behavioral embedding, or MEG–fMRI fusion. Those source analyses establish provenance and distinctions. Their original outcomes are not the answer key to this new question. The complete paper was retained at publisher version 4; the exact main XML includes Methods, all embedded captions and three appendices. Its MRI acquisition supplement is not a missing MEG methods supplement.

The strongest mechanistic hypothesis is that a measured neural representation provides useful semantic regularity for a computationally bounded decoder that the specified image and eye features do not capture efficiently. The strongest alternative is deficient visual representation/model capacity, ocular availability or timing artifacts, subject/sensor mismatch, or exposure leakage. **The labels are deterministic annotations of the displayed concept/image. A positive finite-model gain cannot establish irreducible information conditional on the complete image and annotation rule.** We therefore use “predictive value relative to declared controls,” not conditional mutual information, causal neural contribution, or removal of every visual explanation.

## Complete scope and fixed source boundary

Retain all four BIGMEG participants, 12 main sessions each and 10 runs per session. The published acquisition has 27,048 trials per person: 22,248 unique experimental images, 2,400 presentations of 200 repeat images, and 2,400 catch presentations. The benchmark predicts all 88,992 experimental trials across the four people. The 9,600 repeat and 9,600 catch presentations remain in source inventory and provenance; they are excluded from prediction by the predeclared trial type, never by a decoder result. The two additional MEG sessions mentioned in the paper were separately planned datasets and are not part of this release's 12-session task.

All 1,854 concepts, their first 12 exemplars, and all 27 original overlapping labels are retained. Multiple memberships and no memberships are valid. Do not substitute THINGSplus categories, continuous animacy/size, top-level labels, a favored subset, or repeated-image recognition. The complete experimental support is fixed before any candidate or comparator result. Missing or incompatible inputs prevent qualification; they do not silently remove a participant, session, concept, label, window, or arm.

The selected task metadata names OpenNeuro ds004212 v2.0.1, commit `5405e3ef6478cd55da62d14c9340b4f95f620b03`. The paper cites v2.0.0. Both facts are retained. The release-to-release bridge and code-to-derivative provenance are open gates. ## Signals, timing and representations

Keep the existing source-relative, offline 80-ms windows: PRE [-100,-20), EARLY_MINUS [60,140), EARLY [80,160), EARLY_PLUS [100,180), and LATE [200,280) ms. Every window has four half-open 20-ms bins. MEG uses native 200-Hz sample ordinals, with four samples per bin. Eyes use 1,200-Hz ordinals, with 24 samples per bin. Rounded CSV timestamps validate the clock; they do not select bins. There is no selected peak latency, best window, or learned time shift.

The original core representation is preserved: 271 ordered sensor channels × four means = 1,084 neural features; x/y/pupil means and finite fractions for four bins = 24 ocular features; and the exact 103 unlearned file-pixel features. Arms remain VISUAL, OCULAR, NUISANCE (visual + ocular), NEURAL, and JOINT. No family appears in another arm by way of filenames, missingness metadata, tool output, or cached states. A query can expose the permitted numeric family values and feature schema; its source image/concept identity, trigger, row order, response, file path and held labels remain supervisor-private.

The author used a CTF array of radial first-order gradiometers; MNE's selected channel-kind label `mag` is a software classification, not proof that processed values are raw magnetometer tesla. The released MEG values are epoch-baseline z-scores. The paper's 272 available hardware channels become 271 after the source's MRO11 exclusion. A common sensor name does not align anatomy across heads. The author overwrites device/head transforms when concatenating runs/sessions; this cannot establish anatomical registration. All retained sessions remain mandatory while actual header/kind/order and geometry qualification remains open.

The source applies whole-run filtering, epoch baseline normalization and whole-run eye cleaning. The eye source additionally crops one raw object, adjusts event samples, and writes cropped values into a copy of another object. The existing extractor validates relative epochs and image correspondence but not absolute cross-modal onset. This makes independently validated absolute eye/MEG/photodiode origins a mandatory prerequisite for a time-matched nuisance comparison. Source code also contains potentially consequential invalid-sample and pupil-threshold behavior; the exact source expressions and unresolved qualification are recorded in SOURCE_DISCREPANCIES.json. They are not silently repaired or assumed to have affected the released derivatives.

PRE and ±20-ms windows are fixed diagnostics. Zero-phase filtering, normalization, whole-run detrending and their data-dependent masks may use later samples; even perfect origin alignment does not make these windows causal or online. A causal preprocessing/new-release study would require a separately reviewed condition on the full same cohort, not relabeling the current derivatives.

## Splits and identity protection

Preserve the 240 outer cases: 4 people × 12 session ordinals × 5 globally fixed concept groups. The existing concept hash determines the groups. Query rows satisfy held person AND held session AND held concept group. Training rows satisfy other person AND other session ordinal AND other concept group. All other rows are excluded from that invocation. Across the five concept groups, each participant/session contributes every experimental concept once. This is 6,000 case/window/arm predictions, not 6,000 biological samples.

Provide separate training-only opaque person, session, concept and image-group identifiers. The legacy combined person/session group is insufficient for valid grouped inner selection. Opaque tokens reveal equality within one invocation, not source labels or cross-invocation lookup keys. Optional inner folds are constructed from those training-only identities. Any participant-selected tuning, calibration or stopping must operate inside its eligible training partitions. The mandatory references have fixed configurations and perform no hyperparameter search. REFERENCE_BANK.json retains the complete optional inner-group API without multiplying canonical reference fits.

All outer test predictions must be sealed before any test labels or comparative evaluator summary is opened. All candidate modes/replicates and all registered comparator outputs in the selected campaign participate in that barrier. Other folds contain labels for a current held concept; mounting the union of folds, reusing investigator conversations, or retaining trained state across folds violates the contract even if the immediate process has no test-label file. Code is frozen before any benchmark training view. Fit state is frozen before query access. The inference worker receives one query row at a time and cannot learn from other query rows. This is an inductive task; transductive batch adaptation would be a separate condition, not an undocumented optimization.

Public case IDs become opaque. Arm and window can be visible because they declare allowed inputs; negative-control membership, source identities and private exposure labels cannot be exposed in prompts, argv, paths, environment, schemas or receipts. Original named cases remain in the private source map for audit. Every receipt joins the exact task/source/version/campaign/mode/replicate/outer case/window/arm/source program/training state/query/output identity. Labels cannot be recovered by guessing source order or a globally reusable hash.

## Participant programs, investigators and capability claims

The participant supplies real executable learning/analysis source, not just a numeric artifact. The controller builds it in an isolated runtime, invokes training against the permitted training view, freezes outputs and fitted state, serves allowed per-query views, collects complete 27-probability predictions, and invokes the independently protected scorer. The scorer never imports participant code. Independent source custody and actual launch/completion/streams/resource observations are required; an author-supplied success flag is insufficient.

P is a prewritten frozen program. I is an adaptive investigator with exactly the same family inputs, training groups, licensed offline knowledge, installed numerical tools, reference-model tools and total accounted budget. I may choose analyses on training data and perform query-specific tool calls under a frozen access policy, but cannot access other queries, mutate fitted state using queries, request labels, or receive hidden-score feedback. Each query investigator is reset from the same permitted training-state checkpoint. Its state cannot carry information across query rows or folds. A decoder-only condition and matched model-tool availability ablations distinguish assistance from unaided capability; every fit, tool call and model invocation is charged.

D is a separately labeled, no-tools diagnostic on a trusted training-only eight-dimensional projection and class summaries, covering the full outer query support. Its information and capacity differ from P/I; it cannot be pooled into the same ranking or described as raw-signal understanding. All 27 binary classes receive positive/negative training counts, means and population variances in the same eight coordinates; the query receives the eight coordinates and the class names. No fitted logistic weights or test labels are supplied. Fixed diagonal-Gaussian and nearest-centroid decoders receive exactly the same serialized numbers. This tests direct use of a compressed numerical representation, not full raw MEG comprehension. Missing rank, class support, exact serialization or context capacity invalidates this diagnostic; no silent truncation or chosen exemplar subset is allowed.

R is scientific interpretation after every prediction is frozen. It receives a deterministic report of all admitted arms/windows, individual-person effects, support and uncertainty together with the source limitations. It must distinguish measured differences, alternatives, unsupported extrapolations, and missing evidence. Arithmetic consistency and evidence citation are machine-checkable; the quality of a causal or methodological explanation requires a separately qualified blinded rubric or human review. No reward for a preferred positive biological story is specified. R cannot change predictions, and exposure to reports is recorded. Subsequent episodes using those reports belong to a new exposed campaign.

## Independent controls and estimands

The legacy eight-total-PC logistic recipe remains a historical reference, with its exact rank and convergence refusals. It does not isolate conditional information: JOINT reallocates the same eight-dimensional representation away from its nuisance families. The mandatory reference bank adds fixed regularized binary LDA on the full features and on additive projections retaining eight coordinates per family. The paper used default LDA; these regularized 27-label references are explicitly different methods. Their configurations are fixed before training, independently fitted and sealed. The primary nuisance comparator is full-core R1; the participant cannot provide or weaken it. Nonlinear and tuning studies are separate prospective full-scope extensions.

The benchmark's operational primary endpoint is EARLY balanced log-loss reduction of candidate JOINT relative to that independent core NUISANCE comparator, on exactly the same held rows. Report raw candidate loss and candidate JOINT improvement over an independently qualified JOINT comparator as separate endpoints. The primary difference mixes the candidate method with the information-set difference; label it accordingly. Do not call it a causal or information-theoretic neural increment. Candidate JOINT minus candidate NUISANCE is retained descriptively and never used as the ranking denominator.

The scientific diagnostic compares each fixed R0/R1/R2 family on JOINT and NUISANCE, preserving all windows and explicit fixed-total/additive capacity conditions. There is no canonical reference selection grid. A more flexible nuisance model may erase the effect; that remains a valid alternative until a separate full-scope nonlinear study is conducted. Equal feature counts are not equal effective capacity. Qualification must establish exact training support, stable fitting, independently checked numerical semantics and adequacy for the declared bounded model comparison. It does not certify the strongest possible nuisance predictor. Missing required qualification makes the primary score null; a correct weak, null or negative scientific result is still reported.

Stronger visual-feature controls, including the source paper's CORnet V1/IT motivation, are a prespecified extension gate. Exact model code, weights, training exposure, feature layer and stimulus rendering are not currently closed. They cannot be silently downloaded or substituted. Results of the core 103-feature condition must name that restriction; absence of a stronger control prevents a claim that visual explanations have been exhausted, but does not turn a correct core predictive score into an estimate of a different information set.

For every label within a participant/session, class-balance the two Bernoulli outcome classes, then average equally over all 27 labels, 12 sessions and four people. Preserve threshold 0.5 and natural-log floor 1e-15 for legacy comparability, while recording exact-zero/one counts because clipping changes the unbounded logarithmic rule. The design also requests unweighted prevalence-respecting log loss as calibration context; it is not interchangeable with balanced loss. In the balanced pseudo-population a constant 0.5 predictor is a proper reference, whereas a training prevalence prior serves the unweighted metric. Report both honestly.

Keep all five windows/arms and their paired contrasts. EARLY_MINUS/PLUS are sensitivity checks, PRE is a leakage/missingness diagnostic, and LATE provides a fixed temporal contrast; no best-window selection. The original four-person mean/SE/t interval is descriptive, not a calibrated population inference. Do not infer independence from 88,992 trials, 27 correlated labels, overlapping windows, or 240 shared-training folds. Retain all four person results, whole-concept dependence, and the shared-stimulus limitation. Any future inferential resampling needs an exchangeability/estimand plan and an actual full refitting protocol; a convenient row shuffle or post-hoc sign threshold is not selected.

## Grading and completeness

Validation first checks complete source and split identities, all candidate/reference jobs, exact output support, finite independent probabilities and original process evidence. Any missing artifact, malformed probability, unsupported abstention, changed program/state, unexpected query, unaccounted process or resource violation prevents a complete primary result before target access. All failures and partial costs remain visible. A successful null or negative effect passes correctness; it is not converted into a benchmark failure.

Only the supervisor maps source rows to opaque query IDs and holds target descriptors. The grader binds one target bank and one support bank to every paired comparison before scoring. Candidate/control arms cannot differ in actual labels, order, inclusion, version or denominator under matching metadata. A changed dataset-version string cannot reset the physical THINGS exposure history. NEURAL-053, this task, other THINGS-MEG views and any relevant checkpoint training exposure are tracked as one source family, with different scientific endpoints rather than independent replications.

## Build and qualification boundary

This packet is a complete scientific/API design amendment. It modifies no legacy source, registry, installed interface or data asset. It implements or executes no decoder, loader, numerical scorer, candidate, model, data/header probe or native reference. Its static checks inspect source identities and design correspondence only. All planned engineering controls are future work and are explicitly not reported as passing tests.

The producer graph covers provenance, source admission, clocks/geometry, full feature extraction, protected roster, outer views and optional inner groups, independent references, actual participant build/train/query, global sealing, scoring and interpretation. Every row has explicit inputs, outputs, private/public custody and blockers. A future implementation must close this complete graph; installing a fixed reference runner or accepting participant JSON is not completion.

Resource qualification must use the full workload. The known source footprint is about 66.18 GB before images, derived arrays and scratch; the existing projected arrays require about 1.98 GB of numerical payload before headers/manifests. The full training family pass nevertheless visits each experimental row in 132 outer memberships, about 265.15 GB of logical numerical input before repeated solver passes. Stored size is not computation cost. Per-view memory, runtime, fitting, decoder tools, model context/tokens, and storage are not measured. The workload table gives counts and formulas rather than guessed wall times or dollars. The initial eight-configuration ×27-fold reference grid was removed following feasibility review; it was not justified by the source's default-LDA analysis. All biological cases, labels, windows, arms and endpoints remain. Pure trusted VISUAL references can reuse their five identical window inputs, yielding 5,040 distinct views per fit family. R1/R2's pooled class covariances differ by specified rank-one terms, allowing a common factorization and27 label corrections; REFERENCE_ALGEBRA.md derives this exactly. Qualified arm-block reuse gives 5,280 common LDA factorizations, with a 10,080 separate-arm upper schedule and an explicitly budgeted direct-label fallback if native parity fails. For the selected P/I lane, R0 retains 136,080 independent binary logistic fits. D-only requires none of those full-core fits: it has separate training-only summary preparation and12,000 independently produced same-summary control artifacts. A run plan must prospectively specify actual per-process and aggregate read/write, CPU/wall, memory, retained storage, token/tool and parallel-worker limits, and record failures without refunds. No such scientific run is selected now.

Research-grade qualification still requires exact authentic inputs and source bridges, a valid complete environment and isolated executor, numerical/independent reference agreement, competent comparisons, grading integrity and full empirical evaluation. The current result is a defensible design for review, not a finished benchmark or measured LLM result.
