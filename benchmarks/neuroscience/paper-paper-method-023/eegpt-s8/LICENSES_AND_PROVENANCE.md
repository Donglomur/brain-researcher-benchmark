# License and provenance boundaries

The retained root LICENSE is MIT, copyright 2026 Brain Researcher, for benchmark-owned implementation and documentation. It does not relicense upstream code, datasets, papers or pretrained weights.

The unmodified ARL EEGModels.py and full LICENSE.txt are retained together at the exact selected revision. The notice combines CC0 1.0 and Apache 2.0 portions and includes jurisdiction and patent notes; it must not be abbreviated to CC0. Its selected-source provenance lists upstream source identities. Additional upstream examples, weights and README bodies are not included.

The EEGPT root Apache-2.0 license is retained as provenance, but 80 other EEGPT author-family files remain external as a whole. Concrete nested provenance includes Meta copyright headers, code explicitly copied from PyTorch, DN3/BENDR and a linked LDAM loss, LaBraM/BIOT, and a TUEV converter reference. The selected packet does not close all applicable inherited notices. Importing a library and incorporating its source are different: ordinary external imports alone are not treated as proof of copied code. The cited copied/adapted bodies and their complete family closure motivate exclusion. Root licensing alone has not been treated as clearance.

Exact BIOT and LaBraM MIT notices are retained, but their eight architecture modules remain external as a whole. BIOT residual blocks cite PyHealth examples, ST-Transformer declares modification of EEG-Transformer, and LaBraM declares BEiT-v2/timm/DeiT/DINO ancestry. The required nested notice sets are not closed by the selected packet. This package does not assert these families are non-redistributable; it records the unresolved provenance needed before bundling.

EXTERNAL_SOURCE_DEPENDENCIES.json maps each required external original role to its exact relative source path, revision, extent and SHA256 identity. This is a source intake specification, not permission to fetch data or models. The complete original 157-role union is partitioned exactly once by PROGRAMME_ROLE_DISPOSITION.json. No opaque private-history aliases are used as public code.

The exact source/copy licenses do not grant rights to authentic signals, participant metadata, protected labels, pretrained weights or paper bodies. Those assets and the native scientific runtime are external and unqualified. Public publication also does not authorize scientific execution or imply that any historical result was reproduced.
