# Five neuroscience construction drafts

Snapshot: **14 November 2026**. The programme registers **210 contracts/drafts: 108 paper and 102 neural**. **Zero are scientifically qualified or submission-ready.** This revision adds two paper designs to the previous 208-draft publication. The five specifications in this dated snapshot follow the [205-draft predecessor registry](../expansion-20261113/registry.json).

| Track | Draft | Intended evaluation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paper research | [Cortical consensus](tasks/cortical-consensus.json) | Reproduce held-neuron prediction and assess improvement under a separate protected protocol. |
| Neural data | [Patch-identified neuron localization](tasks/patch-neuron-localization.json) | Localize an identified extracellular spike source under channel degradation. |

| Paper research | [REM dynamics](tasks/rem-dynamics.json) | Reproduce spontaneous-transition and stimulation-outcome analyses, then assess event-conditioned prediction with past-only inputs and held-mouse evaluation. |

LatentCircuit covers the empirical Figure 6/Extended Data 9 result in both monkeys, including all required eligible neurons and trials and the full 200-start ensemble per animal. Published test R-squared values of 0.88/0.76 describe a learned projection representing 11%/7% of population variance; they are not full-population predictive accuracy. Improvement is assessed in fixed original-neuron coordinates. The native continuous task-output loss and any binary choice metric remain distinct. Unit counts, the 150-by-10-ms model to 15-by-50-ms neural timing join, the empirical loss and official reference adapter remain unresolved gates.

REM dynamics covers Figure 2h in six mice and Figure 5e in five mice as two strata within one paper family. The reported 110-second result is event-conditioned and uses whole-recording preprocessing; it does not establish an online warning system. The proposed improvement uses predeclared cutoffs, causal preprocessing, permitted earlier calibration and per-mouse Brier loss, reported separately for both protocols. Complete cohort support, timing, exclusions, dependency closure and the native LDA procedure remain to be established.

Each new paper design compares paper plus data with the same condition-specific inputs plus official code. Full-data reproduction and protected improvement use separate packets, workspaces and fresh authoring contexts. Reproduction transcripts, fitted artifacts and protected outcomes cannot transfer into improvement. Fresh contexts do not establish absence of public-paper or data memorization during pretraining. Published aggregate anchors are included for design review; protected outcomes are not.

The Cortical consensus, localization and StructuredExperience task files retain their exact previously published identities. Their registration-stage limitations remain operative. StructuredExperience retains all 13 sessions from eight mice, trial-grouped evaluation and a separate prospective mouse-level claim. This documentation update does not promote any task to runnable or scientifically qualified.

All five designs identify papers, repositories and data sources. Source and dependency rights, complete authentic intake, competent references, protected scorers, grouped uncertainty and real end-to-end evaluation remain required. Animals, events, conditions, folds, random starts and controls do not add benchmark registrations. No scientific data, official code, weights, private answers, model comparison or SOTA result is included.

[Machine-readable registry](registry.json) records counts and specification identities. These are public design documents, not candidate input packets or benchmark results.
