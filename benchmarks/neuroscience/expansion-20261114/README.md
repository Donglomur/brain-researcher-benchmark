The nine specifications in this dated snapshot follow the [205-draft predecessor registry](../expansion-20261113/registry.json).

LatentCircuit covers the empirical Figure 6/Extended Data 9 result in both monkeys, including all required eligible neurons and trials and the full 200-start ensemble per animal. Published test R-squared values of 0.88/0.76 describe a learned projection representing 11%/7% of population variance; they are not full-population predictive accuracy. Improvement is assessed in fixed original-neuron coordinates. The native continuous task-output loss and any binary choice metric remain distinct. Unit counts, the 150-by-10-ms model to 15-by-50-ms neural timing join, the empirical loss and official reference adapter remain unresolved gates.

REM dynamics covers Figure 2h in six mice and Figure 5e in five mice as two strata within one paper family. The reported 110-second result is event-conditioned and uses whole-recording preprocessing; it does not establish an online warning system. The proposed improvement uses predeclared cutoffs, causal preprocessing, permitted earlier calibration and per-mouse Brier loss, reported separately for both protocols. Complete cohort support, timing, exclusions, dependency closure and the native LDA procedure remain to be established.

The two paper designs published earlier compare paper plus data with the same condition-specific inputs plus official code. Full-data reproduction and protected improvement use separate packets, workspaces and fresh authoring contexts. Reproduction transcripts, fitted artifacts and protected outcomes cannot transfer into improvement. Fresh contexts do not establish absence of public-paper or data memorization during pretraining. Published aggregate anchors are included for design review; protected outcomes are not.

The seven previous task files and registry rows retain their exact published identities. The Cortical consensus, localization and StructuredExperience registration-stage limitations remain operative. StructuredExperience retains all 13 sessions from eight mice, trial-grouped evaluation and a separate prospective mouse-level claim. This documentation update does not promote any task to runnable or scientifically qualified.

Stroop uses the complete published 55-person, 3-session cohort, with 35-channel HbO/HbR inputs and three task conditions. Every participant received the same schedule within a session, so the design holds session 3 globally out and groups the fivefold evaluation by participant, suppresses chronology and audits common-mode, pre-onset and timing-only predictors. Matched direct-signal, program and interactive-tool conditions receive the same fixed six-example class-stratified pack; full-training conditions are reported separately. Each fold and condition starts from fresh model/context/decoder/cache state. Participant bootstrap intervals describe fixed fits and this observed schedule, not unconditional training-distribution or arbitrary-schedule uncertainty. The published 64-percent binary within-person peak is not a comparable three-class holdout score or our result. Exact concentration units, archive census, timing offsets, preprocessing, code hashes and competent baseline execution remain gates.

Retinal encoding preserves the original two preparations and 170-cell paper-selected cohort across white noise and natural movies. Preparation-to-animal mapping is unresolved; cells and preparations must not manufacture independent biological replication. Strict source-only new fitting/calibration is conditional on the historically test-selected cohort: it cannot undo the original test-reliability ascertainment. A prospective quality-selection claim requires verified unfiltered original membership or independent evidence for an unselected roster. Natural-movie models calibrated with WN-derived STAs are mixed-stimulus or target-assisted routes and cannot populate strict NM-to-WN fitting scores. Historical test-informed smoothing curves remain separate from development-frozen predictive baselines.

The original retinal loader opens protected test responses even when its responses flag is false. Candidate execution therefore requires an audited train-only adapter, protected-path denial and no target-bearing derived objects. Protected prediction cases expose stimuli/timebase and opaque IDs only, never neural targets; encoding accuracy alone does not prove native neural understanding. Complete calibration-packet capacity and evidence parity remain open. The original retinal deposit advertises 47 GiB; the 15,148,964,517-byte alternative processed route has unresolved two-versus-four-preparation mapping and rights, and is neither a substitute verified cohort nor another family. Stroop's exact versioned archive is 5,076,812,783 bytes. No archive was acquired, and compressed sizes do not establish expanded disk or runtime feasibility.

Auditory brainstem response transfer holds the complete 104-mouse Marcotti cohort out for expert-threshold prediction, with every eligible animal from the complete Manor/Liberman support releases available to the separate full-support condition. The intended input is an averaged evoked voltage stack; physical units and sound-level calibration remain to be authenticated. It is not a single-trial signal or behavioral hearing measurement. The shared 244-point/10-ms representation and click encoding require native authentication. The four-example native, advance-written program and interactive-tool conditions receive identical labelled stacks and a byte-identical documentation packet; complete case/pack context feasibility is still a gate. Full-support comparisons are separate, and bundled mixed-lab weights cannot serve as unseen-lab baselines without provenance excluding all target animals.

ABR loss averages threshold error within each animal and then across all 104 animals. Finite estimates count even when flagged as abstentions; any missing, invalid or nonfinite required estimate withholds the aggregate primary score. Censoring must follow authentic source labels, never a model default. The original code's all-negative first-level return, consecutive-negative stopping rule and interpolation/truncation behavior remain distinct from paper-rule reconstruction and a validated predictive baseline. Animal bootstrap intervals are conditional on fixed source fits and this one held laboratory. The published mixed-lab AUROC of 0.98 is contextual, not a score on this target or our result. The three selected archives total 165,144,756 compressed bytes. Data records declare CC BY 4.0; an explicit author-code/weight redistribution grant remains unresolved. An independent implementation from published methods is possible but has not been built or validated here.

APCx covers all three mice, all 42 reported sessions and all original quality-passing units. The proposed canonical processed file is 281,948,826 bytes; its identical alias is not another cohort. The intended target counts physical valve-pulse events across 0–5 seconds, while the neural observations cover 0.5–5 seconds. Every case uses the same initial 500-ms exclusion; masks or sniff features triggered by unknown pulse times are forbidden. The published integrated-command ceiling count and positive-difference event detector are not proven equivalent. Authentic command units, duration, overlap, boundary rules and released labels must be reconciled across every trial before this primary can run.

Each APCx session uses chronological calibration/development/test partitions because its neuron basis differs. Native, tools and programs receive the same first 16 calibration and eight development trials plus byte-identical documentation; full-calibration comparisons stay separate. Paper DUNL receives true odor timing and count-dependent information, so it cannot populate a blind decoder comparison. Historical AIC selection cannot be undone by a new train-only selector: if the release contains only selected units, the preferred all-quality-passing primary remains blocked. A conditional historical route requires an explicit versioned decision and matching baselines, cannot silently replace the primary and is not another family. Animal-macro RMSE, three animal-level values, all session values and conditional temporal uncertainty must not imply 42 independent subjects. Data are CC BY 4.0 and pinned code has an MIT license; native coverage, chronology, targets, full neural-code closure, protected adapters and competent references remain unverified.

All nine designs identify primary papers and data sources, with code availability stated per source. Stroop author scripts are reported inside its versioned data archive; no separate author GitHub repository was verified. Source and dependency rights, complete authentic intake, competent references, protected scorers, grouped uncertainty and real end-to-end evaluation remain required. Animals, events, conditions, folds, random starts and controls do not add benchmark registrations. No scientific data, official code, weights, private answers, model comparison or SOTA result is included.
