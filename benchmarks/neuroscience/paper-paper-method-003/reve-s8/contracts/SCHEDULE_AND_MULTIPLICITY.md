# Schedule and multiplicity clarifications

## N01: cyclic WSD is not intrinsically non-paper

The primary page 6 explicitly describes a cyclic trapezoidal variant. Cyclicity, or a cooldown occurring across epochs, cannot by itself fail paper fidelity. Its prose specifies first-epoch 10% warmup, 80% peak and decay to 1% peak, but does not uniquely recover every integer boundary, resume convention and optimizer-update trace. Do not invent a complete historical trajectory from that prose.

Retained utils/optim.py defines W=floor(0.1*N), D=floor(0.1*N), P=N-W-D for its selected fractional config and cycle length K=W+P+D. For scheduler state k, cycle position is k modulo K. The first cycle starts at start_lr; later cycles start at end_lr. The warmup uses cycle_start+(peak-cycle_start)*(cycle_position/W); the plateau uses peak; the cooldown uses peak-(peak-end)*((cycle_position-W-P)/D). Integer endpoint conventions mean the last cooldown call need not equal end_lr before the next cycle starts. Nonpositive segment lengths or K must be explicitly rejected or source-disposed; no hidden denominator workaround. This is a static reading, not a native run.

src/train.py passes len_train_sampler divided by configured total devices as N, creates the scheduler once, and calls scheduler.step in the loader loop. The actual optimizer-update index under Accelerate/DDP/accumulation, initial scheduler constructor step, skipped updates, rank behavior and resume state depend on the qualified environment. These are not inferred to equal sample, microbatch, optimizer or epoch indices. Native config start_lr=1e-5, end_lr=1e-6 and peak inherited from trainer.lr stay distinct from the paper's end-to-peak fraction.

ScheduleProfile and StepExposureTrace must bind exact source/config/environment; first applied LR and scheduler-before/after-optimizer order; integer W/P/D/K; first/subsequent cycle start; peak/end; microbatch, accumulation and synchronized optimizer counters; scheduler last_epoch and resume state; skipped/overflow updates; rank scaling; per-step windows/masked-patches/samples/people exposure; per-group LR; total updates and effective batch. Independent reference comparison checks these sequences and source/paper constraints. Two cyclic schedules can disagree; a cyclic schedule satisfying the qualified paper convention cannot be rejected solely for cycling. G09 stays open.

## N02: confirmatory A/B union

The primary prospective improvement family is the fixed UNION of candidate arms A and B: 2 arms × 20 dataset-condition cells × 4 independently fixed comparators = 160 paired balanced-accuracy hypotheses. Each arm retains its 80 original contrasts; neither arm's outputs nor any of the 20 cells/four reference families are dropped. These 160 contrasts are not 160 reference fits: the existing 80 logical reference fit/prediction families remain, with separately reported run/domain multiplicity and lawful reference reuse.

Apply Holm to the full 160 predeclared p-value slots, retaining p=1 solely for unavailable-adjustment bookkeeping and null scientific estimates. Do not choose an arm after inspecting outcomes and claim its within-arm 80-slot correction covered the search. Both complete arm results are reported. Person-cluster replicates must carry paired A/B/reference predictions and complete relevant supports together; shared identities across datasets still require the qualified alias/joint-resampling manifest. Undefined replicates, bootstrap calibration and small biological sample limitations remain unchanged. The ordinary 95% intervals are marginal intervals, not simultaneous familywise intervals.

The 20 direct A-versus-B primary-endpoint differences are explicitly exploratory descriptive comparisons in this selection. They receive no confirmatory superiority declaration and are not silently covered by the 160 candidate-versus-reference family. A future confirmatory A/B question would require a newly selected preregistered multiplicity family, such as the complete 180-slot union, before any relevant label/outcome exposure; it cannot be added after seeing results. No experiment or new condition is executed here. G23 remains open.
