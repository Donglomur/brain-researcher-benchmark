The operator fixes the complete reference output index; the participant cannot supply it.

The bank must cover all required case/run/event/class rows and all corpus/architecture/soup products. Hashes alone cannot certify native correctness. The protected grader computes reference metrics from admitted predictions and targets and compares candidate/reference estimates on identical support.
