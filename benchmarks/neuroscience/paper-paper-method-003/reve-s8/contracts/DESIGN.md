# Scientific question and conditions

REVE combines a spatial/temporal EEG transformer with large heterogeneous pretraining and adaptation. The scientific question is whether its reported representational advantages survive complete reproduction and controlled transfer, and whether a participant-derived method can improve those outcomes under matched information and budgets. Classification of a dataset label alone does not establish neural understanding, biological mechanism, clinical usefulness or causal reasoning. Full reporting therefore includes the architecture, corpus, negative ablations, sparse montage behavior, few-shot calibration and resource costs rather than rewarding a single favorable accuracy.

The primary reproduction mode is full participant-derived training from the selected source corpus through all downstream families. A supplied-checkpoint replay can later be a separately labeled supplementary condition, with identical authorized checkpoint exposure in A and B; it never replaces full pretraining or inherits full-paper completion. The current design does not acquire weights. Participants may submit arbitrary scientifically appropriate source, but reproduction claims must identify how it implements each required original operator. For improvement they may change the learner, representation, adaptation and preprocessing within fixed data/exposure/compute constraints; a wrapper that simply selects a fixed evaluator-owned model is not the participant programme.

R-paper is the participant reproduction purpose in both A and B. Its results determine which source-specific comparisons can be qualified. Their differences are evidence, not silently reconciled. Neither an unresolved native path nor a repaired source clone proves the published result. I-person preserves the predecessor strict_person question across all ten datasets, with genuinely disjoint biological training/validation/query people, training-only fitted transformations and validation-only search. I-person-montage additionally preserves strict_person_and_montage: exact normalized channel-set memberships and geometries must be disjoint between train, validation and query. Many source datasets have a single fixed montage; that condition is currently a feasibility gate. A renamed channel layout is not a held montage. An artificial sensor-partition experiment would require a separately selected amended scientific protocol and must not be passed off as existing natural montage transfer. Absence of a valid condition is reported against the whole denominator, not silently dropped. Source within-person speech and supervised few-shot calibration are separate source conditions; neither becomes zero-shot held-person transfer by relabeling.

Full-paper improvement means the candidate addresses the complete architecture/pretraining/ablation/soup and downstream programme, while superiority is assessed on protected outcomes against independent competent references. The 20 existing prospective dataset-condition cells remain distinct. Reproduction of a negative or null ablation earns faithful scientific credit; no metric rewards agreement with a favored positive conclusion. There is no invented single scalar reward or universal ten-dataset mean. Signed endpoint differences, uncertainties, completeness, methods fidelity and resources are reported separately. A frontier claim additionally needs a current independently authenticated comparator set under equal inputs; the paper's 2025 rows alone do not establish current SOTA.

## Fixed denominators and failure treatment

Before any query release, the operator seals a programme roster with all source obligations, original case IDs, additional missing scientific products, training identities, run rosters, A/B/purpose conditions, reference roles, query supports and required products. Each obligation ends as complete-qualified, complete-unqualified, unavailable-with-evidence, invalid, failed, timed-out or resource-exhausted. Missing data, unsupported montage, missing baseline, absent checkpoint, absent class, undefined metric and failed producer remain counted. A failure may produce a bounded terminal status, not fabricated uniform predictions or replacement gold. Completeness is reported separately from conditional performance. Full-paper completion requires every mandatory obligation, not a favorable subset.

Published mean/SD tables are provenance-bound public comparands. They are not 2,144 hidden per-case gold values. Exact historical reference predictions and original run identities remain unavailable. Regenerating a baseline produces a new matched-reference experiment, labeled separately from copied published numbers. All narrative conclusions are tied to measured outputs, uncertainty and exposure; discussion-only claims such as exact scaling laws or clinical diagnosis cannot be scored as experimentally demonstrated findings.
