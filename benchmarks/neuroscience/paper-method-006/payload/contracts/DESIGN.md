# Complete scientific design: Spike sorting with Kilosort4

This is the complete-scope design for existing **PAPER-paper-method-006**, DOI10.1038/s41592-024-02232-7. It does not qualify, register or implement a benchmark. All five main figures, seven Extended Data figures, Table1, primary Methods, Supplementary Note, Reporting Summary and original construction support remain mandatory. `ORIGINAL_SCOPE.json` expands the captions into71 panel/GUI obligations and34 Methods sections. Those are bookkeeping units, not additional benchmarks. Unknown supplementary obligations are an open inventory, not an empty one.

The scientific question is whether an investigator can reconstruct the original method and evidence, and improve recovery of single-unit spike trains under realistic overlap, noise and drift, with the same paper/data resources either without or with authenticated author code. The original three-hybrid package is insufficient. This design retains the full drift/probe, comparator, ablation, upstream construction, real-recording and biophysical support.

## Assessment cells and scientific meaning

There are four access/objective cells: A–R, B–R, A–I and B–I. A receives the admitted paper, complete observations, common resources and written benchmark contracts. B receives exactly that packet plus the admitted output-free author-source version. R is reproduction; I is meaningful improvement. R has two separately reported numerical profiles, `R_source20` and `R_paper40`; these do not create additional canonical tasks. I uses `I_bijective_full`, an explicitly authored evaluation convention that preserves the original sorting problem and fixes its metric contract. No F/I provider condition or capability claim is selected.

`R_source20` preserves the captured v4.0.2 algorithms, source defaults or authenticated publication overrides, event-reuse behavior, score floor, quality classification and failure behavior. It is exact released-source reproduction, not automatically publication reproduction. `R_paper40` preserves the paper's40-neighbor definition, complete experimental scope and published procedures. Where the paper leaves a numerical choice ambiguous or contradicts captured source, that output remains required-unscorable until the publication producer/configuration or a separately reviewed reconstruction policy resolves it. A corrected implementation cannot overwrite the literal-source result. Explicit reconstruction choices must be labeled as such and cannot be passed off as historical author behavior.

The I estimand is performance on a fixed, historically public finite suite. It is conditional on the author's waveform selection, simulated noise, injected-unit truth and accessible original cases. It is not accuracy on every naturally occurring neuron, proof of generalization to new animals, or evidence that the candidate never previously encountered these public labels. Protected future grading controls protocol leakage; it cannot erase prior public exposure.

## Improvement decision

I commits one method artifact per access arm before any protected evaluation truth or R follow-up artifacts are released. The method may estimate unsupervised state from the entire current recording; spike sorting is a transductive signal-decomposition task. It may not retain fitted state from a previous query, receive final truth-feedback hyperparameter search, or replace complete-recording sorting with chronological behavioral prediction. The selected I policy has no supervised label-feedback tuning. Internal SVD, k-means initialization, drift fitting, clustering and merge work are still fits and are fully counted.

The scorer recomputes spike matching and quality from output events. `I_bijective_full` uses exhaustive unit-pair support, one-to-one event matching and an injective recovered-unit assignment. This prevents repeated detection of the same injected/simulated unit from multiplying its recovery credit. It is a benchmark metric, not a claim that the paper used this matching algorithm.

Let h be the mean recovered-unit fraction over the three hybrids, d the mean over all six drift conditions, and b the mean over normal/twice-speed biophysical conditions. The primary recovery endpoint is r=(h+d+b)/3, with equal weight for each scientific family and equal case weights inside a family. Each case denominator is every admitted source truth unit; the caption's250 biophysical units must be confirmed before fixing that denominator. This family weighting is a declared benchmark choice, distinct from the paper's pooled300-unit hybrid curve.

The guard endpoint q is the mean unmatched-good-unit count divided by the complete evaluation-truth-unit count over the six full synthetic and two biophysical cases, with equal family weight between synthetic and biophysical. Hybrid unmatched natural units are excluded from this false-positive-unit endpoint because they are not labeled. A known-truth duplicate endpoint counts extra detected units with a recovery edge to already represented evaluation truth. For each case c define v_c=duplicate_count_c/U_c, where U_c is its complete designated evaluation-truth roster. The duplicate guard is v=[mean_H(v_c)+mean_D(v_c)+mean_B(v_c)]/3, using all3 hybrid, all6 synthetic and both biophysical cases with equal within-family case weights. It requires aggregate v not to increase; there is no additional per-case duplicate inequality. Every case's count, denominator and paired delta is reported. Per-unit spike FP/FN, precision/recall, independent best-score curves, quality decisions, runtime and memory accompany these summaries.

A method is called a **meaningful finite-suite improvement over a frozen comparator** only if: its r increases by at least0.02; neither h,d nor b decreases; no individual truth-supported case loses more than0.02 recovered fraction; q does not increase; and the normalized known-truth duplicate count does not increase. Every mandatory diagnostic/source/output and predeclared resource gate must also pass. The0.02 margin and guard policy are prospective benchmark conventions, not publication thresholds or estimated statistical significance. Continuous deltas remain visible even when this joint decision fails. A p-value, selective high-norm gain or aggregate gain hiding a dropped case does not suffice.

Mandatory comparator reports include source-qualified Kilosort4 and every authenticated original comparator on its original support. The primary superiority claim is first against frozen Kilosort4. A broader “strong-reference improvement” claim additionally requires the same joint criterion against each prespecified strong comparator (IronClust, MountainSort4 and SpyKING CIRCUS) on all11 truth-supported I cases. The prospective I reference core is therefore4×11=44 complete sorts before diagnostic roots, two metric banks and failures; exact immutable reuse may avoid repeated native sorts. This deliberately adds biophysical strong-comparator runs beyond the paper's original Kilosort2 comparison and fully charges them. There is no final-truth oracle selection of the easiest reference. Missing or incompetent references make that claim null; they are not quietly removed. Historical paper tuning is disclosed when it influenced the fixed comparator, so the result is retrospective engineering comparison, not an untouched-test generalization estimate.

Real, linear-probe and tetrode outputs support interpretation and failure detection rather than fabricated truth scores. They must preserve complete events, waveform/ACG summaries, required processing stages and original source windows. Missing original method-specific plots cannot be concealed by offering generic attractive visualizations. The investigator must explain what changed mechanistically, where recovery/error tradeoffs occur, why synthetic success may fail on real data, and the limitations of injected-unit and quality-surrogate truth.

No inferential independence is assigned to600 units, six simulations, shared waveform donors, two step geometries or two biophysical speeds. The selected result is a descriptive fixed-suite effect. No unit bootstrap, animal-level confidence interval or population p-value is authorized by this design. New-source generalization requires an additional prospectively generated/source-separated experiment and its full costs.

## Resources, failure and stage gates

`workload/` defines the literal operation graph, source-derived allocations, candidate contexts, two numerical banks, historical tuning and failed work. One sorter launch is not one fit: the default pipeline has multiple SVD/k-means operations and two graph-clustering passes, plus per-window seed/update/merge/split work. The literal simulator allocates a full signal rather than streaming it. CCGs visit all pairs in their window; matching scans labels repeatedly; dense clustering/merge state can dominate memory.

I uses one fresh-recording worker per admitted distinct query root and arm, with selected scientific-worker concurrency1. Thus base candidate workers are2J_I, not automatically18 or22. Full R has two complete arm graphs,2J_R, with explicit node reuse only when immutable inputs, profile, version and outputs are identical. Numerical-bank workers and comparator runs are separate. Concurrency1 constrains simultaneous scientific workers, not the sorter's internal CPU/GPU threads; the environment profile must pin those too.

Store original objects, admitted decoded observations, protected truth, source/environment, generated outputs and logs, and peak concurrent scratch according to the typed retention schedule. Record logical data reads/writes, physical I/O where measured, CPU/GPU elapsed time, peak host/device memory, subprocesses and failed/partial jobs separately. A source byte count is not measured RSS or runtime. Exact dimensions, numbers of spikes/units/clusters, old sorter dependencies and full512-recording storage remain unknown. No fabricated finite hardware estimate or shortened recording closes these gaps.

Implementation remains blocked until original source/data/producer closure, exact profile semantics, full numerical-bank contracts, environments and a feasible finite per-family resource reservation have been reviewed. Authentic admission, independent numerical implementations/controls, native parity, competent reference execution, isolation tests and full empirical qualification require later grants. This phase performed only source reading, metadata authoring and specifically selected primary-source retrieval. It ran no authentic or invented scientific fixture, sorter, matching reference, simulation or candidate.
