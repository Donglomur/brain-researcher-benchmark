# NEURAL-097: protected MEG content prediction

## Decision and scientific question

Complete the existing NEURAL-097 assignment as **single-trial face/object prediction from fixed early MEG sensor features, jointly generalizing to a held-out person and held-out image identities**, with measured task-condition controls. Preserve every one of the 33 declared SA-prefix V1/task-dur participants, both image halves, three durations, three orientations and both permitted relevance conditions. This is one existing assignment and one reused COGITATE corpus. It is not a reproduction of the full Nature paper, a source-space ROI decoder, an online decoder or a verdict on consciousness theories.

The principal finite-panel estimand is the performance of a prespecified learning procedure on each selected person's previously withheld trials, after learning only from other selected people and the opposite image half. All 33 people are held out in turn. “Unseen person/image” describes this training–query boundary; it does not prove population, site, instrumentation or external-corpus generalization. No usable complete cohort or authentic input has yet been verified.

The scientific extension is a **descriptive comparison of fixed, bounded prediction procedures on the same finite panel**. Retain SIGNAL, PRE and NUISANCE and add SIGNAL+NUISANCE and PRE+NUISANCE. Their paired score differences quantify how the specified learner/search/query policy performs when given these different projections. Function class, optimization, regularization, input dimension, training sample and seed effects are part of that comparison. It is neither a conditional-information estimator nor a conditional-independence test. Neither sign proves information presence or absence, neural origin, removal of measured or unmeasured confounding, population generalization or a consciousness theory.

The original three-arm programme has 66 folds and 198 fold/arm outputs. The complete proposed five-arm programme has 330 fold/arm outputs per access/mode/seed setting. These are workload counts, not new benchmarks or biological replicates. No existing source is changed in this design phase.

## Why the paper decoder is not the benchmark reference

The paper uses source-reconstructed MEG, 1–40 Hz filtering, 100 Hz sampling, temporal smoothing, label-defined pseudotrials, normalization, top-30 feature selection and linear SVMs. Its category comparisons include within-task and cross-task decoding. The pinned author Category_CCD/Category_WCD explicitly group observations by category before averaging even the evaluation-side pseudotrials. Those procedures require evaluation labels to construct their inputs and cannot be a hidden-label single-trial predictor.

The six magnetometers come from the author's **plotting configuration**, not the published source-ROI feature set. No claim is made that these are the paper's optimal sensors. The fixed six-sensor choice and [0.10,0.30) s window are benchmark conventions established before any authentic evaluation in this project. A result measures the specified procedures' predictive performance using these particular summaries. It cannot identify an amount or anatomical source of neural information, general raw-signal understanding or the paper's posterior-versus-prefrontal comparison.

Further exact-source discrepancies remain gates: the paper describes fixed amplitude rejection while pinned P07 uses data-dependent autoreject thresholds; config constants differ again. The paper describes volumetric forward modelling, whereas the retained ROI code selects a surface forward file. The source inverse helper's SNR/lambda expression also requires native/historical reconciliation before any reproduction claim. None is silently repaired or imported into N097. N097 does not execute that inverse pipeline.

For person p and held image half h, query every eligible trial of p whose image is in h. Train only on other people and images in 1−h. The two query halves are disjoint and pooled once before person-level summaries. Entire-person exclusion automatically excludes every run/session belonging to that person; additionally require a complete run/session map and prohibit identity aliases that split the same person across folds.

Training-only sidecars are a required API amendment. Nuisance variables are query features only in arms whose contract explicitly includes them.

Inner validation crosses four disjoint training-person groups with two training-image groups. The image split is formed from training-only identity groups, stratified using training labels; it never reuses the outer half parity, which is constant inside the outer training set. All views of each image and all runs of each person remain grouped. Pool the two disjoint inner image predictions per held training person. The fixed model-selection criterion is equal-person task-irrelevant class-balanced accuracy; ties use balanced log loss, then lower complexity. This training criterion is explicitly less finely stratified than the final nine-cell metric. Missing inner classes or participants invalidate that configuration; no query support or labels are consulted to rescue it.

The prediction stage is inductive; the historical upstream derivative may not be. This distinction must appear in results.

## Normative estimand and reporting hierarchy

ESTIMAND_REPORTING_CONTRACT.json is the normative definition of the measurement, reporting order, exact settings, seeds, signs and failure rules. SCIENTIFIC_CONTRACT.json, INTERFACES.json and PROGRAMME.json pin its exact file bytes. A future controller must refuse a mismatched selected contract before private targets or references are opened. This is a design requirement; no new grader or runner is implemented here.

For each complete candidate procedure L, feature arm a, access/mode setting z and seed s, the score is M(L,a,z,s,r,m) on the same fixed query panel, relevance r and metric m. Both held-image halves are pooled once for each person. The metric averages the nine duration/orientation cells and then all 33 people; class-balanced metrics also weight the two classes equally within cells. A feature contrast is the literal left score minus right score, and equals the equal-person mean of the paired person differences. R2 comparisons use the same feature arm, numerical observations and splits, but a separate fixed algorithm. They are not a matched LLM access/mode intervention.

The fixed reporting order is:

1. **H01 primary prediction:** B/F/SIGNAL, Irrelevant, balanced accuracy, separately for seeds 97097, 97098 and 97099. This preserves the legacy within-panel primary metric.
2. **H02 reference comparison:** for each same seed, that candidate score minus the fixed qualified R2 SIGNAL/Irrelevant balanced accuracy. R2 uses the declared seed 97097 and is reused transparently across candidate conditions. R2 is never chosen from test results.
3. **H03 feature augmentation:** B/F, Irrelevant, balanced accuracy of SIGNAL+NUISANCE minus NUISANCE, separately for all three seeds.
4. **H04 temporal-feature control:** B/F, Irrelevant, balanced accuracy of SIGNAL+NUISANCE minus PRE+NUISANCE, separately for all three seeds.
5. **H05 mandatory secondary reporting:** every absolute score and all four feature pairs across five arms where applicable, all four settings, both relevance groups, all three seeds and all four metrics; all R0–R4 absolute and candidate-relative comparisons; paired B−A access and I−F mode comparisons under their qualified matching conditions; support, failures, complete costs, planned controls and separate interpretation reports.

H03/H04 are prespecified secondary feature comparisons, not co-primary information tests. They share the same SIGNAL+NUISANCE operand and panel, so they are not independent confirmations. The hierarchy fixes display and interpretation priority; it supplies no alpha-spending scheme, p-value, significance threshold, combined score or permission to select whichever result is favorable. Relevant non-target results never enter the Irrelevant primary.

All contrast values are literal left minus right. Positive balanced-accuracy differences favor left predictive accuracy; negative log-loss/Brier differences favor left loss. Legacy log loss uses within-cell trial prevalence, while the additional log loss and Brier weight classes equally. Report units and both operands; do not silently reverse loss signs or conflate a null value (unavailable) with a measured zero.

For each unchanged report cell, show all three seed records. A descriptive mean and min/max require three complete finite values under the same prospectively fixed campaign-level agent/model/prompt/code-authoring procedure, cohort, projections, splits, metrics, access/mode and budgets. Realized programs, chosen learner families and fitted parameters may differ across seeds as intended agent variation and are reported individually; byte-identical authored code across seeds is not required. Within each setting/seed, the same sealed family/search/query policy still applies across all five feature arms. A missing seed makes that summary unavailable; no mean over survivors. References form a single fixed qualified panel, not three artificial replicates. The three seeds are descriptive realized algorithm/agent variation, not biological replicates or calibrated uncertainty.

After all campaign attempts are closed and predictions/outcomes sealed, a complete condition consists of all 66 folds × five arms for one access/mode/seed. Any missing, malformed, abstained or unclosed candidate output blocks private target/reference access for that condition; no partial condition is scored. An admitted condition with missing private class support retains its exact null cell/person/panel metrics and reasons without dropping people. Empty cells also null legacy log loss. A contrast requires both complete matched operands; missing R2 makes H02 unavailable without substituting another reference. A valid absolute candidate score may be retained as a descriptive result while references remain unavailable, but does not qualify the benchmark. All settings, seeds, references, controls and gates remain mandatory for full completion.

The interpretation stage receives a fixed, complete bounded aggregate projection with this same order, identities, units, support and unavailable statuses. Failure to fit the complete projection within the existing report cap makes that report unavailable; it cannot remove unfavorable rows. Positive contrasts show only higher realized predictive performance of the specified procedures. Zero or negative contrasts do not establish absence of information: model restrictions, optimization, regularization, feature dimension, finite samples, noise and thresholding can limit or reverse measured differences. No sign establishes neural/cortical origin, absence of measured or unmeasured confounding, population generalization or IIT/GNWT truth. ## Scientific interpretation and what this measures

After predictions and all condition outputs are sealed, a separate one-pass interpretation stage receives only a fixed aggregate-results projection and the public source narrative. It cannot change predictions. It must distinguish category prediction from consciousness, acknowledge source-versus-sensor and pseudotrial-versus-single-trial differences, state the exact cohort/membership limits, interpret control/reference comparisons, discuss eye/motor/context alternatives and propose a falsifying follow-up. Results may be null, reversed or unavailable.

A prespecified rubric scores six dimensions (0–2 each): faithful estimand, correct source/cohort reconciliation, valid comparisons, calibrated uncertainty, plausible alternatives and evidence-linked falsifiability. Two blinded expert raters and adjudication are proposed; agreement and construct validity remain empirical gates. This score is separate from prediction accuracy and not delegated to an unvalidated LLM judge. No human review is requested or conducted now.

Trusted operation traces document tool use; group-safe construction and predictive outputs supply evidence about task performance; matched data arms and controls give bounded predictive-performance comparisons; the separately validated interpretation rubric assesses scientific reasoning. These measurements do not identify four independent latent abilities automatically. The inputs are already engineered sensor summaries, and no unaided raw-signal-understanding claim is justified.
