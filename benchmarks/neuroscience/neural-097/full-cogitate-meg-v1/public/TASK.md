# Single-trial MEG face/object generalization

Given the allowed training observations, predict the probability that one held-out observation belongs to the face category. Face is 1, object is 0. Each episode jointly withholds a participant and physical image identities. You receive one selected feature arm: early six-sensor MEG summaries (SIGNAL), matched pre-stimulus summaries (PRE), eight nuisance indicators (NUISANCE), or their two specified combinations. Every arm uses the same retained cases.

The task measures finite-panel performance of a prespecified bounded procedure. It is not a reproduction of the complete Nature paper, a source-localized result, a consciousness test or proof of native signal understanding. Original derivative preprocessing may use whole-epoch or whole-dataset adaptation; no online causal-decoding claim follows.

The primary report is B/F/SIGNAL balanced accuracy on Irrelevant trials, for each of three fixed seeds. Relevant non-target trials are reported separately. Missing required support produces a null full metric; no omitted participants or renormalized successful subset.
