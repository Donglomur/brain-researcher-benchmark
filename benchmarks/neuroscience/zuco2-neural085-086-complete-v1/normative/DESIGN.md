# ZuCo 2: two complete neural benchmark designs

This packet specifies the full scientific and participant API design for the existing NEURAL-085 and NEURAL-086 assignments. They share one original ZuCo 2 cohort. It is a design freeze for independent review, not an implementation, dataset acquisition, model result or readiness claim.

NEURAL-085 evaluates observed reading-condition prediction. Its primary metric is submitted NTGE method log loss on qualified exact matched sentence support, with equal weight for people, classes and items. Full-cohort and condition-exclusive results remain mandatory. Independent fixed-method feature comparisons and same-information method improvements are reported separately. A candidate cannot earn a better primary score by deliberately weakening its own control.

NEURAL-086 evaluates an executable scientific investigation of the signed paired log-loss contrast. A source-owned independent reference procedure defines the primary scientific comparison. Participants can submit different learners and analyses; their method-specific contrasts and predictive performance remain visible. The benchmark evaluates faithful numerical evidence, uncertainty, alternative explanations and interpretation rather than rewarding a positive EEG difference. Null, negative and inconclusive answers are legitimate. Six independently rated dimensions define the investigation score only after the rubric and evidence chain are qualified.

The full source scope is 18 people, one session per person, 36 NR/TSR storage containers, 14 alternating blocks, and 739 presentations per person. The condition order was the same for everyone. The paper reports 63 duplicate sentences; prior material conversion found 56 exact unique shared texts, 58 shared occurrences per condition and 59 broader normalization groups. Those numbers describe different objects and remain unresolved. The entire source join must be reconciled before a complete primary result. Neither count padding nor a convenient smaller cohort is allowed.

Figure 2 adds a consequential confound: the TSR relation question is visible on the sentence screen, while NR questions use a separate screen. Removing postresponse samples does not remove all instruction-related visual processing. The design therefore requires screen-region, gaze, ocular, missingness and chronology controls and keeps causal demand attribution disabled. Fixed order also means that conditioning on block eliminates within-block condition overlap.

Every output carries its actual information set. The default is offline case-local prediction: a sealed training state followed by the entire current case's unlabeled query batch. Both modes may perform local unsupervised query adaptation, which is recorded and charged. This does not imply point-local word inference, strict inductive evaluation or online availability. The native released summaries additionally inherit task-wide retrospective preprocessing.

A frozen programme and a case-adaptive agent receive equal information, tools and resource ceilings. The top-level method, controller, prompts and tool versions are frozen globally before any outer evaluation exposure. Each person/item-fold/context/arm job receives a fresh conversation and process. No mutable state, local method selection, generated code, history, feedback or provider cache may transfer across jobs. This is essential because one fold's held labels occur in another fold's training set. All predictive outputs are sealed before safe aggregate evidence is released for interpretation. Public-data or pretraining exposure is still an explicit limitation.

Four representation routes are separately specified: released summaries, spatial band summaries, cleaned waveforms and acquisition-stage raw waveforms. N085's spatial-band route is explicitly identical to its sentence-summary EEG representation, so it supplies no independent result. Raw-data field names are not proof of acquisition-stage samples. The source-native preprocessing, channel/reference units, sample clocks and every word-window ABI remain unqualified. No result with a band summary may be called native waveform understanding.

The future participant path is concrete: programme admission, source and support preparation, arbitrary submitted FIT, sealed state, permitted query INFER, full original process/resource collection, global protected scoring, safe evidence projection, executable investigation and independent rating. Trusted graders never import participant callbacks or deserialize participant executable state. Original outer and inner completion records, failed attempts, source identities and costs must join every accepted output. The proposed container launcher is a design requirement; its isolation has not been demonstrated.

The packet includes 32 scientific operators, 80 original word-band mean/difference roles, all 9 figures and 3 tables, 22 typed API objects, a 25-stage protected chain, 28 gates and 44 future independent control groups. All original predecessor source bodies and known failures are retained. The primary PDF, extracted text and figures remain local and excluded from source/publication selection because the paper-specific licence is CC-BY-NC.

Read SCIENTIFIC_CONTRACT.json for exact estimands and aggregation, REFERENCE_PROGRAMME.json for fixed procedures and controls, ACCESS_AND_CAPABILITY.json and CROSS_FOLD_EXPOSURE_POLICY.json for information access, and PROTECTED_EXECUTION_CHAIN.json for the complete execution and central API plan. Nothing in this design authorizes scientific or authentic-data execution.
