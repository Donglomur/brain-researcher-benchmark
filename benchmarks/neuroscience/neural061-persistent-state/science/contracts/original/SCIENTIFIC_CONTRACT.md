# Complete prospective scientific contract

## Question and inferential unit

The primary estimand is the difference in held-animal prediction loss between a common background plus a matched control population and the same background plus a selected region. Positive differences favor the selected region. This is conditional predictive information under the chosen decoder, matching policy and sampled co-recorded population. It is not necessity, a causal intervention, an anatomical connectivity estimate, or a statement that an unrecorded region contains no information.

Probes within a session are simultaneous observations. Sessions from the same animal are nested observations. Ten matched samples are Monte Carlo repetitions, not biological replications. Reduction is: equal timestamps within a window; equal windows within a session; equal matching seeds; equal eligible sessions within an animal; equal animals. The report retains all original session/seed dispositions, animal coverage and the unmatched support census. Comparisons between two anatomical regions with different animal support are not direct region rankings.

## Original facts and new choices

The upstream source is brainbench-org/ibl-bwb commit `6fbd666fe8170a2165f5198c1f490911424a654d`. Its `extract_spikes` puts actual `probe_id` and probe QC fields into each cluster row, concatenates probes, offsets and remaps spike unit indices, and keeps the resulting unit table in the same order. Unit IDs, probe-local cluster IDs, probe IDs, session ID and subject ID are checked as distinct axes. No probe is inferred from a filename or unit-ID prefix. The raw Data object is read before the benchmark loader adds namespaces. A same-open original producer must bind that raw object to the admitted release and whole cohort.

The full N058 source/evaluation animal split is retained: 423/29 sessions, 126/13 animals, five source-animal folds. Its seven additional sessions from evaluation animals remain outside all fitting, preprocessing and checkpoint lineage. The original body realizing that roster has not been accessed here. The later capture producer must authenticate it and independently establish every session/probe/animal join and original excluded-session census before data acquisition. A copied list with the right cardinalities is not that evidence.

The original TS1 vocabulary has eight tasks: choice, reward, stimulus contrast, whisker motion energy, wheel speed, right and left paw speed, and licking rate. N061 includes all eight and a separate ninth signed wheel-velocity endpoint. This is an explicit new benchmark design, not reproduction of the published TS1 score or Nature regional analysis. Canonical classification loss is multiclass log loss; continuous loss is squared error in the original physical units; licking uses Poisson negative log likelihood without the outcome-only factorial term. These unlike losses are never averaged across heads. Classification probabilities use a stated 1e-12 log floor; lick rates use the same numerical floor. Targets and raw probabilities are otherwise unchanged.

Choice/reward/contrast are original class indices, with two/two/five classes. Original masks and every target timestamp are retained. Paw-confidence masks are the original masks, not method-specific exclusions. Licking rates are converted to 20ms counts with the upstream round(rate/50) convention. Continuous targets preserve physical units; no whole-session normalization fit is inherited. Float64 readouts undergo the upstream float32 target transform before canonical float64 loss arithmetic. Wheel sign and units require explicit original extraction-lineage admission; no guessed conversion or sign flip is permitted.

The full source TS1 task-specific interval, domain and split intersections and TrialSampler emission are used with C=1. A clipped interval still refers to its original one-second task window. There is no extra context and no evaluation-animal behavioral history. All six regional arms use exactly the same target/mask/window axes. Whole-window neural observations make this retrospective decoding, not online causal prediction. Timing/exposure information is available even to ZERO and must be disclosed as a context baseline.

## Matching and biological support

The complete Beryl grey-matter ontology list and atlas version must be fixed from an admitted source before results. It is not selected by favorable decoding, observed test effects, or matching success. `void`, `root` and missing anatomy are recorded as ineligible, not reassigned. Every full session and original unit remains in the private census. All selected regions appear in the final report, including regions never recorded or lacking matching support.

Prospective unit budgets are 5, 10 and 20. Matching seeds are integers 0 through 9. All selected units have original neural and anatomical QC PASS, unit label exactly 1, and positive training-domain firing rate. This matches the available original categorical quality fields; it does not establish equality of every possible isolation statistic. Continuous quality metrics and source-field semantics remain an explicit future sensitivity/qualification requirement, not invented columns.

Firing rates are recomputed solely on original source pretrain_train domains or evaluation ts1_train neural domains. Evaluation training labels are never used or exported. Evaluation training neural activity is used only for this predeclared, task-independent matching census. No rate comes from the held-out query windows, target values, full-session stored `firing_rate`, or validation outcomes. The source all-unit build is required; a prior selected-units build cannot be relabeled all-units.

Strata require the same actual probe, original QC category, unit-quality label and fixed rate band with edges 0, .5, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 and 64 Hz. Each region unit is paired without replacement with two non-region units from that stratum, each within a 1.25 ratio of its training firing rate. One becomes the replacement control C and one the common background B. A deterministic, outcome-independent randomized greedy construction is specified in `matching.select`; it is not claimed to maximize available matching. Failure to obtain k triples remains an unavailable cell. No outcome-dependent relaxation or replacement sampling occurs. Actual rates, stratum counts, capacity, and residual balance are retained privately and published after closure.

Arms are B, BR, BC, R, C and ZERO. BR and BC contain exactly 2k units; R, C and B contain k. The primary replacement contrast is loss(BC)-loss(BR). Additional diagnostic contrasts are loss(B)-loss(BR), loss(C)-loss(R), and loss(ZERO)-loss(BR). Addition has unequal unit counts and is never substituted for the matched primary. Unit sets are physically projected before candidate delivery. Candidates never receive an all-unit array plus a mask instruction. ZERO has no spike events or unit metadata, but retains the same time/exposure support.

The anatomy-shuffle control permutes labels within the same probe/QC/rate stratum before rebuilding the whole sampling assignment. It preserves original neural observations and timestamps. It is a negative-control realization, not an exact exchangeability test or a substitute for an original temporal null. All shuffled assignments and failures remain in the report. An admitted native temporal-null producer remains an open validation gate; no arbitrary trial shuffle is claimed to preserve neural autocorrelation.

## Fitting, candidate work and reference

References are concrete source-only pooled-feature linear/logistic/Poisson models and 512-tree ExtraTrees models, with six fixed configurations. Original within-window spike rates are exposure-corrected, log1p transformed, pooled into per-bin mean and variance, with original bin exposure/availability and target-relative time. Unit columns are never aligned across animals. Source-only standardization and model selection use all five whole-animal source folds; validation losses are weighted by their actual validation animal counts. A final fit uses all matching source animals. Missing complete-fold support or a missing source class produces a declared scientific-unavailable cell. Convergence, library, memory, transport or input failures are execution failures and cannot remove unfavorable cells.

Each F panel authors a method using the full allowed source corpus before any evaluation-animal view. It then fits the frozen method separately to each scientific cell/arm and predicts all evaluation sessions. Each I panel authors separately for every current animal, scientific cell and neural arm, after viewing only that arm's physically projected source and current unlabeled query views. No state crosses those contexts. This separation is essential: viewing BR and BC together would allow BR query information to be encoded into BC's method. T0 has no task-trained reference tool. T1 receives only a fixed same-cell, same-arm source-trained reference tool. It does not receive held-out predictions or targets. Three repeats are independently scoped. F and I have equal prospective total panel ceilings; all I contexts share that same panel budget, not fresh per-cell or per-animal full budgets. The resulting large context count is explicit and requires a feasible full-campaign allocation before execution; no practical affordability claim is made by this source construction.

The current native adapter is not implemented or qualified. The source `production.collect` therefore defines the full concrete sequence and native API obligation rather than masquerading as a launchable central entry. Actual candidate source, imports and method execution occur only in a separately implemented isolated child; `worker.py` is that child-side algorithm. Every source edit, tool call, failed fit and prediction is charged. No hidden pretraining or source checkpoint can enter the strict reference.

## Scoring, inference and scientific reports

Every candidate panel and reference must have every expected cell/session/arm disposition before target capture. Missing or malformed predictions are failures, not matching exclusions. Predictions are ordered by exact opaque query identity and must include every position before applying the common original mask. The scorer never intersects IDs or silently removes nonfinite/empty support. Preparation's necessary target access is separately declared; the late grading boundary does not imply nobody previously handled targets.

The primary inferential family is the fixed reference, signed wheel velocity, budget10, real anatomy, matched replacement contrast, across every predeclared grey-matter region. Two-sided animal sign tests and Holm correction are specified. The sign test concerns a median directional effect, not the reported descriptive mean. It requires at least five matched animals, discards exactly zero differences from its binomial denominator, and retains a distribution-free median interval when available. Other cells receive a separate conservative full-family Holm correction and remain exploratory. All absent/unavailable cells retain p=1 in the family denominator while their raw p remains null. No successful region, method, budget or endpoint can be promoted to primary after results.

The small maximum13 evaluation animals limits power. In particular the minimum attainable two-sided sign probability and whole-region multiplicity must be assessed prospectively using the actual region roster; no source code can cure inadequate support. Lab dependence, selective anatomical sampling and matching eligibility limit population interpretation. Single-animal and fewer-than-five-animal evidence is descriptive. No causal, population-general or SOTA claim is accepted by this source package.

Candidate versus reference loss improvements are paired at the same seed/session/animal support and remain separated by readout and region. Reported numerical claims are checked against recomputed evidence with fixed tolerance. Inconclusive and null findings are valid. Empty reports and unsupported causal stories do not earn scientific-reasoning credit. A blinded human rubric for interpretation, assumptions, sensitivity, and limitation handling remains a required qualification artifact; numerical agreement alone is not a reasoning benchmark.
