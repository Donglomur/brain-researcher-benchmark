# Bounded scientific and file contract

Use the immutable exported PUBLIC_CONTRACT.json identity roster and fingerprint. A complete artifact uses schema `neural046-single-predictor-v1`, a printable predictor_id, status `complete`, and 2,795 rows `[asset_id, cue_event_row0, prediction]`. Every identity appears exactly once; row order may vary. Predictions are finite binary64 numbers, never booleans or null. A whole-abstention artifact has status `abstain`, a reason and no predictions. Unknown, duplicated or missing keys fail before the worker decodes its private reference. See the unchanged scientific `PUBLIC_SCHEMA.md` for exact limits and extra-member rules.

The fixed target is the time-weighted mean of stored dF/F samples in C=[cue,cue+0.5), minus the time-weighted mean in B=[cue-0.5,cue). Each integral is divided by its actual observed span, not inflated to nominal duration. A timestamp-only mask fixed 2,795 eligible slots from 2,797 before amplitude access. Stored dF/F units are retained without a percentage multiplier. MSE uses squared stored units; MAE and bias use stored units.

Every cue has equal weight within its source animal and every one of 13 animals has equal weight in the primary mean. Condition diagnostics average the animals in each fixed condition. No candidate-provided omission can shrink a denominator. Normalized MSE uses each held animal's frozen training-target scale; a zero scale gives a named null, while native errors remain defined. Finite predictions that cause unrepresentable aggregate errors receive explicit numerical status, never silent infinity or a fabricated finite reward.

The saved comparison models are constant, Z, Z+E, Z+N and Z+E+N, with lambda1, an unpenalized intercept and penalized nonintercept features. Training feature scales are intentionally unweighted, with an animal-equal training objective. Training-target normalization is weighted and uses only the other 12 source animals. These facts describe the frozen baselines; an arbitrary prediction artifact is not required or proven to implement them. Baseline-minus-candidate MSE is signed; roundoff-scale differences are not evidence of improvement.

Targets and all-fold training-label unions are exposed development material. No current artifact-only interface safely hides every target while simultaneously releasing every fold's labels. The public roster/methods may be distributed as task specifications subject to original rights; evaluator targets, prepared control answers and baseline prediction artifacts remain operator-only in the runtime package. Original source/data rights are not expanded by this local proposal.
