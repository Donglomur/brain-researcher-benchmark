Canonical question: **Does image novelty alter cell-type responses after locomotion, pupil, outcome and exposure history are matched?** This remains one canonical investigation, with two explicit obligations. 2. Separately predict and estimate the **observational same-image FIRST (Novel 1) minus REPEAT (Novel >1)** contrast in the declared secondary panel. Within-session prior presentation bins are a relaxed history condition, explicitly distinct from lifetime history.
