This is an offline, full-record seizure-detection experiment followed by an analysis-reconstruction task. The construction scope is all686 CHB-MIT paths and41 Siena recordings:727 recordings,23 CHB biological people and14 Siena people. chb01/chb21 belong to one CHB person. Official RECORDS confirms686=664 paths fromchb01–chb23 plus22 fromchb24; that filename census does not authenticate the local corpus, biological grouping, duplicate content or independent observed time. Q1 retains all queries for the logical job: a held CHB person's records or all14 Siena people. ## Four arms and their interpretation

`native` uses the documented valid measured EEG channels at their native rates, CHB256Hz and Siena512Hz. Siena's acquisition channel list must be joined to original source channels; an electrode-looking label alone does not admit a channel. Auxiliary and EKG channels are excluded.

`harmonized` applies per-channel affine calibration to physical microvolts first, then the fixed18 bipolar edges, fourth-order1–30Hz Butterworth SOS whole-record forward/backward filtering with odd padding, and Siena512→256 polyphase resampling with Kaiser5/constant padding. CHB receives the same physical band filtering. This bundles montage, channel count, filter and sampling changes; it is not an isolated sampling-rate effect and does not undo acquisition filters.

`normalized` adds whole-record channel median subtraction and division by the linear25th-to75th percentile IQR. It uses unlabeled statistics from the whole query recording, including future samples. Forward/backward filtering also uses future samples. This task is retrospective/transductive, not online detection or preictal prediction. Zero IQR, missing edges or nonfinite support invalidates the full comparison rather than excluding the inconvenient recordings.

The18 edges and explicitT3/T4/T5/T6 aliases are in control_recipe.py. M1 requires a unique measured forward or reverse CHB edge, never a guessed duplicate or graph-derived substitute. Siena requires unique endpoints, the release EEG whitelist and genuine common-reference evidence before subtraction. The trusted supervisor owns original source validation and scientific admission, then stages only the permitted phase inputs read-only. The candidate's physical reader checks exact staged bytes, channel geometry and calibration; it does not receive or reconstruct host authority. Its common-reference flag selects the declared computation and does not certify physical provenance. Actual host staging refuses while authentic adjudication is unavailable. Hashes and booleans cannot manufacture it.

`timing` is the exact source-audited reference: training-person-balanced, Laplace-smoothed ictal prevalence by elapsed minute0–58/59-and-later. It receives no waveform, original clock, filename, clinical metadata or query labels. The case still contains permitted opaque IDs, cohort and durations; arbitrary code using those fields would be a broader metadata-only control. The actual program classifier therefore requires exact audited timing code. If all three signal arms also use the exact reference source, their comparison is the controlled reference preprocessing comparison. Otherwise the result is a bundled candidate-method comparison; independent program hashes do not establish the same detector family or compliance with the named transforms.

The provided signal reference uses median-channel log line length and the median over training people of their nonictal99.5th-percentile feature threshold. Each arm fits its own threshold. Its implementation has not been empirically run or qualified in this construction.

## Two distinct outcomes

The detector outcome reports the prespecified primary endpoint, equal-person mean Siena eventF1, for every arm. Secondary endpoints are event sensitivity and false alarms per24 observed hours. Combine each person's counts across all their recordings before computing their metric. Retain Siena paired harmonized−native, normalized−harmonized and harmonized−timing contrasts and all participant/condition support, regardless of sign. No best-arm selection after target release.

Event rules remain fixed: one score per recorded second; threshold0.5; merge gaps strictly below90s; split events above300s; reference tolerance30s before/60s after; many-to-many overlap. F1=2TP/(R+TP+FP), sensitivity=TP/R and false alarms=86400FP/observed seconds. Ictal observed time remains in the denominator; inter-file gaps are excluded. Tolerance does not resolve clock errors.

The reconstruction task receives the complete analysis packet and returns submission.json with schema_version1, task_idNEURAL-012, input_sha256 and report. Reconstruct every person/arm metric, cohort equal-person summaries, target paired contrasts, unpaired cohort differences and all Siena false-alarm intervals including empty lists. The grade is maximum scaled reconstruction error, scale=max(1,abs(expected)), with tolerance1e-8. This grade measures analysis accuracy, not detector competence or frontier improvement. The scorer publishes reconstruction and detector outcomes separately.

Descriptive person-level SE uses sqrt(sum((x−mean)^2)/(n(n−1))). The cohort-difference formula combines the two SE values by their Euclidean norm; its field is descriptive_standard_error. The CHB folds share training people, Siena predictions share a fitted model, and CHB audit fits use22 people whereas Siena fits use23. These summaries do not establish independent training replicates, causal transfer, clinical utility or population-wide uncertainty. Null/reversed outcomes are valid. Native signal understanding and SOTA require additional evidence beyond either accurate reconstruction or this unqualified comparator.
