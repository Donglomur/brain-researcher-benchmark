# Complete original-source annotation census

The authenticated [DOSE-I v2 source](https://zenodo.org/records/22145442) has now been streamed through a complete annotation/time census: **171 recordings, 70,760,492 source rows**. All members reached EOF with their complete expanded bytes, CRC and SHA256 recorded. The saved outputs and their bindings were independently reviewed after a successful run.

The census found **7,345 nonempty MOAAS cells and 1,132 nonempty SOC cells**, matching the release metadata. These are lexical observations, not an admitted assessment or transition denominator. The inherited scorer's 7,328/1,129 counts remain discrepant by +17/+3; no rows were removed to make them agree. The complete protected annotation ledger has 13,174 rows, all with nonempty EVENT; the remaining marker grammar and biological-person identity crosswalk are still unresolved.

Recordings 41 and 43 each contain one duplicate timestamp. The other 169 report contiguous 4 ms table times. Census completeness did not depend on clock or EEG validity: duplicates were retained, with no timestamp repair, resampling or interpolation.

`census-verification.json` records the source, completed scope, exact aggregate observations and review pins. Raw source and private annotation ledgers remain outside this repository. The preceding `source-archive-evidence-v1` receipt is unchanged historical evidence of archive/header inspection; this successor adds complete annotation census evidence.

The run took 443.258 seconds and retained 5,014,503 bytes of native outputs. These are preparation costs, not AI benchmark results. EEG numeric validation, source-aligned 30-second packets, person grouping, semantic assessment eligibility and canonical scorer activation remain unfinished. The adopted retrospective marker-relative condition does not imply that a marker is a verified challenge onset.
