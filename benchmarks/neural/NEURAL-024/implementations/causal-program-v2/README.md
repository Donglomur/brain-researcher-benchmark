# Complete causal count forecasting programme

This component recovers NEURAL-024's original persistent candidate controller,
trusted preparer and embedded private grader into the current repository layout.
It closes a source-publication gap. It does not create a new controller, new
dataset, independent biological case or newly qualified runtime.

The complete endpoint is the exposed Indy 2016-10-05 to 2016-10-06 development
transfer: 18,700 source bins, 3,000 target-day calibration bins, then all 22,100
causal forecast calls across 96 M1 electrode channels. The first ten calls are
required warm-up. All 22,090 remaining rows, or 2,120,640 channel-bin cells, are
scored. Each reply contains 96 strictly positive finite expected **counts per
20 ms bin**. The complete vector is written, flushed and fsynced before the
controller reads the current observation for later release. A persistent program
may update from the legally revealed prefix. A source freeze does not freeze its
in-memory weights or establish its fitting policy.

This is an agent-built programme route over processed neural counts. No direct
or viewer-mediated interpretation result is supplied. One monkey/day pair and
many dependent bins do not support population inference or causal circuit claims.
The entire original source corpus has been publicly exposed. In particular,
NEURAL-023's public assessment packet contains these future counts. It must never
be mounted here, and prior exposure cannot be erased by later isolation.

## Current metric and separate scorer interfaces

The recovered original `causal-science-v1/grade.py` and `score_core.py` implement
the **current** finite-task metric: full Poisson log-likelihood, including
log-factorials, minus the immutable calibration-rate reference log-likelihood,
divided by total observed spikes and ln(2). The ten warm-up rows are excluded.
The original scorer keeps all six signed comparisons, mean NLL and per-channel
descriptions. Quiet channels remain; zero spike denominators are undefined;
negative gains are valid; arithmetic failure receives no clipped or partial score.
An independent scalar audit checks the candidate likelihood.

The six closed historical reference **score records** are matched to the complete
support, channel order and observed spike totals. They remain externally supplied
operator evidence. Their historical evaluation is not rerun or requalified by
this source recovery. The six arms are source_rate, calibration_rate,
self_history_frozen, self_history_adapted, self_population_history_frozen and
self_population_history_adapted. Adaptation/compute comparisons are descriptive
until policies and resources are matched.

The separately published `complete-count-scorer-v1` is an offline interchange
requiring six reference **mean vectors for every round** and accumulating direct
likelihood contrasts. Its mathematical objective aligns when identical references
and support are independently established, but its inputs, accumulation and
causal provenance are different. Closed aggregate score records cannot recover
per-round means. This recovery does not supply or invent those missing means,
invoke that alternate scorer, or establish numerical interchangeability.
