# Delivery and validation scope

The deterministic descriptive calculation has completed one authentic preparation and one independent arithmetic verification. [VALIDATION.json](VALIDATION.json) records this stage and its source, data and receipt commitments. This adds execution evidence after the initial source-only implementation; it does not mark the full benchmark complete.

The verified packet contains 204 trials, the original 150 training / 54 validation split, 54 source units and 20 Set–Go phase bins across six recording segments. All 48 split × segment × effector × direction comparison groups remain present: 27 contain both priors and 21 are explicitly unsupported. Unsupported comparisons keep null numerical results and any available single-prior means. These groups belong to one benchmark condition.

The selected outcome is fixed by [ROOT_SELECTION.json](ROOT_SELECTION.json) and [CONTRACT.json](CONTRACT.json). Each trial's rate uses its own actual bin exposure; prior means give each trial equal weight. For each supported group, long-minus-short differences yield four signed quarter means and four quarter RMS values over all 54 units. No positive effect is required. These are new descriptive benchmark summaries, not a reproduction of an original paper metric. The eight values do not identify complete unit-level trajectory geometry, and RMS can be positive under noise.

An independent standard-library parser and arithmetic implementation checked available prior means, full signed differences, the eight summaries, support, the common display maximum, matched observations and the expected-answer structure. Its 182,954 scalar comparisons include repeated reference, observation and answer representations; they are not independent samples or additional tasks. The maximum difference was 2.842170943040401 × 10⁻¹⁴ Hz under the frozen rule `abs(a-b) <= max(1e-12 Hz, 1e-12 * max(abs(a), abs(b)))`. Metadata, support and nulls required exact agreement. This rule checks implementation arithmetic; it is not a participant tolerance or significance threshold.

Successful programmed arithmetic alone does not establish learned or native neural understanding. This condition asks whether a participant can calculate or interpret declared descriptive quantities and qualify their support. One exposed animal/session and six recording segments do not establish population replication or randomized blocks. Inferential uncertainty remains `not_qualified`; no confidence interval, causal conclusion or prospective timing prediction follows from these response-conditioned observations.

The matched viewer and programme conditions are designed to receive the same prior-specific 54×20 means and support. The SVG renderer uses 256 colour intensities and finite visual resolution; numerical programmes retain numeric precision. The same continuous scoring contract applies, without an extra viewer tolerance. A measured gap would include representation precision as well as access to computation. Individual-trial access would be a separate information condition.

[VALIDATION.json](VALIDATION.json) remains the historical preparation/arithmetic receipt; that phase rendered no authentic SVGs. [DELIVERY_VALIDATION.json](DELIVERY_VALIDATION.json) records a subsequent accepted local delivery exercise: 48 SVG documents and typed-view checks, nine fixed grader controls and three typed-selector refusals. The grader exercise produced six accepted grade records and three intended refusals. These are engineered control outcomes, not AI attempts or evidence of task difficulty.

The complete operator exercise contains 70 files and 9,887,828 bytes. Its 51-file observation bundle contains both numeric programme data and SVG views; it is not a wholesale native-participant payload. Native participants must receive only a separately enforced typed visual interface. Private references, expected answers, control submissions and grade payloads remain operator-owned.

The initial operator checker failed because its expected metadata omitted a 48-cell layout-check key. That checker was corrected, without rerunning the native delivery/grader operation. The corrected postflight and independent saved-result review accepted the receipts, identities, inventory, permissions and hashes. The independent review did not rerender or regrade the saved artifacts.

This acceptance covers local SVG/package/API and fixed grader-control integrity. Raster conversion, provider image delivery, participant isolation, statistical inference and AI performance remain unqualified. No model was fitted and no participant was exposed. SVG generation and typed selection alone do not establish learned neural understanding or a pure tool-use effect.

The earlier arithmetic audit authenticated the saved packet and decoded six arithmetic operands. Raw spike-time and offset members were checked at the header level; their values were not decoded in that audit. It adds no fresh original-NWB spike recount or independent recovery of the fourteen extra unit identities. Operator reference vectors and expected answers remain outside participant delivery and are not published here. The public hashes support evidence identification; access to the retained evidence and its review is still needed to assess the underlying execution.
