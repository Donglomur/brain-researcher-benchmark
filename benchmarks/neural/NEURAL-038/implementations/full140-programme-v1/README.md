## Concrete observed-exit and immutable-report connection

The original controller freezes one UTF-8 source, prepares the full public
view, executes the candidate in the reviewed isolated profile, removes the
producer before trusted output capture, retires the view and performs structural
admission. Its GRADE_HANDOFF.json is deliberately unauthorized and names a
historical saved-reference command. **This route never executes that command.**

The new caller runs and observes the original observer process itself. Natural
zero exit, reaping, closed streams, affirmative process-group absence, no timeout
or kill, and clean timer custody are required. A retained COMPLETE or external
receipt alone cannot establish that exit. An outer failure preserves partial
evidence and may require independent recovery of nested Docker CLI groups and
native resources; closing one process group does not prove their cleanup.

After that actual exit, a separately authorized numerical worker verifies the
same source, selection, authoring authority and caller proof. It calls the
unchanged `observe.admit`, rechecking the complete public-view/retirement,
program commitment, both native-phase closures and original report pins.
The exact candidate-report read is intercepted only at its fixed path and
expected stopped-report pin. That captured buffer is decoded once and supplied
to `evaluate(root, panel, inputs, submission)` without rereading the report path.
A valid replacement after capture cannot silently become the scored report.

The numerical worker binds panel/input metadata and every array descriptor to
the same complete candidate-visible export. The unchanged evaluator admits the
complete submitted structure before reading the 18 public arrays and recomputing
all numerical values. It does not use a saved private answer. The numerical
child must itself exit naturally and publish a matching score before the caller
writes its final receipt. The caller's final flush and exit are also part of the
external supervision contract. Partial files after failure are retained and
never authorize automatic continuation or retry.
