## Decoder source and empirical questions

decoder.py has no filesystem, HTTP, NumPy or pickle operation. It accepts byte strings supplied by a future selected owner and returns small descriptions, never event labels or repaired signals. It checks:

- NPY magic/version, bounded literal header, exact unique header fields, one-dimensional nonempty shape, explicit little/big-endian float32/float64 dtype, exact data extent, finite values and exact declared frame count.
- Strictly increasing native time vectors, while preserving each represented scalar through hexadecimal descriptions. Difference summaries are numerical diagnostics, not a reconstructed clock or a definition of physical support.
- Exactly three unique raw NPZ names time.npy, voltage.npy and stimulus.npy; supported flags/compression, single-disk terminal EOCD and complete central extent, exact local/central names/versions/flags/times/methods/CRC/sizes, local ZIP64 and descriptor checks, no prefix/trailing/inter-record junk, bounded raw-DEFLATE with complete EOF/consumption and CRC, and equal nontrivial channel lengths. Central ZIP64 and nonregular entries are outside this explicitly bounded profile.
- A 12 MiB per-inner-file and 32 MiB total-inner-NPZ ceiling before reading members. Arrays are read serially and discarded after QC. These are prospective refusal limits, not observed formats or an assertion that every deposited payload will pass.

The supported formats intentionally remain explicit. An object array, integer channel, unexpected shape, extra member, unexpected container structure or exceeded bound is an empirical refusal, not permission to widen the parser during an acquisition. Preserve its authenticated original and report the unresolved format. No complete-family success can be claimed if even one required member remains unqualified.

Headers and native values must establish actual shape/dtype/order/count, sample spacing, first/last timestamps and gaps, agreement of channel lengths and coordinate correspondence to the companion metadata. Compare array endpoints and rate distributions with metadata as diagnostics. Do not demand exact equality to a nominal rate or infer a missing endpoint convention from the 29.999995-second duration. Never cast native times to rounded ticks as an implicit repair.

The current descriptors do not identify all internal gaps, quantify rail saturation, determine valid biological support or adjudicate recording quality. A future reference-production stage must inspect complete native time/voltage/stimulus arrays and preserve acquisition provenance; no claim that these descriptors alone establish usable references is made.

## Prospective electrical reference protocol

Available sources do not provide a recoverable electrical reference-event rule. The proposed new reference is therefore an explicitly prospective electrical AP-peak annotation, not paper-author ground truth or replay. The current decoder implements none of it.

1. Preserve each original sweep, native timestamps and all three channels. Do not concatenate across gaps or reapply voltage/LJP/bridge corrections. Establish support from actual samples and electrical-only quality adjudication, independently of optical outputs. Any disagreement with declared sweep timing is logged and resolved from provenance rather than fitted alignment.
2. For reviewer assistance only, create the union of positive derivative-threshold candidates at 10, 20 and 30 mV/ms using actual adjacent time differences, only within qualified connected support. Never differentiate across a within-sweep or between-sweep gap. Within 3 ms after each upward threshold crossing, suggest the earliest native sample attaining the largest local voltage; an incomplete search window is flagged for review. This is a proposed candidate cue, not an AP definition, sensitivity guarantee, smoothing filter or reference implementation.
3. Two electrical-only reviewers inspect complete sweeps, including detector-negative intervals, without seeing optical traces, optical predictions, model labels or candidate performance. They identify biological APs and nominate native voltage-peak sample indices. Disagreements and ambiguous plateaus/artifacts require adjudication; never silently select by optical correlation. No arbitrary refractory suppression is applied to the adjudicated primary event list.
4. Use injected-current changes as a cue for reviewing stimulation artifacts, not a rule that removes every stimulated interval or genuine evoked spike. Preserve neutralization and acquisition-filter/export uncertainties as QC strata. Predefine technical exclusion criteria and retain every affected movie and its coverage accounting. If reference/support ambiguity cannot be resolved, preparation is incomplete; do not silently drop a difficult case.
5. Freeze the full primary peak-index and valid-support annotations before optical predictions are generated or exposed. Store exact native sample times and provenance. Matching is the already proposed inclusive5ms one-to-one native-frame rule; prediction count remains at most one per eligible optical frame. Do not move electrical peaks onto optical frames.
6. Predeclare sensitivity comparisons for candidate thresholds, electrical landmark (onset versus peak), and automated refractory proposals (0.5/1/2 ms), with label disagreements and coverage effects reported separately. They cannot replace the adjudicated primary reference or be selected for favorable optical scores. Keep every change versioned; do not retroactively call a new reference the original endpoint.

The candidate thresholds/windows are benchmark-author proposals requiring source implementation and electrical validation. They are not recovered paper parameters. A complete blinded annotation protocol and deterministic indexing/adjudication format must be frozen before authentic reference production. The competent optical MAD and matched-filter comparators remain separately trained/calibrated within the accepted six-animal scheme; electrical test traces, stimulus and reference-derived validity remain evaluator-private. No comparator run or improvement is established here.
