# Full32 source status and historical correction

This component prepares a separate condition within NEURAL-077 for 32 ERP CORE participants and 6,400 stimulus events. The existing 400-query condition from the original two development participants remains unchanged. The full32 condition uses the same four labeled demonstrations and preserves every query's complete two-channel, 1,024-sample waveform. It adds no benchmark registration or measured AI result.

At this component's initial source freeze on 2026-11-19, metadata admission authenticated all 96 SET, EEG-JSON and event-table objects (30,799,299 bytes). They describe 200 stimulus events per participant: 40 targets and 160 standards, totaling 1,280 targets and 5,120 standards. All 32 FDT objects (1,639,993,344 bytes) were then acquired and independently checked against their expected hashes. Those initial material-identity records alone did not establish waveform validity, a completed full32 packet build, model performance or scientific readiness. Later that day, the [accepted materialization](../../docs/full32-materialization-20261119.md) completed the 32-person, 6,400-query packet: 72 files, 236,692,236 bytes, 1,056,000 scalar construction checks and one complete saved-material review. The [subsequent numerical-delivery record](../../docs/full32-numerical-delivery-20261119.md) reports two fixed non-learning canaries with zero model/provider calls. Neither result establishes full 6,400-query delivery or visual/provider qualification.

The task classifies target versus standard event codes; it does not label the physiological presence of a P3. The paper's averaged-P3 exclusions and correct-response bins are not imported into this all-stimulus condition. Epochs use released event-marker coordinates at 1,024 Hz, from 256 samples before the event through 767 samples after it. Available metadata do not establish whether the physical 26 ms display-delay correction was already applied. The source recipe adds no timing shift, filter or trial rejection. See the [ERP CORE paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7909723/) and [pinned official target/standard bins](https://github.com/lucklab/ERP_CORE/blob/c18b43d70d791ca914d90410afe4ff06d6f7f429/P3/EEG_ERP_Processing/BDF_P3.txt).

The historical description of the development pair as one F and one M is inconsistent with the provider metadata. Both selected records, sub-007 and sub-010, are marked F in the pinned participants table. Their IDs, selection history, files and outcomes are unchanged. This corrects the descriptive claim only. The [provider participants table](https://data.nemar.org/nm000132/v1.0.0/participants.tsv) was authenticated at 792 bytes, SHA-256 `047b681e6f6434403629c85fc04f9ceeb61a30a5e46ad80ca6d93df7ab1bf67b`.

The builder sorts the staging manifest's file map by opaque key to remove its prior insertion-order association with participants. Per-query delivery excludes that manifest and source grouping. Sorting alone does not qualify isolation, unpredictability of an operator's private key, or absence of public-data contamination. A capable backend must receive all four complete demonstrations plus the complete query; truncating waveforms or reducing them to features changes this condition.

The evaluator reports participant-macro balanced accuracy and log loss, plus paired per-participant differences. The separate [paired-participant bootstrap sidecar](../full32-paired-bootstrap-v1/README.md) implements the declared 10,000-resample calculation with an explicit versioned interval policy; no empirical interval or reference-grade comparison has been produced. No interval is inferred by treating 6,400 trials as independent participants. The additive [portable runtime successor](../full32-paired-runtime-v1/README.md) provides separately controlled delivery source. The retained two-canary operation qualifies only its stated episodes and probes; full provider/sandbox qualification and completed visual delivery remain outside this component's accepted source preparation.
