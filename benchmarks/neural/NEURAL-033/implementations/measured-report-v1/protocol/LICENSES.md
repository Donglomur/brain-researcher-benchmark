Wright et al. (2025), *Distinct synaptic plasticity rules operate across dendritic compartments in vivo during learning*, Science, DOI [10.1126/science.ads4706](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.ads4706). Data/code release: [Zenodo 15047046](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15047046). Retained primary release metadata declares CC BY 4.0; preserve attribution and this modification notice when sharing derived observations.

The benchmark extracts selected primitive fields from all released Early control and Kir dictionaries, encodes nonfinite numbers as null, and adds stable row IDs/cohort metadata. Its rank-based protocol and software are newly authored; they are not the article's original analysis. The article-cited restricted version 14549129 and unavailable supplemental Methods are not silently substituted.

The complete 2.1 GB archive's advertised checksum was not verified by selected byte-range intake. Individual selected members have recorded ranges, CRCs and local SHA256 hashes. These checks establish which local bytes were used, not verification of the unacquired whole archive.

The reconstruction helper uses inflate64 1.0.4 only during isolated original-data preparation. Its binary wheel is not bundled here; consult its license when acquiring it. Normal task preparation and scoring use the supplied primitive JSON and do not need pickle decoding.
