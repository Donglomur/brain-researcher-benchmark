# Matched support, inference and missingness

## Proposed complete-support successor rule

This is the proposed correction of the independent peer's N061-W02, to be implemented only in a distinct future source successor. Frozen n061/grading.py is unchanged.

Construct an expected key census from the admitted full source-owned cohort/ontology and the prospectively selected study policy, before targets. A primary regional session key is (head,region,k,realization,session), with exactly ten required matching-seed keys and each selected contrast's complete required arms. Reference/candidate outputs must bind the same original target axes, masks, windows, assignments and biological identities.

A session contributes to the primary estimate only when **all ten** expected seed dispositions are complete for every comparison operand. An explicit matching/head/source-support scientific-unavailable disposition makes that entire session unavailable for this estimator, retaining the exact seed reasons. A missing key, malformed output, failed fit, convergence failure, unobserved exit, budget overrun or uncertain capture is a production failure, not a scientific-unavailable exclusion. Incomplete execution blocks a complete-primary verdict; its expected rows and costs remain.

For candidate–reference competence, derive the same complete-seed support once from the common comparison record. Do not skip individual unavailable records and average the remainder, as frozen grading.py currently does. Every eligible session has ten paired candidate/reference losses and identical support; reduce equal seeds, equal sessions within animal, equal animals. Export the competence coverage table, included animal IDs, planned13-animal census, per-session dispositions, required and completed seed counts, and exact absent/failure causes.

For F/I or T0/T1 comparisons, use a single predeclared matched-support object across all compared modes/tool conditions as well as the required reference operands. Do not compute two separately selected animal means then subtract them. A stronger matched-comparison domain may be smaller because of declared scientific support, but every change and exclusion remains explicit and cannot be outcome-driven. Candidate execution failures cannot improve a score by removing a difficult session.

The proposed policy preserves original all-arm primary reference audit eligibility. Primary agent BR/BC outputs require a new exact two-arm typed graph; they cannot be supplied to the old all-six-arm scorer by padding, copying, empty rows or aliases. Optional addition/region-only/neural-information contrasts are scored only when their actual required arms were prospectively selected and produced. Planned-not-selected arms are not matching-unavailable biological observations.

## Biological and sampling units

The full planned evaluation cohort is13 animals and29 nested sessions. Probes/units/windows are nested observations; ten matching seeds are Monte Carlo repeated populations. They never enlarge biological n. Keep the original order of averaging and report per-animal estimates and their session/seed coverage. The candidate versus reference BR-loss contrast is paired on exactly the same support, not merely on equal-shaped arrays.

Region r has an actual eligible animal count n_r≤13, currently unknown. Region-specific matching can change its population support. Therefore differences between two regional mean estimates with different contributing animals are not intrinsic anatomical rankings. The source-fixed complete grey-matter ontology defines R before outcome access. Absent anatomy or insufficient matching stays in the complete region table; no favorable-coverage subset silently becomes the primary family.

## Power and interpretation gate

The existing two-sided sign test concerns the median directional effect across eligible animals. The mean effect is descriptive. Zeros leave the sign-test nonzero denominator but remain reported; no significance inference can count seeds, sessions or regions as additional animals. Marginal median intervals are not simultaneous all-region confidence bands.

With n_nonzero nonzero animal differences, the smallest attainable two-sided sign p is2^(1−n_nonzero). A necessary best-case condition for a first Holm rejection at familywise alpha is2^(1−n_nonzero)≤alpha/R. At the source maximum13 animals andalpha=.05, the full region family must have at most204 members for even an all-concordant result to clear that first step. At205 or more, no such first rejection is possible. This is symbolic statistical resolution, not a computed data result. Fewer eligible animals or zero differences make the bound worse. The source threshold of at leastfive animals is a descriptive/inference policy gate, not assurance of useful power.

R and every n_r require later actual source-bound atlas/matching qualification. Do not shrink the region family, recruit pseudo-replicates, select a different test or change the primary endpoint after outcomes to evade this limitation. If inferential resolution is insufficient, preserve the reference region tables as an explicitly descriptive finite-cohort study or design a separately reviewed larger biological study. No data expansion is authorized here.

Laboratory dependence, selective recordings, residual quality/rate/anatomy differences, public historical exposure and conditioning on fitted methods remain limitations. No causal, population-general, native-understanding or SOTA claim follows from these source-level plans.
