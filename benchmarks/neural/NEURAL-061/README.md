## Current neural-understanding objective

Measured signed wheel velocity supplies the participant known answer. The operator also estimates the regional contrast loss(BC)-loss(BR) under outcome-independent matching; its sign is not a preordained answer, and no participant interpretation-accuracy scorer is delivered.

**Source delivery:** partial\_delivery. Current source selects the complete A0 frozen-program slice: signed velocity, k=10, real anatomy, all regions/ten seeds/423 source sessions/29 evaluation sessions. It implements persistent participant state and complete-support scoring but retains authentic preparation, publication and late-target holds. Adaptive I, trained-tool T1 and the broader multi-head sensitivity catalogue are prospectively unselected.
