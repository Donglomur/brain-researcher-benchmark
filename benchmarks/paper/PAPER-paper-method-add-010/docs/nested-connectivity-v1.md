# Nested connectivity evaluator

The [implementation guide](../implementations/nested-connectivity-v1/README.md)
documents a usable conditional 246-feature evaluator, exact label boundaries,
generated controls and remaining scientific requirements. The
[prospective design proposal](../implementations/nested-connectivity-v1/PROSPECTIVE_DESIGN.json)
specifies an endpoint, metric, estimator and outcome-blind 10-outer/5-inner fold
recipe. [Proposed folds](../implementations/nested-connectivity-v1/PROSPECTIVE_FOLDS.json)
use the exact 94-identifier published roster; they do not establish authentic
feature or biological identity.

This component starts from label-independent computed connectivity features.
The released author route uses known-category averages; authentic prospective
features and regional-time-series extraction agreement remain unverified. The
historical 246-feature domain also incorporates full-cohort QC. Conditioning on
that domain does not establish fully nested eligibility or globally unseen data.

Matched nuisance inputs and comparison definitions remain missing, as does the
corrected-supplement version/hash join. The component therefore does not complete
the canonical connectivity-versus-nuisance comparison, reproduce the paper, or
establish a scientific improvement. All source outputs retain those limits.
