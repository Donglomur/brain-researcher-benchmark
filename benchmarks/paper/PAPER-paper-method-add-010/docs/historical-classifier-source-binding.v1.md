This creates a reproducible new ordering; the historical filesystem-glob order remains unknown.

The 188 face/object endpoint values do not establish the complete input bank for source-faithful QC. The author first selects the measure, directional ROI pair, relevance and presentation. It computes missingness, modal frequency and sample standard deviation across that entire bank, imputes only when missingness is strictly between zero and 10%, applies the original rejection conditions, and only then restricts to the stimulus pair. No additional duration or Data_Type predicate appears in this route. The exact 10% boundary and pre-imputation diagnostics must remain explicit. A new collector must preserve all matching categories and rows, original identifiers and order, and refuse missing participant support rather than omit it.

For the named endpoint, the measure is `bary-sq_dtw_max`, relevance is `Irrelevant`, and presentation is `on`. Category_Selective→Prefrontal_Cortex is the primary directional view; the reverse orientation is retained as a paired report, not an independent sample. The source-authored scaler's transformation uses unlabeled held-out batch ranges. A source-faithful baseline preserves and discloses that access, with identical test-batch access for reference and candidate arms. A training-fitted-range variant is a separately named comparator.

Before any fit, the complete bank, parser behavior, runtime, fresh fold membership and full participant ledger must be qualified and pinned. The source summary statistic is the equal mean of ten fold accuracies; its population standard deviation is descriptive fold dispersion, not a confidence interval. Neither fold maps nor predictions have been generated by this source inquiry.
