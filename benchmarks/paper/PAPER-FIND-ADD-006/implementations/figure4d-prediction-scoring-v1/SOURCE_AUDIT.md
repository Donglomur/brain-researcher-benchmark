# Source findings for the original Figure 4D endpoint

Inspected sources are the already cached paper text and the authors' repository at commit `95b94942608b2f3e3f7389aeed724459ab3e679e`; their exact body hashes are in `SOURCE_PINS.json`. No recording archive, historical private reference, saved solver prediction or hidden answer was opened. Findings below concern source text/code, not results from a native execution.

The [paper](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2417025122), Figure 4B,D and Methods “Global and Conditional Decoding,” specifies 25 sessions, 100 stratified 70/30 splits, replacement resampling within the resulting partitions, matched draws for global/conditional/shuffled conditions, and equal averaging over conditional groups. Figure 4D compares context conditional on position against global decoding and context/position shuffle controls. The global shuffled-context comparison from Figure 4B is also retained by the panel scorer.

For two context labels, normalized absolute difference is exactly the mismatch indicator. Its average is error; its complement is exact-match accuracy. This identity resolves how to calculate both quantities from prediction exports. It does not resolve the paper's inconsistent naming or establish the plotted numeric baseline. The component therefore exposes both quantities without an improvement direction or pass threshold.

The scorer does not modify these frozen author sources or implement a different decoder. Source-faithful execution still requires a reviewed repair, source speed-equalized preparation, explicit condition support and a draw ledger. A documented regenerated draw protocol can preserve the original task; missing historical seeds alone do not require a different train/test scheme.
