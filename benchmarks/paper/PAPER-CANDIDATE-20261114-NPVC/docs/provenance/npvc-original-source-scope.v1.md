This helper accepts two condition-specific data matrices and returns decoded information and classification accuracy. It constructs binary condition labels, partitions supplied matrix rows when cross-validation is requested, marginalizes over a 1,000-point response grid, and compares conditional likelihoods between the two conditions. Its signature accepts a fold count; neither that argument nor row partitioning authenticates the biological unit or fold assignment used for the paper's empirical analysis.

Consequently, this helper does not establish the selected Figure 2 neural-response FDE caller or recover its fitted operands.
