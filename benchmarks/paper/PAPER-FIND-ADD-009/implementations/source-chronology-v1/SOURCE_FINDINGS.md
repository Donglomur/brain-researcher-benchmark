# Source findings and integration proposal

The previously unbound [published Supplementary Table 1](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41586-025-09127-3/MediaObjects/41586_2025_9127_MOESM1_ESM.pdf) has been recovered and visually inspected. Its evaluation days are **25, 109, 110, 125, 139, 165, 172, 174, 179, 181, 186, 188, 193 and 195**. The complete numeric table is preserved in the generated schedule, including cumulative training sentences and hours. This identifies training chronology facts that were missing from the implementation; it does not identify which table rows are the predetermined blue sessions in Fig. 2b.

The table reports day195 evaluation with the most recent decoder trained on day193, using 8,315 sentences and 37.8 hours. The [pinned author demo configuration](https://github.com/Neuroprosthetics-Lab/brain-to-voice-2025/blob/cff4875decd35ff0f98a944035377a25b43ba79c/configs/config.yaml) instead declares 8,184 total trials and a `brain2voice_t15_day193.tf` model name. The difference is **131**, but its cause is unresolved: it is not evidence of 131 specific excluded or missing trials. The [demo session configuration](https://github.com/Neuroprosthetics-Lab/brain-to-voice-2025/blob/cff4875decd35ff0f98a944035377a25b43ba79c/configs/training_data_args.yaml) has 36 training sessions through day193 and day195 held out. Sharing a last day does not establish equality of the original and demo training populations.

There is a literal source discrepancy: the row for evaluation day139 gives training day132 and a reported interval of five days, although subtraction gives seven. Both values are retained, with a discrepancy flag. No date is repaired by assumption. The reported table intervals range from zero days for the first-session demonstration to twelve days for day165. A universal seven-day exclusion rule cannot be derived from this table or substituted for original trial memberships. In particular, the column labels specify the **decoder training date**, not the final acquisition date of every training input.

The existing `initial-evaluator-v1` supports a single day195 or prospective session holdout, one decoder per condition and global train/test session exclusion. That remains useful within its declared scope, but it cannot represent the full original sequence of session-specific decoder histories. The correct next implementation depends on source trial membership and original model identity, not simply replacing its test-session list with these fourteen dates. Earlier evaluation recordings may later become eligible training data only where the authentic original decoder provenance establishes that use.

The canonical task and improvement contract are not edited in this task-local component. Integration should cite the recovered table in their chronology evidence, replace the claim that Supplementary Table 1 itself is unavailable, and retain the unresolved **956-trial identity, original training memberships and per-decoder artifact mapping**. Exact cutoffs remain unresolved; neither a blanket seven-day cutoff nor treating every table session as the primary cohort is supported.

Both reproduction and improvement retain the original T15 participant, causal neural observations, acoustic waveform/40-Mel-band metric, trial aggregation, and the original comparison conditions. Reproduction should have its own outcome under full source fidelity; improvement should have a separate source-matched comparison and uncertainty decision. Paired paper-plus-data and paper-plus-data-plus-author-code conditions remain future participant delivery work. None is admitted by this source report.

The original 956 vocalized trials cannot be replaced by the 58 mimed trials, the day195 example, or an arbitrary subset. The 240-trial ten-fold biosignal experiment, nonspeech events, acoustic-contamination analysis and the fixed-day165 transfer safeguard retain their separate cohorts and procedures. No recordings, private targets, weights or waveform predictions were accessed in this component; no author baseline, model trial or scientific comparison was run.
