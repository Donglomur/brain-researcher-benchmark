## Scientific questions and claim boundaries

Reproduction asks whether the submitted program reconstructs the paper's complete evidence: expert and early-training waiting behavior; optimal inference and divisive alternatives; muscimol effects, physiology and alternative explanations; event-specific cellular responses across brain areas; TCA factors and cluster responses; two-timescale latent dynamics, filtering, neuron prediction, reward decoding and mutual information; novice/expert differences; and 200 additive/divisive simulated datasets with ATI recovery. A successful scalar prediction alone cannot satisfy reproduction.

Improvement asks whether a submitted method improves fixed, protected estimands while retaining scientifically interpretable controls. The primary improvement estimand is future-time held-neuron conditional prediction. Secondary families are prospective waiting-time prediction, reward decoding, descriptive latent/block association, and simulation mechanism recovery. Report them separately; do not sum seconds, information bits, MSE, p-values and ATI errors into an unexplained scalar. The participant can propose an additional frontier analysis, but an exploratory claim cannot become a scored primary outcome after seeing test results.

Neural block decoding is not evidence of inference by itself. MI is association, not decoding accuracy. ATI is not a calibrated probability of an animal's strategy. Neural prediction gains do not establish causal necessity. Muscimol/control comparisons involve repeated measurements of nine reported animals, control anesthesia without infusion, washout exclusions and a separate acute physiology animal; they do not provide randomized mechanistic interventions at every neuron or session. A result on the retained cohorts is not cross-species or never-seen-neuron generalization.

## ATI correction and interpretation

Original MathML FD13 and FD17 contain two separately fenced norms in the denominator:

`ATI = E[ ||B z_k|| / (||A y_t^k|| + ||B z_k||) ]`.

FD17 substitutes the divisive state. This is an expectation of per-case ratios, not a ratio of mean norms. Where its denominator is positive, the ratio lies in [0,1]. Zero/zero is undefined and must be counted and disclosed. Any required zero-denominator bin makes its complete-support ATI null and the affected full-family numeric comparison required-unscorable; no mean over only defined ratios substitutes for that endpoint. The earlier planning note's norm-of-sum/cancellation warning was a flattened-equation transcription error; it is preserved in provenance but superseded here by direct original MathML. Boundedness does not make ATI a probability of inference or a causal strategy label.

ATI also depends on the fast-state coordinate metric under nonorthogonal reparameterization. Preserve the paper's exact learned-C SVD/orthonormalization and source-to-transformed A/B/state algebra; validate similarity-transform equivalence and truth/fit coordinate conventions before comparing values. Do not calculate ATI from arbitrarily scaled hidden activations. An improved nonlinear model may honestly declare ATI inapplicable while supplying a separate faithful native hLDS reproduction. Its predictive score remains meaningful, but it cannot claim improved ATI recovery without an identifiable comparable state decomposition.

The paper specifies 100 additive and 100 divisive simulations. Their exact generator, geometry, seeds, noise, parameter distribution and truth-coordinate mapping remain unbound. Preserve all 200 cases; do not substitute an easy invented grid. Recovery products include every truth/fit ATI, error distribution, bias, rank agreement and failures per mechanism. A thresholded strategy classifier is a separate, training-calibrated endpoint, not a meaning assigned to ATI post hoc. Source `plotFigure7_naiveEphys.m:70` uses bottom/top **40%**, omitting the middle 20%; native low/high ATI groups are not median groups and 1,072+924 neurons are not the entire naive cohort.

## Prospective improvement design choices

The following choices are proposed benchmark policy, not claims about the paper's native split. Use a mechanical chronological 60% fit /20% validation /20% test split within each eligible session, with optional final refit on the first 80% after choices are fixed. Cut by original ordered trial index without using true block, performance, outcome or future unit quality to select boundaries. Preserve complete session/rat identifiers and report every missing class or insufficient-support disposition. Eligibility uses administrative stage and training-prefix evidence only. Native and prospective populations therefore need explicit different keys; neither quietly replaces the other.

The primary prediction returns a target neuron's expected square-root 50-ms spike count at a test time, conditional only on permitted current/past peer-neuron observations and admissible prior behavioral events. The target neuron's full test stream is never exposed. Each target-neuron context is isolated: observations seen as peers in another target's job cannot flow through shared mutable test state. The sealed fitted model is read-only; local filtering state belongs to one query stream. Train-time loadings may include that target's training observations. The collector commits each prediction before revealing any target. No future-bin smoothing, centered filter, whole-trial summary or unsealed precomputed latent is allowed to cross the query watermark.

Primary loss is MSE in square-root-count units, averaged bins within neuron, neurons within session, sessions within rat and then rats. Include every required query. Any missing/invalid required prediction makes the whole family invalid_candidate with null official score/gains; mathematically undefined or unavailable required targets make it required_unscorable. Fixed support and exact weighting are defined in the S2 score policy; no favorable-neuron selection or invented numeric penalty is permitted. Compare paired absolute loss and gain against independently qualified hLDS, ordinary LDS and a train-prefix per-neuron mean square-root-count baseline. Prospective 11-state ordinary LDS is an explicit total-state match to 1+10; a 10-state fast-only control separates that convention. The original ordinary-LDS dimension is still an unresolved native fact. Give rat-clustered uncertainty and per-rat losses. A prospective gain claim requires a predeclared family and a confidence interval exceeding zero plus a scientifically justified minimum gain fixed before tests. That minimum is not guessed here.

Behavior queries cover every administratively eligible test trial reaching the pre-wait cutoff, including rewarded and unrewarded trials. Candidates and references predict this identical target-independent universe; future catch/timeout/outcome roles never determine visible presence, order, cardinality or fields. The private catch mask joins only after full prediction closure. The endpoint is observed restricted waiting min(T_optout,100 s) on authentic reward-omitted trials: documented administrative timeouts contribute100 s with a valid-censoring flag, while absent timestamps/uncertain clocks remain unavailable targets. It is not unobserved uncensored waiting. Official loss is raw-second MSE, equal trials within session, sessions within rat, rats; MAE uses identical support. Every query requires a finite prediction in[0,100], even if privately unscored. Missing required predictions invalidate the family; missing target or empty required stratum makes it required-unscorable. No outlier trimming, strategy-conditioned inclusion or hidden fallback is allowed. Inference/divisive and train-prefix catch-mean comparators use the same allowed view, full support and output domain. Exact timing, receipts and counterfactual invariance are specified in the S2 query policy.

For prospective reward decoding, fit latent transforms and the downstream probe using training data only, choose regularization on validation data and score test balanced accuracy with all five classes. Use a separate neural-only query view without current offered reward/tone/true-block covariates. Each required session×250-ms event-window stratum must contain all five classes; otherwise the complete decoder family is required-unscorable, never renormalized to available classes. Weights and comparators are fixed in the S2 score policy. Native cross-validation on full-session learned latents is reported in its native lane, not passed off as inductive evaluation. ## Matched conditions and contamination controls

R_A and R_B reconstruct the entire paper; I_A and I_B run the protected improvement protocol. A receives the same paper and allowed data as B; B additionally receives the exact pinned author subtree and explicitly bound dependencies, never hidden targets, model outputs or grader code. Resource limits and investigation budgets are matched; unrestricted independent program design is allowed in both. The absence of an author producer does not justify silently providing its hidden outputs to B.

Reproduction exposes historical outcomes that overlap prospective tests. Therefore execute I before R with independent contexts, or use strictly separated agents/containers whose R products cannot be read until I is complete. Freeze each I source/model-selection policy before any R exposure. A and B also require fresh independent contexts: an agent cannot unsee B's code. Disclose public-data and paper contamination; these controls do not make publicly released data provably absent from pretraining. Score within-arm improvement against same-access baselines and separately estimate A/B effects; code access and scientific improvement are different causal contrasts.
