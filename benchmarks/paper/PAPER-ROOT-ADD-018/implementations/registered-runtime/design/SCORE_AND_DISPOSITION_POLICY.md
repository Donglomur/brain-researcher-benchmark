# Complete-support score and disposition policy

This S2 policy repairs OFC-D04. It specifies future scientific contracts without executing a scorer or inventing targets, measured performance, native tolerances or practical-effect thresholds. `FAMILY_SCORE_CONTRACTS.json` is the finite family/type catalogue. `SCOPE_POLICY_BINDINGS.json` binds every existing main/supplement obligation and cohort role; all 74 obligations, 20 roles, 200 simulations and ten open gates remain.

## Typed values and admission

Every submitted prediction/product row has an immutable operator-issued key and one of the finite candidate dispositions `value`, `declared_fit_failure`, `declared_prediction_failure`, `declared_undefined`, or `declared_inapplicable`. `value` contains only finite numeric values of the prescribed shape/unit or an allowed categorical/provenance type. `declared_inapplicable` is admissible only for I_SIM_ATI/I_REAL_ATI when the submitted improved architecture has no identified comparable ATI decomposition; it records an explicit no-score/no-gain result while all mandatory native hLDS reproduction remains required. It cannot excuse a neural, behavior, decoder or native omission. A tagged nonfinite native arithmetic result is carried as `{kind: native_nonfinite, reason: nan|positive_infinity|negative_infinity, native_stage: ..., source_binding: ...}`, never as a JSON NaN/Inf or a finite sentinel. Its scientific status is independently checked. Ordinary predicted values may not use this native tag to evade an obligation.

Collector-discovered `missing`, `duplicate`, `unknown_key`, `bad_type_or_shape`, `out_of_domain`, `nonfinite_prediction`, `unbound_producer`, `bad_watermark`, `resource_failure`, and `receipt_mismatch` are distinct candidate-failure codes. A candidate's own failure/undefined assertion does not establish scientific undefinedness: the grader recomputes the relevant predicate from admissible inputs and qualified operator evidence. Omitted/failing required outputs always remain in the original roster. No unknown key may replace an expected key, no best-attempt selection occurs, and source/model changes require a separately admitted submission and budget.

Private target dispositions are `observed`, `observed_restricted_censored`, `outside_scoring_mask`, `scientifically_undefined`, `unavailable_input_binding`, and `unavailable_operator_target`. Private reference dispositions are `qualified_value`, `scientifically_undefined`, `unavailable_reference`, and `reference_execution_failure`. Reasons and underlying evidence are mandatory. These are separate fields, so an operator outage does not erase a candidate failure. Missing input/reference binding is not a participant's incorrect numerical prediction. A valid censored observation is not scientific undefinedness.

`FAMILY_SCORE_CONTRACTS.json` fixes each family's keys, required public support, protected target support, admissible domains and aggregation. Native matrix shapes and exact authentic row rosters must be bound during G02/G07 before execution; inability to bind them is `required_unscorable`, not permission to generate a convenient smaller roster. Public support is independent of target values. Protected scoring weights/masks can depend on the prespecified scientific role or label, but are committed before predictions and never adapt to candidate or reference failures.

## Fixed support and exact family disposition

The final report preserves three orthogonal assessments: `candidate_status` (`complete_valid`, `invalid`, `scientifically_undefined`, `inapplicable`); `target_status` (`complete_defined`, `complete_with_valid_censoring`, `scientifically_undefined`, `unavailable`); and a per-baseline `reference_status` (`complete_qualified`, `scientifically_undefined`, `unavailable`, `failed`). Counts include all required keys, scored keys, structurally unscored keys, missing/invalid/undefined/censored/inapplicable keys, and each native source exclusion. The operator chooses statuses by this exact rule:

1. Any missing or invalid required candidate row, process/receipt failure, unsupported undefined assertion or changed support makes the family `invalid_candidate`, with official aggregate score and every gain/CI null. This includes omissions on behavior queries whose hidden mask is zero. Keep complete target/reference dispositions for attribution. A finite diagnostic on valid rows is labeled `noncomparable_partial_diagnostic` and never substitutes for the official score.
2. Otherwise, an undefined required candidate statistic validated as mathematically undefined, admissible ATI inapplicability, any required undefined target, unavailable input/target binding, empty required scoring stratum, or unbound metric/clock yields `required_unscorable`, with official numeric aggregate, gains and inferential claims null. No candidate or operator may drop the offending case, switch denominator, set it to zero, or carry last observation forward. Admissible ATI inapplicability requires a complete keyed disposition roster, not a missing file; it bars an ATI improvement claim without invalidating an independently complete neural family.
3. Otherwise the official absolute score is `scored` (or `scored_with_censoring` for valid restricted-wait observations) using the full fixed support/weights. For each named comparator, a paired gain exists **only if** its entire required prediction support is complete, qualified and defined on the same roster, with the same masks, weights, inputs and information access. A missing/failed comparator leaves the candidate absolute score intact but that gain null with a named reference disposition. The family-wide improvement comparison is `required_unscorable` if any mandatory comparator is unavailable/failed/undefined; do not quietly rename the best available comparator as the baseline.
4. A directional improvement claim additionally requires all prespecified primary/secondary family obligations, controls, paired uncertainty, multiplicity policy and practical-effect threshold qualified and frozen before test access. These thresholds remain G07 gates. Without them report the complete absolute value and numerical paired differences as descriptive only. No p-value or bootstrap replicate can fabricate qualification. Rat-level resampling uses the same complete rat roster for candidate and reference; an undefined replicate/statistic prevents the affected inferential claim, not an unreported rerun or selective resample.

No finite penalty is invented for invalid submissions. Null scores plus explicit statuses prevent denominator gaming without conflating engineering failure and scientific error. The fixed denominator means its roster/weights never change; it does not mean forcing an undefined number into arithmetic. Candidate failure in one family bars an overall whole-program improvement claim, although genuinely complete other families can retain separately named results. No mixed-unit overall scalar exists.

## Neural primary

For each required test bin of target neuron n in session s of rat r, the target is `sqrt(count_in_50ms)` and the prediction is its finite real conditional mean under the permitted peer/history view. Negative finite predictions are allowed because linear Gaussian baselines can produce them; no scorer clipping is performed. Squared error is in count units after the square-root transform, explicitly labeled `squared_sqrt_count_error`. Count/exposure units and complete bin geometry must be bound. No normalization by private test variance or candidate-selected active bins is allowed.

The exact loss is equal mean over required bins per neuron, then equal mean over required neurons per session, then equal mean over required sessions per rat, then equal mean over required rats. Each weight is frozen from the operator roster; any zero-support required level is required-unscorable. An unobserved target bin is not an observed zero count. Comparators are qualified causal hLDS(1 slow+10 fast), ordinary LDS(11 total states), fast-only LDS(10) and a train-prefix per-neuron mean square-root-count predictor. All require identical full query support and target-stream isolation. Gain for each comparator is `reference_loss - candidate_loss` (positive is better); uncertainty is paired by rat. Native original LDS dimensionality and folds remain unresolved facts, distinct from these prospective choices.

## Observed restricted waiting time

The prediction on **every** pre-wait query is a finite real value in [0,100] seconds estimating `E[min(T_optout,100) | allowed past, current offer, reward-omitted trial]` under the catch role. A catch-conditioned estimand does not imply a catch-conditioned query schedule: the model predicts it on the full target-independent universe without knowing which trials will be scored. No prediction-time future catch flag is supplied. The qualified policy must bind the original catch-allocation role; this is descriptive performance on those catch trials, not an assumption of causal randomization or generalization to all rewarded trials.

The private scoring mask includes every query assigned to the authentic reward-omitted/catch role, with the administrative 100-s waiting clock, in the fixed test roster. A documented opt-out before the limit has target equal to observed elapsed waiting. A documented no-opt-out administrative timeout has target 100 and disposition `observed_restricted_censored`; this is an exactly observed restricted/capped endpoint although the uncensored opt-out time is unknown. Neither an unfinished recording, an absent timestamp nor an uncertain clock is a timeout. Such cases remain expected masked queries but have unavailable target dispositions, making the family required-unscorable. If the input version uses a different cap/clock or does not identify catch allocation, do not reinterpret it as this endpoint: G02/G07 stays open. Reward delivery before opt-out on a noncatch trial has `outside_scoring_mask`, with its prediction still mandatory. No post-test outlier deletion, winsorization, strategy-conditioned selection or replacement with average waiting is permitted.

Official loss is raw-second squared error, equal mean over masked queries within each required session, sessions within rat, and rats. The mask and all weights are operator-frozen before predictions; any required session/rat with no masked trial makes the complete family required-unscorable, not excluded. MAE on the same full weighted support and complete residuals are secondary diagnostics. Comparators are fitted inference and divisive-history predictors under the identical allowed view plus the train-prefix catch restricted-wait mean (same [0,100] output domain; no test fallback). Fitting/calibration of model scales must use train/validation only. An undefined training mean or failed comparator remains unavailable. Gain is reference MSE minus candidate MSE. This score does not estimate an unobserved uncensored wait, a survival likelihood or optimal willingness to wait beyond 100 seconds.

## Five-class reward decoding

Every administrative neural window has one mandatory class prediction from the fixed ordinal set {1,2,3,4,5}; a probability vector, if supplied as a diagnostic, must have five finite entries in[0,1] whose sum differs from1 by at most1e-8. This is a proposed serialization-validity rule, not a scientific agreement tolerance; the grader does not renormalize. The official metric uses the submitted class, with no hidden argmax/tie repair. Physical female/male volumes map to the paper's common ordinal classes. Inputs are neural-only as specified in the query policy; directly copying an offered reward from a behavior query is inadmissible.

For each required session and declared 250-ms event-time window, calculate five recalls `correct_c/number_c` and take their equal mean. Then average fixed windows within session, sessions within rat, rats equally. All five true classes must have support in **each required session×window stratum**. No privately balanced subset is exposed; weights implement balance. Missing even one class yields required-unscorable for the complete decoder family, with the missing-class table, not a four-class renormalization or removal of that session/window. Native original ten-fold balanced SVM remains separate; original class balancing/folds must be authenticated, not guessed from this prospective metric. Comparator products include train-only hLDS latents plus SVM, train-only ordinary-LDS latents plus the same probe, and a no-neural training-majority class predictor with lowest-ordinal tie-break. Gain is candidate balanced accuracy minus comparator balanced accuracy; report chance context 1/5 without treating it as an independently executed reference.

## ATI, simulations, associations and native products

Each ATI request binds a complete fixed time-bin/trial support and coordinate map. Return separate nonnegative norms u and v, the ratio u/(u+v) and all provenance. Positive finite denominator gives a value in [0,1]. Any 0/0 makes the requested full-support ATI mathematically undefined; the official mean ATI is null, with exact zero-denominator count and support. The mean over only defined ratios may be shown as a noncomparable diagnostic, never the full ATI. Nonfinite inputs and unqualified coordinate algebra have their own failure/binding dispositions. The operator recomputes ratios and aggregation; it does not trust a candidate-supplied aggregate. Native historical code NaN/omission behavior, once obtained, is retained as a **separate native statistic**, not silently adopted as a new full-support improvement estimand.

Simulation recovery requires all 200 original cases, 100 additive and 100 divisive. Within each case use the full bound time/trial support for truth and fitted ATI; case-level undefined truth or fitted ATI yields required-unscorable for the full recovery family. Candidate prediction/fit failure yields invalid_candidate. Official recovery loss is `.5*mean_100_additive((fitATI-trueATI)^2) + .5*mean_100_divisive((fitATI-trueATI)^2)`; all 200 contribute with weight1/200, with no failed-case deletion. Bias and MAE use the same weights; Spearman rank agreement is diagnostic and explicitly undefined for a constant rank vector. A calibrated mechanism classifier is not selected as an official endpoint. A nonlinear method without an identified comparable decomposition can honestly mark improvement ATI inapplicable, but then has no ATI-recovery gain; it must still deliver the required faithful native hLDS simulation reproduction. The mandatory recovery comparator is an independently qualified original hLDS fitted to all 200 cases using the exact original generator/noise/seeds, currently unbound. No toy substitute qualifies it.

Real-session ATI is a descriptive family, keyed separately from simulation ATI and the neural primary. Native low/high selectors remain Naive-only strict ATI<40th percentile and ATI>60th percentile, with the middle/ties explicitly excluded from those native subgroup products. That legitimate source-defined exclusion is not deletion from the complete ATI-session roster. Missing/undefined ATI cannot be assigned to the middle group. No scientific claim is available for an incomplete complete-session comparison.

Native reproduction reports a vector over all74 obligations and named estimator components. Every producer family has a finite list of typed products in the JSON catalogue, keyed tensors and full inclusion/status rows. Continuous comparison uses independent qualified references with pre-frozen tolerances; categorical/key and provenance comparisons use exact equality. There is no default numeric tolerance or pass threshold. No reference or no tolerance means `required_unscorable`; a source-faithful undefined native value is documented with its predicate and reference match, not converted to quantitative agreement or a scientific p-value. Source-defined omissions, e.g. acute NaN-bin fit exclusion and TCA zero-fill, are reproduced with their full provenance; they do not disappear from the obligation ledger. Non-statistical panels require exact provenance/schema correspondence and cannot receive fabricated accuracy.

Association/MI, slow-history regression, block decoding and d-prime stay complete descriptive/native families under their authentic estimator support and units. They have no official composite gain score here. Ross MI in bits, 1,000 native shuffles, 58/42 role sessions, native five-fold block decoder, OLS current+ten prior offers and native d-prime15 shuffles retain their own source/Methods gates. Prospective blocked-null/rat-clustered sensitivity products require separately bound training-only latent transforms, whole support and reference/null specifications before any inference; a favorable association cannot substitute for neural prediction or causal mechanism evidence.

## Complete report and future controls

The assembler requires one record for every expected family, role, obligation, query, simulation and comparator, plus candidate/target/reference statuses and full source/build/model/input/universe/mask/reference/scorer receipts. Native, corrected sensitivity and prospective lanes have distinct keys. An overall full-paper reproduced label requires all quantitative and provenance obligations admitted, scientifically evaluable where claimed, compared to qualified references and passed under frozen policies; unresolved supplement/native producers and all ten current gates prevent that label. Numerical values on a partial family may only be labeled partial diagnostics.

Future controls must demonstrate: difficult-query omission is not rewarded; unscored behavior omissions still fail admission; genuine timeout100 is scored while missing recording data is not; changed hidden catch flags cannot change visible queries; missing fifth class does not become four-class BA; zero ATI support cannot disappear; one of200 failed simulations nulls the whole recovery score; a failed comparator cannot produce a gain; fixed-support/weight swaps fail binding; undefined source p-values cannot become evidence; and native TCA/distance quirks remain distinct from corrected products. These are specified tests, **none run here**.
