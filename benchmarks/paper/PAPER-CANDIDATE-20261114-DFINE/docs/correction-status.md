# DFINE publication correction

Source status checked: 16 November 2026.

The [15 January 2025 author correction](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41551-025-01345-4)
addresses the publication license of *Dynamical flexible inference of nonlinear latent
factors and structures in neural population activity*. The notice identifies the
[original article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41551-023-01106-1), DOI
`10.1038/s41551-023-01106-1`, published online on 11 December 2023. It states that the
article changed from the standard Springer Nature license to open access under
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), and that the online
article was corrected accordingly.

**The stated scope of this notice is licensing.** It reports no amendment to
methods, data, figures, results, missing-observation definitions or code. This
finding establishes the subject of this particular correction; it is not a
version-by-version comparison of the article or its software.

The publisher's correction text was available through search-indexed content.
Direct publisher-page requests for the notice and original article redirected to
an inaccessible identity endpoint. Independently, the original article's
[Crossref record](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1038%2Fs41551-023-01106-1)
confirmed its title, DOI and online publication date, and listed CC BY 4.0 for
the version of record. No article PDF was downloaded.

This note updates only the correction-scope uncertainty recorded in the frozen
[missing-observation source note](../implementations/missing-observation/README.md).
That historical note and the [canonical task definition](../task.json) remain
unchanged. The article's license does not establish the licenses of repository
code, data or third-party material; those require their own source checks.

The benchmark still needs its existing claim-specific checks: exact public
session/file mapping, observation-ratio and masking semantics, causal versus
noncausal information boundaries, preprocessing and train-only fitting rules,
historical code lineage, independent references and candidate execution. This
source annotation does not qualify the task, reproduce its result, or change
any readiness claim.
