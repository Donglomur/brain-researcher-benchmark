# Figure 5 baseline regressions

This component constructs the two original **baseline** regression panels from aligned extracted CTRL maps and computes the source fitted OLS statistics. It is separate from `initial-evaluator-v1`, which implements descriptive Figure 2 CALC–CTRL map contrasts. Neither implementation establishes reproduction of all Figure 5 results or improvement beyond them.

The recovered author source is [`F_Fig4_5.ipynb` at the pinned revision](https://github.com/NeuroenergeticsLab/two_modes_of_hemodynamics/blob/1b22c2cb03bab0d5cf70da6a45c4d720194dc523/F_Fig4_5.ipynb), authenticated by its full Git blob hash against the repository tree. Cells 4, 9–10 and 13–17 establish:

- The exact primary 40 participants, CTRL maps and MNI152 2-mm support; CBF is divided by 0.75 before subject reduction.
- Per-voxel subject `nanmedian`, then concatenation of CALC-negative, CALC-positive, MEM-negative and MEM-positive masks within each response stratum. A physical voxel selected in both tasks occurs twice. A spatial union would change the fitted observations.
- Entire-row exclusion when median CMRO2 is strictly greater than 350 or median OEF is strictly greater than 1. Equality remains. Predictor centering follows these exclusions and precedes joint missing-row exclusion.
- An intercept plus mean-centered OEF, CBF and CBV, with centered-total-sum-of-squares R². This implementation follows the source **statsmodels formula branch**. The preceding Pingouin branch drops predictor/target missing values separately and is not silently treated as equivalent under mismatched missingness.

`prepare_baseline(subject_ids, voxel_indices, maps, masks)` accepts the four source arrays of shape `(40, V)` and all eight task/sign/stratum masks. The exact array keys and source subject order are constants in `baseline.py`. Array extraction and artifact authentication remain operator responsibilities. `evaluate_baseline(...)` returns both fitted cells separately, coefficients, F, degrees of freedom, row-identity hashes, counts of repeated physical voxels, and transparent exclusion counts. A perfect nonconstant fit with exactly zero residual has positive-infinite F; JSON represents this with `f_statistic: null` and `f_statistic_nonfinite: positive_infinity_perfect_fit`. It reports no inferential voxel p-value, combined score, or success reward. Source NaNs retain their source median and complete-case semantics; infinities, ambiguous sign masks, rank-deficient designs and changed cohorts are rejected.

The [primary paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-025-02132-9) reports baseline concordant/discordant R² of .91/.91, with residual degrees of freedom 21,809/14,544. For the stated three-predictor full-rank models with intercept, these imply **21,813/14,548 fitted rows**, not independent participants or necessarily unique voxels. This inference does not authenticate row membership. The paper's task-change .86/.84 cells remain separate.

## Why the task-change route is unresolved

The recovered notebook's cell 30 uses the first 30 participants for **both** CALC and MEM, with `desc-semi-quant` / `desc-semi-quant-corrected` OEF and CMRO2. The paper's Figure 5 caption specifies CALC medians across 40 and MEM medians across 30; its Methods distinguish BOLD-informed semiquantitative maps as a Supplementary Figure 5 control. Thus executing the recovered task-change branch would not establish the original Figure 5 comparison conditions. Cell 31 also contains CALC row-drop expressions indexed through already-filtered MEM frames; it must not be repaired by assumption. The adjacent pinned [`F_Fig4.ipynb`](https://github.com/NeuroenergeticsLab/two_modes_of_hemodynamics/blob/1b22c2cb03bab0d5cf70da6a45c4d720194dc523/F_Fig4.ipynb) has now been recovered and authenticated against the exact revision's Git blob. The [adjacent-source audit](taskchange-source-audit.v1.json) records its identity and zero-based cell locations. Its cell 31 also uses first30 for both tasks and semiquantitative task CMRO2/OEF; cell 32 repeats the MEM-indexed CALC filtering. Thus recovery closes the missing-notebook inspection step while leaving the original support, map lineage and row-filter conflicts unresolved. The notebook's saved .860882/.842562 task-change fits were not recomputed and cannot establish provenance or qualification; a stored plotting failure also prevents treating its saved outputs as evidence of an uninterrupted execution. The earlier `provenance.json` remains historical evidence of the original implementation and inspection.

No authentic maps, masks or row arrays were prepared or evaluated. Original mask construction and exact fitted-row membership still need authentication, along with the native environment and author baseline. Reproduction is a first-class future outcome. Improvement is a separate unresolved scientific question: the CMRO2 target is Fick-derived from related inputs, so increasing fitted R² alone does not establish better physiological estimation. The paired paper+data and paper+data+author-code participant conditions are not yet delivered.

Generated controls test analytical factorial-design coefficients/R²/F, duplicated task voxels, source CBF units, missing-data centering, strict source cutoffs and invalid inputs. They are software evidence, not a source-data run. Upstream notebook code is not redistributed; its recorded CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms remain distinct from the dataset's CC0 terms.
