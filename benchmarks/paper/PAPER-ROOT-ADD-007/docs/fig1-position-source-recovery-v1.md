Its source-only extract is pinned in [source_pins.json](../implementations/fig1-position-scoring-v1/source_pins.json). The original notebook outputs had been discarded by the prior acquisition. The [new prediction scorer](../implementations/fig1-position-scoring-v1/README.md) implements these evaluation indices, metric and reduction without importing the source decoder. This fills a missing endpoint-scoring component for two distinct first-class outcomes: reproducing original results and exceeding them on the same task. It records reproduction prediction discrepancies separately from candidate-minus-author metric deltas, and retains the paired paper+data versus paper+data+author-code access declaration. Neither outcome is decided by software controls. The existing literal decoder in the older programme was inspected and is deliberately not duplicated or vendored. The earlier recurrence/trajectory implementation is preserved as a separate component.

The metric's prediction-dependent selection is consequential. For truth `[0,0,0,.9]`, predictions `[.1,.1,.1,.8]` have source RMSE `.1`. Predictions `[1,1,1,.9]` obtain source RMSE zero by excluding their three wrong modal predictions, while their all-frame RMSE rises from `.1` to approximately `.866`. This is an analytic generated counterexample, not measured paper performance. Therefore the scorer always retains full-frame and fixed-author-support errors plus coverage counts, separately from the source metric. No candidate can be declared superior from the filtered number alone by this component.
