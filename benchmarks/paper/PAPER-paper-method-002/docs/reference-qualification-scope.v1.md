# Jaxley reference qualification: current scope clarification

This note clarifies historical replay attribution and prospective reference construction for the supplied-cell retinal prediction task. The current [reproduction contract](../reproduction.v2.json), [improvement contract](../improvement.v1.json) and generated blocked reports retain wording that historical cache equivalence is an execution gate and bundle cache/checkpoint identity with scientific source identity. Those documents have not been rewritten. The historical artifact limitation is claim-specific; it does not categorically require recovery of an old pickle or checkpoint before constructing a qualified same-task source-method reference.

A newly produced cache may be used only after independent source-equivalence and numerical qualification of the original observation realization, ROI-to-site correspondence, response transformations, masks and source procedure. Qualify the full producer and its dependencies, then the reference execution in a recorded compatible runtime. A representation-only dtype/index change is not automatically a different task, but compatibility alone cannot prove scientific or numerical equivalence. A fresh cache cannot infer missing original biological identities, positional joins, split assignments or validity conventions.

Attribution to the exact historical execution still requires its historical cache/checkpoint provenance. A fresh controlled source-method reference must be reported separately from replay of the published rounded mean 0.25. It does not by itself establish the published run, a numerical equivalence tolerance or the retained published-versus-reproduced improvement baseline. A distinct source-method rerun claim requires its separately reviewed supported contract; this note does not select one. The current same-original-split requirement remains active: a newly preregistered split cannot substitute for missing original train/validation/test identities.

The scientific requirements remain unchanged:

- Authenticate cell 20161028_1, its morphology, all 15 recording fields, the first 1024 source images per field, all 232 raw ROI identities and their actual ordered correspondence to the 147 model recording sites. Preserve positional raw/calcium joins, complete stimulus/current-input production, target labels and relevant noise/parameter conventions; a shape of 147 is not identity evidence.
- Qualify the source's 7 Hz calcium low-pass filtering, per-ROI standardization, 1.8-second label delay, compartment/site grouping, response transformations and operator-owned masks. Resolve dtype and invalid/undefined-value conventions from source evidence; software defaults cannot establish them.
- Recover the exact original training/validation/test support and independently qualify source-equivalent fitting and checkpoint selection. A default 128-test-row configuration, the separate seven-field reduced-data design, or a newly sampled split cannot establish the original evaluation realization.
- Preserve the full held-out prediction matrix and every site's support and undefined status. Compute masked per-site Pearson correlations and their original arithmetic aggregate; do not pool sites, weight by support, drop undefined sites or replace prediction with simulator agreement. Qualify source-grounded tolerances and dependence-aware uncertainty; one cell's sites are not independent biological replicates.
- Resolve the original fixed/untrained OR constrained-parameter control's actual primary-source status, membership and matched evaluation support. Do not invent a roster, require both alternatives, choose the easiest alternative or substitute the reduced-data comparison. Qualify authentic reference competence, granular-output verification, candidate execution, protected held-out custody and concrete cumulative budgets.

Both access conditions retain identical original stimuli, morphology, training labels, validation mechanism and evaluation support. The author-code condition adds only author-code information, never held-out targets, another split or additional input privileges. J17/J18/J19 and the broader 36-family programme remain separately named evidence; a primary prediction result establishes no whole-programme success or mechanistic validity.

This note resolves no scientific hold, admits no execution and changes no flag, count, criterion, tolerance or outcome. Existing author evidence and historical limitations remain intact.
