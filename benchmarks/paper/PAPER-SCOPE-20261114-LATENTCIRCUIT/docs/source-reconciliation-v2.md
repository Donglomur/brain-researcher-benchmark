# Source preparation exists; the original empirical endpoint remains unresolved

This is a source and prior-evidence reconciliation, not a new preparation, model run,
scorer qualification or paper improvement. The current task correctly preserves the
original two-monkey endpoint. Its acquisition and census blockers do not yet reflect
the existing external preparation evidence now located. The task's separate lack
of authentic archive admission remains valid: located preparation does not resolve
rights, historical-paper equivalence or scientific qualification.

## What can be reused

The retained whole-metadata gate reports all **1,402 source records**, **2,812 archive
dispositions** in the preparation outcome, and **1,755,306 source-local trial rows**.
The rows are not a count of independent population trials. The population index
preserves **483 A/ar and 323 F/fe selected source coordinates**, with training tensors
of **72 conditions × 15 times × 483/323 coordinates**. Eligibility uses at least four
correct trials in every joint context/motion/directional-color cell. Dominant color
alone is insufficient because the target colors change location across trials.

These are metadata observations from prior preparation, independently reread here;
the underlying arrays and raw neural archive were not opened in this revision. The
preparation outcome declares `historical_equivalence=false`, zero model fits, and
`scientific_qualification=false`. Its full source census supports the Methods'
762/640 available and 483/323 eligible counts. It does not establish the lineage of
the Results' 727/574 counts or authenticate distinct biological neurons, sessions,
cross-unit events, or historical half-trial memberships.

The original source release is already retained at
[v1.0.0, commit 3e151da](https://github.com/engellab/latentcircuit/tree/3e151da42379a0e659f3fefcdc858a66eba1eaac).
The previous complete release audit and named reconstruction are also present.
Reacquiring the same archive or reimplementing those reconstruction routines is not
the missing scientific step.

## Why this is not a native empirical baseline

| Requirement | Located evidence | Remaining limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original population and all 72 conditions | Complete source census; 483/323 selected coordinates match Methods | Count agreement does not authenticate historical membership or reconcile the separate Results denominators |
| Original half-trial comparison | A frozen source-local split and every source/trial disposition exist | The historical seed, odd-count policy, weighting and trial identities are not recovered; a new source-conformant stochastic policy still requires prospective calibration and paired comparison |
| PCA retaining 50% variance | Actual named preparation retained **47/36 PCs**, with shapes 72×15×47/36 | Paper reports **40/31**; neither the source operands nor the historical PCA pool/configuration are authenticated as identical |
| Neural/model clock | Source-defined 50-sample boxes have centers **125–825 ms**, with one final native sample unbinned | Paper describes a first center at 100 ms, 150 model states of 10 ms, and fitting the last 15 model steps; the reconstructed 650-ms shift and interpolation are choices, not recovered author behavior |
| Empirical objective and published fit | Paper equations 28–32 include an intercept and three named terms; released helper source is available | Printed norm reductions, published R-squared conversion and released helper denominator/dimension conventions are not interchangeable |
| Native task safeguard | A reconstruction specifies continuous targets 1.2/0.2 over the final 25 model steps | Binary choice accuracy cannot replace this continuous loss; a competent same-task baseline is not established |
| Original start ensemble | The prior named reference code records complete start identities and failures | Code presence and generated controls do not constitute the original 200 fitted models per monkey or its score aggregation; connectivity top-ten summaries are a separate selection |

The retained rank diagnosis already distinguishes causes that could change the PCA
matrix (unit-relative scaling, condition weights, split realizations, input pool,
actual bin support) from changes that cannot by themselves explain normalized-rank
differences (row/unit permutations, component signs, a common covariance denominator,
or timestamp relabeling without changing values). No cause was measured. Do not force
40/31, narrow the cohort, change the threshold, or select preprocessing because it
reproduces the reported integers. A predeclared source-conformant stochastic rerun
need not recover historical membership, but must preserve the source protocol and
establish justified calibration; this record does neither.

The released `latent_net.py` was read directly. With row-orthonormal `q`, data `y`
of last dimension `N`, and latent `x` of last dimension `n`, its MSE reductions imply

```text
nmse_y = nmse_q + (n / N) * nmse_x
```

All three use the same full-dimensional centered-data denominator. Thus counting
two versus three terms alone is not proof of a mathematical discrepancy. Conversely,
`1 - nmse_x` is not established as the reported projected test R-squared: its
numerator averages over `n` dimensions while the denominator averages over `N`.
The paper-specific intercept, reduction, centering, clock and conversion remain to
be bound together. This derivation was already recognized in the prior scientific
join; it is retained here to prevent an incorrect replacement scorer.

## A concrete reuse hazard

The original population index incorrectly labels both
`R_splitlocal_reconstruction.validation_calibration.fit_scope` fields as
`training_only`. The R profile fitted calibration on validation, and already marks
`valid_for_protected_prediction=false`. A previously retained correction, pinned to
the exact index, assigns `validation_fitted_reconstruction_only` for both animals.
Future consumers must apply that effective view or refuse the ambiguous original
labels. Historical arrays and metadata remain unchanged. The I profile applies
training calibration to validation, but it is a differently specified extension;
its protected-prediction semantics do not recover the paper's within-split protocol.

## Integration proposal and qualification

Preserve the existing task and improvement contract as `source_unresolved`.
Propose updating the acquisition and census blockers to distinguish located
complete named preparation from unresolved admission and original empirical parity.
Keep the original monkey-specific 0.88/0.76 projected-fit anchors, 11%/7% captured
total variance, continuous task safeguard, both animals, full eligible population,
all 72 conditions and 200-start-per-monkey requirement. Keep the existing
fixed-original-neuron scorer labelled as an extension.

Before a native author-calibration protocol can be frozen, bind an empirical
preprocessing/configuration or justified source-conformant reconstruction, exact
score conversion, temporal join, start/selection policy and prospective calibration
tolerance. A fitted weaker baseline cannot lower the published targets. The existing
named reference's four objective alternatives, two noise laws and two architectures
must not be silently searched for whichever best matches a paper anchor.

This revision adds no executable implementation because the located, reviewed
reconstruction code already implements the identified alternatives. Porting that
code would not resolve source identity. No new download, installation, raw-array
read, native numerical preparation, model fit, candidate trial or generated test
was performed. Validation consisted of source/metadata inspection, selected
metadata comparisons and SHA-256 verification. No new benchmark readiness or
scientific result is claimed.
