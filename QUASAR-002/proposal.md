## QUASAR-002

**Proposal Title:** QUASAR multi-TI ASL model-free / model-based perfusion quantification — an execution-hard reconstruction task (recipe divergence + coupled-physics assembly + declared robustness with hidden realization)

**Scientific Domain:** Life Sciences · **Field:** Neuroscience · **Subfield:** Perfusion MRI / arterial spin labelling

**Source paper:** Petersen et al. 2006, *MRM*, "Model-free arterial spin labeling quantification approach for perfusion MRI" (QUASAR; https://doi.org/10.1002/mrm.20906). Dataset: a **paper-parameterized** multi-TI pulsed-ASL cohort with paired flow-crushed/non-crushed acquisitions (Petersen 2006 model-free ASL model), generated deterministically at `synth_build/generate_fixtures.py`; the true perfusion physiology (CBF/ATT/aBV) is held out for grading under `tests/planted_truth.npz` (built by `synth_build/build_truth.py`).

**Status: FULL runnable Harbor task.** This is a **reconstruction** task, *not* a recognition / "spot-the-confound" task — the instruction names the deliverable (estimate per-voxel CBF, ATT and, where determinable, aBV, and write them out); the difficulty is *execution*, not an un-cued judgement. It follows the shape of the maintainer's `pcasl-cbf-quantifier`: the agent implements the full QUASAR model-free and model-based quantification **from scratch** (no fitter bundled), over a **heterogeneous 8-subject cohort where subjects need fundamentally different computations**, with a **declared robustness** requirement (the corrupted-repetition realization hidden), graded voxelwise against the **planted physiology** on a **convention-invariant** physical quantity.

### Why this is hard (the four difficulty drivers)

1. **Recipe divergence** — subjects need *structurally different* analyses, discoverable only from each sidecar's acquisition field. Five subjects carry a **flow-crushed** acquisition paired with the non-crushed one: the intravascular arterial signal is directly measurable as `<noncrushed> − <crushed>`, so the **model-free** analysis applies — aBV and ATT are read off the T1-corrected arterial curve (a scaled area integral and its first moment), and CBF comes from regressing the crushed tissue curve onto the convolution of the **measured** local arterial input with the pinned single-compartment residue. The other three subjects have **only** the non-crushed acquisition: the arterial input can no longer be measured, so the analysis must **fall back** to a structurally different **model-based** kinetic fit (a transit-time search with a closed-form flow amplitude against the assumed dispersed-boxcar delivery) that yields CBF and ATT, while **aBV is not determinable and must be omitted**. A naive uniform pipeline fails a majority of the 24 panels either way.
2. **Coupled-physics assembly** — the crusher subtraction, the blood-T1 decay correction, the measured-AIF convolution, and the model-based delivery must **all** be assembled correctly, in the right units, with a per-voxel M0 calibration; an error in any one compounds.
3. **Declared robustness, hidden realization** — a **majority** of subjects carry one or two grossly motion-corrupted TI repetitions that must be rejected before the per-TI repetition average (a plain mean biases their curves), plus voxels with ~0 equilibrium magnetization that must be zeroed. `instruction.md` now **declares** (under *Robustness / data-quality contract*) that some repetitions are grossly corrupted and must be detected and rejected, and that low-M0 voxels must be zeroed; only the *realization* (which subjects/repetitions) is hidden.
4. **Convention-invariant grading** — CBF (mL/100g/min), ATT (s) and aBV (dimensionless) are uniquely-determined perfusion macro-parameters; the graded targets are the *planted* values, and the model-free crusher branch recovers them exactly on clean signal (slope·aBV = f, ATT/aBV off the arterial curve) while the model-based branch fits the same CBF/ATT — so any correct implementation of either branch recovers the planted physiology within tolerance (measured below); a from-scratch solver passes while wrong pipelines fail — no reporting-convention ambiguity.

### What changed in this revision (addressing the review of the hidden-contract design)

1. **Robustness contract made public.** `instruction.md` now states, under *Robustness / data-quality contract*, that a subset of subjects carry grossly motion-corrupted TI repetitions that must be detected and rejected before the repetition average, and that low-M0 voxels must be zeroed — only the *realization* (which subjects/repetitions) is hidden. A correct robust pipeline is no longer penalized for failing to guess an undeclared step; the model-free/model-based fork and the aBV omit rule were already public.
2. **Graded against the true physiology, not one fitter.** The verifier no longer recomputes with a private pipeline on the real data and demands agreement. It compares each submitted map, voxelwise, to the **held-out planted physiology** (`tests/planted_truth.npz`, built by `synth_build/build_truth.py`): the true CBF/ATT/aBV. Because the perfusion macro-parameters are identifiable and the pinned signal model / crusher convention / constants fix the estimand, the planted arrays ARE the convention-invariant target. The hidden reference modules (`quasar_pipeline.py`, `quasar_ref.py`) were removed from `tests/`.

### Verifier

`tests/test_outputs.py` grades **voxelwise** over the grey/white-matter mask against `tests/planted_truth.npz`, one parametrized test per (subject × map) panel — **24 panels** (8 subjects × {CBF, ATT, aBV}). A computable map passes when ≥90% of mask voxels match the planted value within a per-map tolerance (CBF rtol 10%/atol 3; ATT rtol 10%/atol 0.15; aBV rtol 12%/atol 0.002); an unsupported map (aBV for a non-crusher subject) passes only when the submission **omits** it. Reward is **binary** (test.sh: pytest rc==0 → 1 else 0), so every panel must pass.

**Validity / discrimination evidence (recomputed for this revision).** The oracle recovers the planted physiology on **all 24 panels (21 computable at 96–100 % voxel agreement, 3 aBV correctly omitted)** — the model-free branch recovers the planted CBF/ATT/aBV and the model-based branch the planted CBF/ATT, both from the real (noisy, motion-corrupted) data with the corrupted repetitions rejected. Discrimination: a **naive plain repetition mean (no gross-motion rejection) fails 9 panels**, on the corrupted subjects (CBF/aBV collapse), while the clean subjects still pass. So a from-scratch correct solver passes and the non-robust-average shortcut fails, on an axis the instruction now *declares*.

### Difficulty — frontier gate

Oracle **reward 1.0** verified in-container on this revised verifier (all 24 panels pass against the planted truth). On the *previous* (hidden-contract) version, **gpt-5.6-sol (codex, xhigh) scored 0.0**, solving 15/24 panels — failing the model-free/model-based fork, the measured-AIF convolution and T1-decay-corrected aBV, the corrupted-repetition rejection, and/or the aBV omit rule.

**Frontier re-gate on this revised (public-contract) version: PENDING.** Because the revision *discloses* the corrupted-repetition requirement, the old gate number does not transfer and must be re-measured — not overclaimed here. The remaining difficulty is the multi-axis assembly (the structural model-free/model-based fork with the aBV omit, the crusher subtraction and measured-AIF convolution, the T1-corrected integrals, the per-voxel M0 calibration, and discover-and-reject the hidden corrupted repetitions); the local discrimination above shows the non-robust-average shortcut still fails 9/24. A 2nd frontier family (Claude/Gemini) gate is likewise pending at maintainer calibration.

### Notes / honesty

- **Reconstruction paradigm.** This task's difficulty is executional (get the coupled physics, units, per-voxel M0 calibration, and per-subject adaptation right with no bundled quantifier), unlike the recognition-tier "un-cued judgement" tasks in this repo. `instruction.md` names the deliverable, the QUASAR conventions and (now) the corrupted-repetition robustness contract, but never enumerates *where* the pitfalls fall (which repetitions are corrupted) — the agent must discover them from the data.
- **Data.** Paper-parameterized, small, deterministic, and **leakage-clean** (the planted truth lives only in `tests/planted_truth.npz`, never under `/app/data`). Regenerable via `synth_build/generate_fixtures.py` + `synth_build/build_truth.py`.
- **allow_internet=false.** The task is self-contained (dependencies baked in the image; no network at run or verify time).
