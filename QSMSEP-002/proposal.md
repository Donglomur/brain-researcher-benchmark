## QSMSEP-002

**Proposal Title:** Magnetic susceptibility source separation (chi-separation) of a heterogeneous cohort — paramagnetic (iron) and diamagnetic (myelin/Ca) maps from GRE+SE relaxometry — an execution-hard reconstruction task (recipe divergence + coupled-physics assembly + declared robustness with hidden realization)

**Scientific Domain:** Life Sciences · **Field:** Neuroscience · **Subfield:** Quantitative MRI / susceptibility source separation

**Source paper:** Shin et al. 2021, *NeuroImage* (χ-separation: magnetic susceptibility source separation toward iron and myelin mapping in the brain, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2021.118371). Dataset: a **paper-parameterized** chi-separation (GRE+SE) susceptibility cohort (Shin 2021 signal model), generated deterministically at `synth_build/generate_fixtures.py`; the true physiology (R2*/R2/chi_para/chi_dia) is held out for grading under `tests/planted_truth.npz` (built by `synth_build/build_truth.py`).

**Status: FULL runnable Harbor task.** This is a **reconstruction** task, *not* a recognition / "spot-the-confound" task — the instruction names the deliverable (separate the given total susceptibility into paramagnetic and diamagnetic sources and write the per-voxel maps, plus R2*/R2 where determinable); the difficulty is *execution*, not an un-cued judgement. The agent implements the chi-separation relaxometry **from scratch** (no fitter bundled), over a **heterogeneous 8-subject cohort where subjects need fundamentally different computations**, with a **declared robustness** requirement (the corrupted-echo realization hidden), graded voxelwise against the **planted physiology** on **convention-invariant** quantities. The dipole inversion (frequency → total susceptibility) is **excised** — the total susceptibility is given — so the graded quantity carries no QSM regularization/reference convention.

### Why this is hard (the four difficulty drivers)

1. **Recipe divergence** — discoverable only from each sidecar: most subjects carry a multi-echo spin-echo (SE) series so R2 — and hence `R2' = R2* − R2` and the full separation into paramagnetic (iron) and diamagnetic (myelin/Ca) sources — is determinable, but a minority have **no** SE series, where R2' cannot be formed and chi_para / |chi_dia| must be **omitted** (only R2* reported); one subject is **single-echo GRE**, where R2* itself is not determinable and is omitted (yet its SE series still yields R2). A single fixed recipe cannot fit the cohort.
2. **Coupled-physics assembly** — R2* (GRE) and R2 (SE) combine into R2', which the given total susceptibility splits via the closed form `chi_para = 0.5·(R2'/D_r + chi_tot)`, `|chi_dia| = 0.5·(R2'/D_r − chi_tot)`; an error in the R2* fit shifts **both** sources together, a swapped sign shifts them oppositely, a missed R2 subtraction or a wrong D_r rescales both. The relaxometric constant is **field-dependent** (`D_r = 90·field_T/3`), so a fixed-D_r pipeline biases the separation on the 7T subjects.
3. **Declared robustness, hidden realization** — a **majority** of subjects carry one grossly motion-corrupted echo volume (in the GRE or the SE train) that must be **rejected** before the mono-exponential fit, or R2*/R2 (and every quantity downstream) is biased on those subjects. `instruction.md` now **declares** (under *Robustness / data-quality contract*) that a corrupted echo exists and must be detected and rejected; only the *realization* (which subjects/echoes) is hidden.
4. **Convention-invariant grading** — because the total susceptibility and D_r are pinned *inputs* and only physical relaxation rates are estimated, the graded quantity is the *planted* physiology, and the closed form with the true R2′ returns the planted sources exactly: a from-scratch implementation (leave-one-out echo rejection, scipy normal-equation backend, explicit separation solve) recovers every computable panel at 100% voxel agreement (measured below), so a correct solver of any estimator family passes while wrong pipelines fail — no reporting-convention ambiguity.

### What changed in this revision (addressing the review of the hidden-contract design)

1. **Robustness contract made public.** `instruction.md` now states, under *Robustness / data-quality contract*, that a subset of subjects carry one grossly corrupted echo volume (GRE or SE) that must be detected and rejected before the mono-exponential fit — only the *realization* (which subjects/echoes) is hidden. A correct robust fit is no longer penalized for failing to guess an undeclared step; the per-subject train forks and the field-scaled D_r were already public in the protocol.
2. **Graded against the true physiology, not one fitter.** The verifier no longer recomputes with a private pipeline on the real data and demands agreement. It compares each submitted map, voxelwise, to the **held-out planted physiology** (`tests/planted_truth.npz`, built by `synth_build/build_truth.py`): the true R2*/R2 rates and the true chi_para/|chi_dia| sources. Because the ill-posed dipole step is excised (chi_tot given, noise-free), the closed form with the true R2′ returns the planted sources exactly, so the planted arrays ARE the convention-invariant target. The hidden reference modules (`chisep_pipeline.py`, `chisep_ref.py`) were removed from `tests/`.

### Verifier

`tests/test_outputs.py` grades **voxelwise** over the brain mask against `tests/planted_truth.npz`, one parametrized test per (subject × map) panel — **32 panels** (8 subjects × {R2star, R2, chi_para, chi_dia_abs}). A computable map passes when ≥90% of brain voxels match the planted value within a per-map tolerance (R2star/R2 rtol 6%/atol 0.8; chi_para/chi_dia_abs rtol 10%/atol 0.006 ppm); an unsupported map (R2* for a single-echo GRE, R2 with no SE, the separation without R2') passes only when the submission **omits** it. Reward is **binary** (test.sh: pytest rc==0 → 1 else 0), so every panel must pass.

**Validity / discrimination evidence (recomputed for this revision).** The oracle recovers the planted physiology on **all 32 panels (23 computable at 100 % voxel agreement, 9 correctly omitted)** — the log-linear fit on the real (noisy, one-corrupted-echo) data recovers the planted rates within tolerance and the closed form with the given chi_tot returns the planted sources. Discrimination: a **naive fit that keeps the corrupted echo (no rejection) fails 14 panels**, exactly on the five corrupted subjects (sub-02..06), while the three clean subjects (sub-01, sub-07, sub-08) still pass. So a from-scratch correct solver passes and the no-rejection shortcut fails, on an axis the instruction now *declares*.

### Difficulty — frontier gate

Oracle **reward 1.0** verified in-container on this revised verifier (all 32 panels pass against the planted truth). On the *previous* (hidden-contract) version, **gpt-5.6-sol (codex, xhigh) scored 0.0**, solving 18/32 panels — failing the corrupted-echo rejection, the field-dependent `D_r = 90·field/3` on the 7T subjects, and the SE-present / single-echo omit forks.

**Frontier re-gate on this revised (public-contract) version: PENDING.** Because the revision *discloses* the corrupted-echo requirement, the old gate number does not transfer and must be re-measured — not overclaimed here. The remaining difficulty is the multi-axis assembly (per-subject train forks with the omit rule, field-scaled D_r, discover-and-reject the hidden corrupted echo without dropping clean ones, and the coupled R2*/R2 → R2′ → closed-form separation); the local discrimination above shows the no-rejection shortcut still fails 14/32. A 2nd frontier family (Claude/Gemini) gate is likewise pending at maintainer calibration.

### Notes / honesty

- **Reconstruction paradigm.** This task's difficulty is executional (get the per-subject train forks, the field-scaled constant, and the echo robustness right with no recipe), unlike the recognition-tier "un-cued judgement" tasks in this repo. `instruction.md` names the deliverable, the physics conventions and (now) the corrupted-echo robustness contract, but never enumerates *where* the pitfalls fall (which echoes are corrupted, the per-subject omit forks) — the agent must discover them from the data.
- **Data.** Paper-parameterized, small, deterministic, and **leakage-clean** (the planted truth lives only in `tests/planted_truth.npz`, never under `/app/data`). Regenerable via `synth_build/generate_fixtures.py` + `synth_build/build_truth.py`.
- **allow_internet=false.** The task is self-contained (dependencies baked in the image; no network at run or verify time).
