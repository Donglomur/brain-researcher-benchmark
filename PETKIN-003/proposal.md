## PETKIN-003

**Proposal Title:** Per-subject / per-region kinetic quantification of a heterogeneous dynamic brain-PET cohort — an execution-hard reconstruction task (per-region model selection + coupled-physics assembly + hidden noise-robustness)

**Scientific Domain:** Life Sciences · **Field:** Neuroscience · **Subfield:** Molecular imaging / PET tracer kinetics

**Source paper:** Lammertsma & Hume 1996, *NeuroImage* (simplified reference tissue model / SRTM, https://doi.org/10.1006/nimg.1996.0066); Wu & Carson 2002, *J. Cereb. Blood Flow Metab.* (SRTM2 / MRTM2, noise reduction with fixed k2′); Patlak et al. 1983, *J. Cereb. Blood Flow Metab.* (Patlak graphical analysis, https://doi.org/10.1038/jcbfm.1983.1); Logan et al. 1990, *J. Cereb. Blood Flow Metab.* (Logan plot); Ichise et al. 2002, *J. Cereb. Blood Flow Metab.* (multilinear MA1). Dataset: a **synthetic** dynamic brain-PET cohort, generated deterministically at `synth_build/generate_fixtures.py`; planted ground-truth kinetic parameters held out under `synth_build/fixture_spec.json`.

**Status: FULL runnable Harbor task.** This is a **reconstruction** task, *not* a recognition / "spot-the-confound" task — the instruction names the deliverable (report each region's binding/kinetic parameter under the single quantity its data support); the difficulty is *execution*, not an un-cued judgement. It follows the shape of the maintainer's `pcasl-cbf-quantifier`: the agent implements the tracer-kinetic estimators **from scratch** (no fitter bundled, the method not spelled out), over a **heterogeneous cohort where subjects and even regions need structurally different models**, with a **hidden noise-robustness** requirement, graded per (subject × region) against a **held-out reference** on convention-invariant kinetic quantities.

### Why this is hard (the four difficulty drivers)

1. **Recipe divergence (per subject and per region)** — six exams provide only a reference region and no arterial input, so a plasma solution is uncomputable and a **reference-tissue model** (SRTM2/MRTM2 with the given fixed k2′) is the only route to BPND; the other two provide a metabolite-corrected arterial input and no reference region, and each region is either a **reversible** tracer (total distribution volume VT) or an **irreversibly trapping** one where BPND/VT are undefined and the net influx **Ki** (Patlak) is required — both plasma subjects mix trapping and reversible regions, so the model family must be chosen **per region** from the TAC shape. A single fixed recipe cannot fit the cohort.
2. **Coupled-physics assembly** — isotope-specific decay-correction of every frame to injection time (¹¹C t½=20.4 min vs ¹⁸F t½=109.8 min), frame-duration weighting, the metabolite-corrected parent plasma assembled as whole-blood × plasma/blood ratio × parent fraction (decay-corrected like the tissue), trapezoidal integration for the graphical linearisations, and the t* linear window must **all** be right or the estimate compounds error.
3. **Hidden robustness (the dominant, unannounced trap)** — five of the six reference-tissue subjects carry heavy counting noise at high/mid binding potential (mostly short-half-life ¹¹C, whose late frames blow up under decay-correction), where an ordinary reference-Logan slope is strongly downward-biased and a free per-region SRTM k2′ is unstable, so only the **noise-robust SRTM2/MRTM2 with the fixed k2′** recovers BPND. A single low-noise reference subject anchors the axis, so it must be discovered from the data, not announced. A naive non-robust pipeline that gets decay, metabolite correction, and model family all right but reaches for an OLS graphical slope still fails a **majority** of panels.
4. **Convention-invariant grading** — BPND (`DVR−1`), VT, and Ki are uniquely-defined kinetic quantities; the graded BPND target is estimator-invariant (reproduced within tolerance by two further robust methods, proven below), so a correct robust estimator passes while an OLS reference-Logan does not.

### Verifier

`tests/test_outputs.py` recomputes every quantity from the bundled TACs with a **held-out reference** pipeline (`pet_pipeline` + `pet_ref`, shipped only under `tests/`+`solution/`, never under `/app/data`) and grades per (subject, region). The design carries **32 (subject × region) panels**, each its own test (the gate enumerated 33 tests). Reward is **fractional** — the score is the fraction of panels correct. A panel passes when the submission reports that region under the **correct** quantity (`bpnd`/`vt`/`ki.json`) within a per-quantity tolerance (BPND rtol 7%/atol 0.10; VT rtol 12%/atol 0.30; Ki rtol 10%/atol 0.003) **and** does not report it under either other quantity.

**Grading-invariance proof (the key check).** The graded BPND target is reproduced within tolerance by two further robust methods — a genuinely-independent nonlinear-least-squares SRTM2 (max BPND diff 0.06) and the multilinear MRTM2 (worst-case tolerance margin +0.07) — and stays within ~12% of planted truth on every noisy panel. The full independent implementation (SRTM2 by NLS, full 2-tissue-compartment VT, irreversible-2TCM Ki, reversibility from the fitted k4) reproduces every panel to well within tolerance (max BPND diff 0.06, max VT diff ~4%, max Ki diff 2%, zero model-family mismatches). The plausible-but-wrong pipelines each fail only their axis (panels failed of 32: ignore decay 30, skip metabolite correction 8, OLS reference-Logan 21, free-k2′ SRTM 17, treat every region reversible 3, force a reference model on the whole cohort 8, force a plasma model on the whole cohort 24), and the combined naive non-robust pipeline (correct decay + metabolite + model family but an OLS graphical slope throughout) fails 21/32 — so partial credit is monotone in correctness and the hidden noise-robustness axis bites a majority of the cohort.

### Notes / honesty

- **Reconstruction paradigm.** This task's difficulty is executional (assemble a coupled kinetic-modelling pipeline and choose the model family with no recipe), unlike the recognition-tier "un-cued judgement" tasks in this repo. `instruction.md` names the deliverable and the decay/metabolite/window conventions but never enumerates the pitfalls (the noise-robust estimator, the per-region model fork) — the agent must discover them from the data.
- **Data.** Synthetic, small, deterministic, and **leakage-clean** (the held-out reference and planted parameters are never under `/app/data`; `fixture_spec.json` is build-provenance only). Regenerable via `synth_build/generate_fixtures.py`.
- **allow_internet=false.** The task is self-contained (dependencies baked in the image; no network at run or verify time).
