## PETKIN-003

**Proposal Title:** Per-subject / per-region kinetic quantification of a heterogeneous dynamic brain-PET cohort — an execution-hard reconstruction task (per-region model selection + coupled-physics assembly + declared noise-robustness with hidden realization)

**Scientific Domain:** Life Sciences · **Field:** Neuroscience · **Subfield:** Molecular imaging / PET tracer kinetics

**Source paper:** Lammertsma & Hume 1996, *NeuroImage* (simplified reference tissue model / SRTM, https://doi.org/10.1006/nimg.1996.0066); Wu & Carson 2002, *J. Cereb. Blood Flow Metab.* (SRTM2 / MRTM2, noise reduction with fixed k2′); Patlak et al. 1983, *J. Cereb. Blood Flow Metab.* (Patlak graphical analysis, https://doi.org/10.1038/jcbfm.1983.1); Logan et al. 1990, *J. Cereb. Blood Flow Metab.* (Logan plot); Ichise et al. 2002, *J. Cereb. Blood Flow Metab.* (multilinear MA1). Dataset: a **paper-parameterized** dynamic brain-PET cohort, generated deterministically at `synth_build/generate_fixtures.py`; the planted kinetic macro-parameters are held out for grading under `tests/planted_truth.json` (built by `synth_build/build_truth.py`).

**Status: FULL runnable Harbor task.** This is a **reconstruction** task, *not* a recognition / "spot-the-confound" task — the instruction names the deliverable (report each region's binding/kinetic parameter under the single quantity its data support); the difficulty is *execution*, not an un-cued judgement. It follows the shape of the maintainer's `pcasl-cbf-quantifier`: the agent implements the tracer-kinetic estimators **from scratch** (no fitter bundled, the method not spelled out), over a **heterogeneous cohort where subjects and even regions need structurally different models**, with a **declared noise-robustness** requirement (the realization hidden), graded per (subject × region) against the **planted kinetic macro-parameters** — convention-invariant quantities every valid robust estimator recovers.

### Why this is hard (the four difficulty drivers)

1. **Recipe divergence (per subject and per region)** — six exams provide only a reference region and no arterial input, so a plasma solution is uncomputable and a **reference-tissue model** (SRTM2/MRTM2 with the given fixed k2′) is the only route to BPND; the other two provide a metabolite-corrected arterial input and no reference region, and each region is either a **reversible** tracer (total distribution volume VT) or an **irreversibly trapping** one where BPND/VT are undefined and the net influx **Ki** (Patlak) is required — both plasma subjects mix trapping and reversible regions, so the model family must be chosen **per region** from the TAC shape. A single fixed recipe cannot fit the cohort.
2. **Coupled-physics assembly** — isotope-specific decay-correction of every frame to injection time (¹¹C t½=20.4 min vs ¹⁸F t½=109.8 min), frame-duration weighting, the metabolite-corrected parent plasma assembled as whole-blood × plasma/blood ratio × parent fraction (decay-corrected like the tissue), trapezoidal integration for the graphical linearisations, and the t* linear window must **all** be right or the estimate compounds error.
3. **Declared robustness, hidden realization (the dominant trap)** — five of the six reference-tissue subjects carry heavy counting noise at high/mid binding potential (mostly short-half-life ¹¹C, whose late frames blow up under decay-correction), where an ordinary reference-Logan slope is strongly downward-biased and a free per-region SRTM k2′ is unstable, so only the **noise-robust SRTM2/MRTM2 with the fixed k2′** recovers BPND. A single low-noise reference subject anchors the axis. `instruction.md` now **declares** (under *Robustness / data-quality contract*) that heavy noise is present and that a naive graphical slope is biased and a noise-robust estimator must be used; only the *realization* (which subjects) is hidden. A naive non-robust pipeline that gets decay, metabolite correction, and model family all right but reaches for an OLS graphical slope still fails a **majority** of panels.
4. **Convention-invariant grading** — BPND (`DVR−1`), VT, and Ki are uniquely-defined kinetic macro-parameters; the graded targets are the *planted* values, and the tolerances are set to the measured spread among valid robust estimators (see below), so a correct robust estimator of any family passes while an OLS reference-Logan does not.

### What changed in this revision (addressing the review of the hidden-contract design)

1. **Robustness contract made public.** `instruction.md` now states, under *Robustness / data-quality contract*, that a subset of subjects carry heavy counting noise, that a naive graphical slope (e.g. an OLS reference-Logan) is biased there, and that a noise-robust estimator (fixed-k2′ SRTM2/MRTM2 for the reference-tissue subjects; MA1/Patlak over ratio-space OLS for the plasma maps) is required — only the *realization* (which subjects are noisy) is hidden. A correct robust fit is no longer penalized for failing to guess an undeclared step.
2. **Graded against the true physiology, not one fitter.** The verifier no longer recomputes with a private pipeline on the real data and demands agreement. It compares each reported quantity to the **held-out planted macro-parameter** (`tests/planted_truth.json`, built by `synth_build/build_truth.py`): BPND = planted binding potential, VT = (K1/k2)(1+k3/k4), Ki = K1·k3/(k2+k3), with the region's kinetic type a structural property of the planted model. The hidden reference modules (`pet_pipeline.py`, `pet_ref.py`) were removed from `tests/`.
3. **BPND tolerance widened to the measured estimator spread.** At the old BPND band (rtol 7%/atol 0.10) the target was *not* estimator-invariant: on the heavy-noise reference subjects the noise-robust estimators genuinely spread by ~10–14% of BPND, so MRTM2 (a protocol-sanctioned estimator) failed 6 panels and a finer-grid SRTM2 one — only the pinned-grid SRTM2 (which the old verifier both produced *and* graded against) passed. The BPND band is now **rtol 10%/atol 0.10**, calibrated so that all three valid robust estimators pass (measured worst 96% of tol) while the biased naive OLS reference-Logan still fails (closest non-anchor at 129% of tol). VT/Ki bands are unchanged (already convention-invariant).

### Verifier

`tests/test_outputs.py` grades each reported quantity against `tests/planted_truth.json`, one parametrized test per (subject × region) panel — **32 panels** (8 subjects × 4 regions). A panel passes when the submission reports that region under the **correct** quantity (`bpnd`/`vt`/`ki.json`) within its per-quantity tolerance (BPND rtol 10%/atol 0.10; VT rtol 12%/atol 0.30; Ki rtol 10%/atol 0.003) **and** does not report it under either other quantity. Reward is **binary** (test.sh: pytest rc==0 → 1 else 0), so every panel must pass.

**Validity / discrimination evidence (recomputed for this revision).** The oracle recovers the planted macro-parameters on **all 32 panels** — comfortably (worst BPND margin ~21% of tol; VT/Ki margins 70–95%). Convention-invariance is measured directly: the pinned-grid SRTM2, the multilinear MRTM2, and a 10×-finer-grid SRTM2 all pass every BPND panel (worst 96% of tol), and MA1/Logan agree on VT — so any valid robust estimator passes. Discrimination: a **naive OLS reference-Logan (skipping the noise-robust estimator) fails 23 of 32 panels**, on the noisy reference subjects, while the low-noise anchor (sub-06, low-BPND region) and the plasma panels still pass. So a from-scratch correct solver passes and the non-robust-estimator shortcut fails, on an axis the instruction now *declares*.

### Difficulty — frontier gate

Oracle **reward 1.0** verified in-container on this revised verifier (all 32 panels pass against the planted truth). On the *previous* (hidden-contract) version, **gpt-5.6-sol (codex, xhigh) scored 0.0 across k=3**, solving 13/33 gate items — failing the noise-robustness axis (OLS reference-Logan / free-k2′ SRTM collapse on the noisy ¹¹C subjects), the per-region reversible-vs-trapping model selection, and the decay / metabolite-correction assembly.

**Frontier re-gate on this revised (public-contract) version: PENDING.** Because the revision *discloses* the noise-robustness requirement, the old gate number does not transfer and must be re-measured — not overclaimed here. The remaining difficulty is the multi-axis assembly (isotope-specific decay, metabolite-corrected parent plasma, per-region reversible-vs-trapping model selection, and choosing/implementing a noise-robust reference-tissue estimator with the fixed k2′); the local discrimination above shows the non-robust-estimator shortcut still fails 23/32. A 2nd frontier family (Claude/Gemini) gate is likewise pending at maintainer calibration.

### Notes / honesty

- **Reconstruction paradigm.** This task's difficulty is executional (assemble a coupled kinetic-modelling pipeline and choose the model family with no recipe), unlike the recognition-tier "un-cued judgement" tasks in this repo. `instruction.md` names the deliverable, the decay/metabolite/window conventions and (now) the noise-robustness contract, but never enumerates *where* the pitfalls fall (which subjects are noisy, the per-region reversible/trapping fork) — the agent must discover them from the data.
- **Data.** Paper-parameterized, small, deterministic, and **leakage-clean** (the planted truth lives only in `tests/planted_truth.json`, never under `/app/data`; `fixture_spec.json` is build-provenance only). Regenerable via `synth_build/generate_fixtures.py` + `synth_build/build_truth.py`.
- **allow_internet=false.** The task is self-contained (dependencies baked in the image; no network at run or verify time).
