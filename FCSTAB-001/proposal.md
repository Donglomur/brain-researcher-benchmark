# FCSTAB-001 — within-run connectivity selection sensitivity

## Scientific scope

A fixed 40-person, single-site ABIDE PCP CC200 cohort supports a transparent
methods/control comparison: how signed later-minus-earlier selected-edge mean
Fisher-z depends on choosing edges from the first half, second half, all 39 other
participants' full runs, or a fixed size-matched random set. This retains the
original task's selection-sensitivity question without treating any numerical
pattern as a required scientific conclusion.

The common support mask uses all participants and all three segments. Thus the
LOSO-ranked scheme is not a fully independent preprocessing pipeline. Shared
training participants and temporal dependence are not accounted for by ordinary
participant t/TOST summaries; these are nominal/model-based diagnostics. The
±.05 margin is operational, not clinically validated. No cancellation of bias,
selection-free estimate, population-valid coverage or ICC claim is made.
